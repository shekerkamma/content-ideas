# Content Routing

## Destination

- Raw source stays in `Clippings/`.
- Enriched analysis goes to `content-research/<type>/`.
- Idea backlog goes to `Ideas/`.
- LinkedIn drafts go to `Content/LinkedIn/`.
- X drafts go to `Content/X/`.
- POC ideas go to `Projects/` through `vault-poc-factory-pipeline`.

## Promotion Criteria

Promote to `Ideas/` when:
- the note has a clear strategic thesis,
- it can become a post, framework, or demo,
- or it links to a priority theme.

Promote to `Content/LinkedIn/` when:
- it has a strong point of view,
- a credible source note,
- and a practical audience: enterprise AI leaders, architects, pre-sales, or operators.
