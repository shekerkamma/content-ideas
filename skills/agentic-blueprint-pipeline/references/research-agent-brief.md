# Deep Research Agent Brief

Use this brief when spawning a research agent for one use case.

```text
Research <USE CASE> for the Agentic Master Blueprint portfolio.

Return an evidence-rich memo with:
1. Incumbent competitors and product categories.
2. Public pricing or pricing-friction evidence.
3. Buyer pain and workflow friction from credible sources.
4. Disruptive agentic wedge.
5. Implementation implications for a 30-day MVP.
6. Risks: regulatory, security, privacy, integration, adoption.
7. Source list with URLs, publication/access dates, and the claim each source
   supports.

Prefer official vendor pages, docs, pricing pages, public filings, regulatory
sources, credible analyst/customer review sources, and high-signal community
threads. Do not fabricate exact pricing where vendors hide it.
Do not edit files.
```
