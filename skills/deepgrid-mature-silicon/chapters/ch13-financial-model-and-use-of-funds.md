# Chapter 13: What ₹10 Cr Buys, and What It Proves (Financial Engine)

## Core Idea
The ₹10 Cr ($1.2M) seed round funds 24 months of runway, six parallel MPW factory runs, four full product qualifications, and production mask sets for Chips 1 and 2—scaling the company from a design thesis into ₹28 Cr of FY28 commercial revenue.

## Production Wafer Economics (Chips 1 & 2)
```
- Standard Wafer Diameter: 200 mm
- Usable Silicon Area (excluding 3mm edge exclusion & scribe lanes): 90%
- Expected Mature Node Die Yield: 85%
- Standard Batch Size: 25 Wafers per lot
- Chip 1 (Motor Controller, ~12 mm²): Yields ~1,800 good dies/wafer (~45,000 dies/batch)
- Chip 2 (Smart Meter SoC, ~8 mm²): Yields ~2,800 good dies/wafer (~70,000 dies/batch)
- First Wafer Batches funded in seed round produce 94,000–165,000 production units (worth ₹2–9 Cr in sales).
```

## Key Takeaways
1. The seed round funds prototypes for 7 chips and full production masks for the top 2 commercial revenue drivers.
2. FY31 ₹1,000 Cr projection represents market category coverage rather than aggressive single-product market share.
3. Live pre-ASIC contracted revenue (₹2.88 Cr) covers 72% of the FY27 revenue target before mass silicon arrives.
