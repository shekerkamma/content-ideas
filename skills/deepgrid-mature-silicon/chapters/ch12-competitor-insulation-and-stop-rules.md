## The Four Ironclad Stop Rules (Written in Advance)

> [!CAUTION]
> These stop rules are documented with hard trigger dates to prevent emotional bias or sunk-cost fallacy during operational pivots.

1. **Rule S1 (The Anchor Signature Gate)**:  
   *If an anchor customer (e.g., Ripple Metering for Chip 2) has not signed a formal Letter of Intent (LOI) by the factory shuttle order deadline, that chip is paused for a full 198-day cycle and its capital is reallocated to Chips 1 and 3.*
2. **Rule S2 (The Military Screening Fail-Safe)**:  
   *If Chip 6 (Voltage Supervisor) fails military MIL-STD-883 screening twice, all forward defence revenue projections across the entire catalog are delayed by 12 months within 30 days.*
3. **Rule S3 (The Commodity Exit Trigger)**:  
   *In FY29, verify landed Chinese chip prices (including import duties). If commodity prices fall below our unit manufacturing cost, immediately exit the commercial ceiling-fan driver market and focus exclusively on electric vehicles and defence sockets.*
4. **Rule S4 (The Sovereign Fab Escalation)**:  
   *If SCL Mohali’s production schedule slips by more than two consecutive factory cycles, publicly disclose the delay and pivot volume production to SkyWater and IHP.*
