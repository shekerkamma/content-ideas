# Sample prompts

## Landscape deck

Create a 32-slide competitor strategy deck for **[company]** in **[market/geography]**. The decision is **[decision]** for **[audience]**. Recall durable prior research first, then refresh evidence through **[cutoff date]** using primary sources. Build an evidence ledger before synthesis. Apply Story Architect, then Grill-Me; convert every accepted finding into atomic controls and integrate them through Meta LOOP. Author slide content/design contracts in sequential batches of at most four. Use **[template path]** and the compound competitor archetype catalog. Deliver a native editable draft plus QA evidence; promote only after OfficeCLI and native PowerPoint inspection pass.

## Single-competitor threat assessment

Assess **[competitor]** against **[company]** across **[segments]**. Separate verified facts, attributed claims, interpretations, and unknowns. Cover buyer control points, segment threat, scorecard sensitivity, messaging collision, channel/partner option, white space, counter-moves, owners, triggers, and falsifiers. Produce 8–12 slides with parallel analytical grammar and one dominant exhibit per page. Do not infer certification, deployment, commercial terms, or customer status without evidence.

## Existing-deck rewrite

Rebuild **[source deck]** using **[reference template]** without losing evidence depth or slide count. Normalize both sources first. Preserve valid content; diagnose each page through Grill-Me; use Meta LOOP to classify keep/rewrite/split/merge/drop decisions. Write a complete content and design envelope for every final slide before editing. Prove material change through claim-slide traceability and before/after slide fingerprints. Never overwrite the source deck.

## Long-deck batch instruction

Author only slides **[n–m]** from the locked slide plan. This batch contains no more than four slides. For each slide return the full content contract, full design contract, evidence IDs, counterargument, decision/owner/trigger, speaker notes, and review-control IDs. Do not reference unstated evidence. Stop if a required claim lacks an allowed evidence ID. Do not draft later slides.
