# Content Strategy: X / LinkedIn / YouTube

Social media content plan for the GTM launch phase. Part of Stage 7 (GTM + Launch Plan).
Generate 10 niche-specific post openings from the Stage 2 use cases.

---

## Content Angle

"What my AI engineering team built this week for [niche]"

---

## 10 Opening Post Ideas for [Niche]

Generate 10 specific, concrete post openings using the use cases from Stage 2.

Each post must:
- Start with a buyer pain statement (not a tech claim)
- Use buyer language (no "agents", "MCP", "OpenHands", "LLM")
- Include ROI math (time saved, cost replaced, revenue generated)
- Be written for a [niche] owner scrolling their feed — not a tech audience

---

## Post Format Template

```
Here's what [specific niche pain] is costing [niche] businesses every month:

[Break it down with numbers]

I built a [plain-English description of the tool] that:
- [Specific result 1]
- [Specific result 2]
- [Specific result 3]

It costs [price]. It replaces [what it replaces].

If you're a [niche] and this is your life, DM me.
```

## Cadence

- **Minimum:** 1 piece of content per week during the first 90 days
- **Milestone trigger:** 1 case study per month once the first client is live
- **Format progression:**
  - Weeks 1-4: problem + ROI math posts (text-only or single image)
  - Week 5+: add short demo clips (3 min max, screen record of the tool running)
  - Month 2+: case study posts (named client optional — "a [niche] client in [city]")

---

## Week-by-Week Content Calendar (First 4 Weeks)

### Week 1 — Establish the Pain
- Post 1 (X/LinkedIn): Pain statement for Use Case #1 + ROI math
- Goal: 3 DMs from niche owners who recognize the pain

### Week 2 — Show the Build
- Post 2 (X/LinkedIn): "I built a [plain English tool] for [niche]. Here's what it does."
- Format: numbered list of what it handles, one concrete result per bullet
- Optional: 60-second screen record clip (no narration needed)

### Week 3 — Proof of Concept
- Post 3 (LinkedIn): "Discovery call findings: here's what [niche] owners keep saying"
- Format: 3-5 direct quotes from discovery calls (paraphrased if no permission to quote)
- Goal: credibility signal that you've talked to real buyers

### Week 4 — Pricing + Offer
- Post 4 (X/LinkedIn): "Here's what I charge and why"
- Format: Starter / Growth / Scale tiers, 1 line each, ROI math for Growth tier
- CTA: "Book a call if you want to see if this fits"

---

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Tech-first posts:** "I built an AI agent using OpenHands and 14 MCP servers" → nobody in the niche cares
- **Vague outcome claims:** "saves time" or "increases efficiency" — use numbers
- **Posting without engaging:** reply to every comment on Day 1-7, especially from niche owners
- **Posting on the wrong platform:** verify before launching (Facebook for dentists, LinkedIn for accountants)
- **Case studies before you have a client:** wait until Week 5+ — fake social proof is worse than none
