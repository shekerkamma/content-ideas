# Sales & Marketing Alignment

ABM only works when sales and marketing run the same play on the same accounts. These workflows keep both teams coordinated.

Read [references/data-source-protocol.md](references/data-source-protocol.md) first. Deliver one recommended play per account with proposed owners and dates when evidence supports it. Signal claims must trace to retrieved records; publishing, assigning, or updating systems requires approval.

## When to use

- Handing an account from ABM/marketing to the sales team.
- Building a shared plan both teams work from.
- Running the weekly account review for the GTM team.

## Inputs

- Active account list and available intent, CRM, campaign, or outreach data. Clearcue is optional.
- Team roles, ownership rules, planning horizon, and success definition.

## Workflows

### Account handoff brief (ABM → AE)

```text
I'm handing off [Company Name] from ABM to sales. From the available sources, pull the accessible signal and campaign history and generate:
ACCOUNT SNAPSHOT (company, buying stage, time in campaign, touches + what they responded to)
STAKEHOLDER MAP (primary contact + last touch; others with signals; decision-maker status; champions vs blockers)
WHAT WE KNOW (pain points from behavior; competitor exposure; what resonated; what didn't)
WHY NOW (the trigger that prompted handoff)
RECOMMENDED FIRST MOVE (who to contact first; exact pre-written message; what to reference; what NOT to bring up)
OPEN THREADS (outstanding touches; unreached stakeholders; promises to fulfill). One-page brief.
```

### Joint account planning doc

```text
A joint account plan for [Company Name]. Combine verified signal intelligence with available CRM data. Generate an account objective, intelligence summary with confidence, buying-committee status, proposed ownership and timeline, messaging alignment, and next review date. Label unconfirmed roles and pains as inferred.
```

### Weekly ABM account review

```text
Generate our Weekly ABM Signal Review for all active accounts using the available sources for the last 7 days.
ACCOUNT HEALTH DASHBOARD: score each 🟢 Heating Up / 🟡 Steady / 🔴 Cooling Down / ⚫ Dark.
TOP 5 TO PRIORITIZE (what, who, why, action + owner)
NEW SIGNALS THIS WEEK (first-time accounts; new stakeholders; trigger events)
COMPETITIVE ALERTS (accounts engaging competitors; response plays)
CAMPAIGN PERFORMANCE (outreach sent; responses + rate; meetings; escalations)
ACTION ITEMS (3 for ABM, 2 to escalate to AE, 1 to remove). Under 2 pages, every line actionable.
```

## Output

A one-page handoff brief, joint account plan, or weekly review draft. Post or update team systems only after explicit approval.

## Tips

- The "what NOT to bring up" section prevents the most common handoff failure — if a topic got no engagement, the AE shouldn't lead with it.
- The "who does what" section is what stops AE and ABM from messaging the same person on the same day.
