# Research To Wiki Handoff

## Classify Each Research Note

- Reference only: keep in `content-research/`, update MOC.
- Reusable concept: create or update `Knowledge/<Concept>.md`.
- Tool/platform: create or update `technologies/<Tool>.md`.
- Creator/person: create or update `People/<Name>.md`.
- Content angle: create `Ideas/<slug>.md` and/or `Content/LinkedIn/<slug>.md`.
- Demo/POC: create `Projects/<POC Name>.md` and task notes.

## Signals For Graduation

Graduate a research note when it contains:
- a repeatable framework,
- a client-relevant pattern,
- a strong LinkedIn angle,
- a POC architecture,
- a workflow that can become a skill,
- or a clear relation to [[TMNA]], [[SAP]], [[The Multi-Cloud POC Factory]], [[Claude Code]], or [[MCP]].
