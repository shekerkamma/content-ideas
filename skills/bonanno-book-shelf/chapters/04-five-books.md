# 04 — The five books to convert first

Each picked for a specific problem the author kept hitting using Claude as a
founder. Convert in this order, if you own them.

| # | Book | The problem it fixes | Say this to Claude |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Breakthrough Advertising**, Eugene Schwartz | Landing page and ad copy that sounds like every other AI page | "Diagnose who's reading this page and rewrite it using Breakthrough Advertising" |
| 2 | **$100M Offers**, Alex Hormozi (plus the ten $100M Playbooks) | Weak offers, vague guarantees, price comparisons | "Build this into a Grand Slam Offer" |
| 3 | **The Challenger Sale**, Dixon and Adamson | Calls where you pitch too early and teach nothing | "Prep me for this call" / "grade this call" |
| 4 | **Pricing Creativity**, Blair Enns | Claude inventing prices out of thin air | "Price this project from the call transcript" |
| 5 | **Decisive**, Chip and Dan Heath | Claude agreeing with whatever you already lean towards | "I'm stuck on a decision, run me through it" |

## Why these five work as skills
- **Breakthrough Advertising** — diagnoses who is actually reading, how much
  they already know, and how many times they have heard the pitch, then writes
  for *them*.
- **$100M Offers** — a five-step process: score each piece on the value
  equation, trim and stack, add bonuses, write the guarantee.
- **The Challenger Sale** — a six-step framework that becomes a PREP/GRADE loop.
- **Pricing Creativity** — chosen specifically because its frameworks are
  *rules Claude can run*, not a mindset. It reads call transcripts, pulls what
  the client said the result is worth and what their budget is, and puts those
  two numbers together instead of inventing one.
- **Decisive** — a four-step process for when you're stuck, run one question at
  a time. Not about outsourcing the decision; about making Claude a thinking
  partner instead of the world's biggest yes-man.
