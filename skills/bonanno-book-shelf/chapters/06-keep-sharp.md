# 06 — Keep the shelf sharp

A book skill isn't finished when the conversion finishes. Three habits.

## 1. Fold your own results back in
Book-to-skill has an update mode. Point it at your own notes plus the existing
skill name and it merges the new material into the chapters and indexes:
```
/book-to-skill ~/Notes/offer-that-closed.md 100m-offers
```
Every time a book skill produces a win — a landing page that converted, a
proposal that closed — write a short note on what you gave it and what came
back, and fold it in. **The book's examples are good. Yours are better, because
they're in your market.**

## 2. Watch the front matter
The name and description of *every* skill loads into *every* session. Twenty
book skills is fine; twenty rambling descriptions is not. Keep each description
to one paragraph of trigger phrases.

**Diagnostic:** if Claude stops pulling a skill it used to pull, the description
is the first place to look.

## 3. Buy the book when the book is the point
The workaround gets you the frameworks. It does not get you Schwartz's
headlines. For the handful of books where the wording is the value, the copy on
your shelf *is* the skill, and it is worth what it costs.
