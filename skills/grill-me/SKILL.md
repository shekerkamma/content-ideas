Run a `/grilling` session, changing only how the frontier is worked.

Ask **one question at a time**. From everything on the frontier, pick the single most
load-bearing question — the one whose answer reshapes the most of the tree — put it to me
with your recommended answer, and wait for my answer before choosing the next.

Every other rule in `grilling` stands: map the design tree, find facts yourself rather
than asking me for them, dispatch a sub-agent when a fact needs digging, carry a
recommended answer on every question, and act only once I confirm we have reached a
shared understanding.
