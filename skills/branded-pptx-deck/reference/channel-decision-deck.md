# Channel Decision Deck Pattern

Use this pattern when a client-facing landscape must progress beyond company profiles into
ICP, GTM, channel readiness, validation actions, and evidence governance.

## Decision boundary

State the decision grain before scoring or authoring:

- A curated or editorial cohort is a **closed cohort**, not the whole market.
- Public evidence supports **candidate archetype readiness** and validation priorities.
- Named-partner fit requires a validated organization profile and local evidence.
- Do not imply investment, capital-allocation, or right-to-win conclusions unless the
  upstream analysis explicitly supports them.

Place the boundary on the executive-answer slide and repeat it in methodology. Do not hide
it only in notes.

## Recommended spine

Use five narrative blocks:

1. **Context** — executive answer, market/category map, business-model archetypes, ICP, GTM,
   and the startup-to-partner value chain.
2. **Companies** — consistent profiles using `Wedge → Proof → ICP + GTM → Risk`, supported
   by approved real assets when available.
3. **Partner lenses** — distributor, systems-integrator, and ecosystem-orchestrator views
   with motion-specific entry criteria.
4. **Actions** — `Act now → Validate next → Monitor`, bounded 90-day tests, activation
   triggers, proof packages, and kill criteria.
5. **Governance** — confidence and sensitivity, methodology, source freshness, rubric,
   evidence-ledger snapshot, and interview/calibration protocol.

Repeated profile and lens layouts are intentional comparison devices. Record that intent
in `deck-design.json`; waive a repetition heuristic only after inspecting the real render.

## Evidence and scoring

- Use qualitative readiness labels until a calibrated scoring model exists.
- Every posture must resolve to a strongest anchor, decisive gap, and next validation
  question.
- Source-independent evidence and coverage determine confidence; a hard-gate failure can
  override an otherwise attractive weighted posture.
- Keep company claims labeled as vendor claims.
- Put evidence URLs and detailed provenance in speaker notes while retaining concise native
  citations on-slide.

## Visual contract

- Use official product, facility, or brand imagery through `place-asset`; do not generate
  company proof.
- Keep titles, claims, tables, heatmaps, matrices, scoring rubrics, and process diagrams
  PowerPoint-native.
- Company tags must not compete with long action titles. Reserve a separate bounded region
  or shorten the tag.
- Dense cohort tables may use compact rows, but substantive text remains at least 12 pt.
  Citations, source lines, pagination, and asset captions may use the configured caption
  minimum.

## Reviewed promotion

In addition to the standard reviewed gate:

- verify the exact requested slide count;
- confirm the executive and methodology slides disclose the cohort boundary;
- confirm every company has the intended approved visual or an explicit `none` route;
- scan for substantive text below 12 pt separately from citations and pagination;
- verify every slide has evidence URLs in notes;
- inspect all contact sheets and a real Office render;
- document any deterministic-lint waiver by stable rule ID and concrete render evidence.
