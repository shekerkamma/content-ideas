The answer: sell the job, not the category.

Beacon is not strongest as “another DAP.” It is strongest as an AI implementation execution layer that configures, validates, tests, sequences, and supports enterprise go-lives inside the product UI.

Do not lead with

“We are a better DAP.” That invites WalkMe, Pendo, Whatfix, and Appcues comparisons on their strongest terrain.

“We execute implementation work.” That shifts the buyer conversation from adoption guidance to go-live throughput.

Evaluation spine

Decision standard: every competitor must be compared by the same buyer job and scoring dimensions, not by feature lists alone.

Three markets, one wedge.

Each adjacent market solves a legitimate buyer problem. Beacon must stay precise about the job it owns.

Digital adoption

WalkMe, Pendo, Whatfix, Appcues. They guide users, measure product behavior, improve adoption, and reduce support after or around launch.

Implementation operations

Rocketlane, GuideCX, Arrows, Dock. They coordinate onboarding projects, customer collaboration, resource planning, and handoffs.

Enterprise AI delivery

Unframe AI, UiPath, and automation incumbents. They deliver governed AI workflows and cross-system automation across many use cases.

Beacon’s wedge: execute implementation work that the other buckets mostly manage, guide, measure, or automate as fragments.

The pressure test.

These are the claims a sophisticated buyer will challenge first.

Weakest claim

“The knowledge graph compounds.” Beacon needs proof that later implementations improve because of platform learning.

Sharpest objection

“Is this just another coordination layer?” The answer has to be a live workflow execution trace.

Biggest risk

Incumbents can reframe Beacon as a feature unless Beacon owns execution, auditability, and implementation-specific learning.

AI transformation consultancies.

Accenture, BCG, McKinsey, Deloitte, IBM, and Capgemini validate Beacon’s market: AI value is won in operating-model execution, not model access.

What they sell

Strategy, sponsorship, workflow redesign, governance, change management, partner ecosystems, and managed delivery.

Why they matter

They control executive budget and can bundle AI implementation agents into broader transformation programs.

Beacon’s slot

Post-consultancy execution infrastructure: repeatable, auditable implementation automation for configuration, validation, UAT, cutover, and hypercare.
