## Operator metrics (Tier 2c — first-party engineering blogs)

- **Intercom** (intercom.com/blog/ai-is-approving-our-pull-requests, 2026-04):
  93% of PRs agent-driven across two main codebases; 19% auto-approved with
  no human reviewer; AI-authored backend revert rate 0.53% vs 5.39%
  human-authored (frontend 0.22% vs 2.00%); downtime from breaking changes
  −35% while deployments doubled; 6–16× faster time-to-approval at p75.
  Companion (Lenny's Newsletter podcast, 2026-04): R&D throughput doubled in
  9 months; 100% of engineers + designers/PMs ship via Claude Code.
- **Atlassian** (atlassian.com/blog/ai-at-work/developer-productivity-improved-with-rovo-dev,
  2026-04, ICSE 2026-recognized): AI code reviewer across 1,900+ repos cut
  median PR cycle time 30.8% and human review comments 35.6%.
- **Shopify** (shopify.engineering/under-the-river, 2026-05): 1 in 8 merged
  PRs company-wide co-authored by the River agent; 3,536 agent-coauthored
  PRs merged in 30 days; 59,918 agent sessions/30 days across 7,000+ people.

## Counter-evidence — ungoverned adoption (Tier 3, context)

- **Faros AI Engineering Report 2026** ("Acceleration Whiplash",
  pages.faros.ai): task completion +34% BUT bugs/developer +54%,
  incidents-to-PR ratio >3×, median PR review time +441%, 31% more PRs
  merged with no review. Thesis: governance, not generation, is the
  constraint — directly supports the earned-autonomy PoV.
