## The structural anchor: Four C's ↔ CIOS Mission

The video's two layers map 1:1 onto the frozen CIOS mission:

packs before pipelines, reference pack before phase-2 coverage.

## What CIOS adds beyond the video

The video describes a *personal* OS for a creator. CIOS extends it where a
consulting practice needs more:
1. **Domain packs** — pre-engineered, citable, tested context per consulting
   capability (the video has folders; CIOS has contracts).
2. **Source policy** — a closed, verifiable source registry instead of
   organic accumulation.
3. **Execution-grade spec** — RFC-2119 contracts so any agent (Claude Code,
   Codex, OpenHands) builds and operates it identically; the video's
   tool-agnosticism, made enforceable.
4. **Governance as gates, not habits** — freshness SLA, citation rule,
   QA gates, delivery statuses, decision records.

One-line anchor: **the video taught the mindset; Company Brain confirmed the
shape; CIOS is the same idea made contractual for a consulting business.**
