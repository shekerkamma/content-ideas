# White Space - Phase 1

## 1. Agent Governance Cockpit For Mid-Market Operators

- Unmet buyer problem: teams deploy AI workflows without a clear inventory, owner, policy, or audit trail.
- Why incumbents underserve it: enterprise platforms assume broad stack adoption; point tools focus on narrow eval/security slices.
- Category adjacency: governance, security, observability, AI operations.
- Target buyer: COO, CIO, head of automation, security/risk lead.
- Recommended wedge: inventory active agents, map tool/data access, define approval gates, track failures.
- Build difficulty: medium.
- GTM difficulty: medium.
- Proof needed: govern 10-20 workflows and prevent one clear policy or data-risk failure.

## 2. Fixed-Scope AI Implementation Diagnostic

- Unmet buyer problem: buyers pay for slow discovery before knowing whether the AI workflow is viable.
- Why incumbents underserve it: dev shops and consulting firms monetize ambiguity.
- Category adjacency: AI consulting, managed services, governance.
- Target buyer: founder, business unit leader, AI transformation owner.
- Recommended wedge: 10-skill operator audit with explicit proceed/kill/scope output.
- Build difficulty: low-to-medium.
- GTM difficulty: medium.
- Proof needed: paid diagnostic converting into a 30-day MVP or kill decision.

## 3. Vertical Workflow Agents With Auditable Handoff

- Unmet buyer problem: generic agents fail where domain workflow, compliance, and human review matter.
- Why incumbents underserve it: horizontal tools lack domain-specific acceptance criteria and audit trail.
- Category adjacency: vertical AI, governance, agent frameworks.
- Target buyer: healthcare, legal, finance, logistics, compliance-heavy operations.
- Recommended wedge: narrow workflow, explicit human handoff, acceptance tests, and source-of-truth logging.
- Build difficulty: medium-to-high.
- GTM difficulty: high.
- Proof needed: measurable cycle-time or cost reduction with no compliance failure.

## 4. AI Pricing And ROI Intelligence For Buyers

- Unmet buyer problem: seat-plus-usage and enterprise custom pricing make AI spend hard to forecast.
- Why incumbents underserve it: vendors benefit from opaque pricing.
- Category adjacency: procurement, FinOps, AI operations.
- Target buyer: CFO, procurement, CIO, AI platform owner.
- Recommended wedge: compare total cost across model/API, platform, observability, security, and services layers.
- Build difficulty: medium.
- GTM difficulty: medium.
- Proof needed: buyer uses analysis to renegotiate, reject, or choose a vendor package.
