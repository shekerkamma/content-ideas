# Enterprise AI Market Map - Phase 1

Status: `phase1_candidate_universe`

This market map organizes the initial company universe. Funding, pricing, and headcount require later source attachment.

## AI Consulting

- Company count: 9
- Representative companies: Accenture, Deloitte, McKinsey QuantumBlack, BCG X, Bain AI, IBM Consulting, EY, PwC, KPMG
- Buyer problem: strategy, operating-model change, governance, and implementation support.
- Maturity: high; differentiation depends on assets and repeatable delivery IP.
- Consolidation pressure: low-to-medium; services remain relationship-led.

## AI Development Platforms

- Company count: 3
- Representative companies: Hugging Face, Databricks, Anyscale
- Buyer problem: build, deploy, evaluate, and operate AI applications.
- Maturity: mid; platform boundaries overlap with infra and observability.
- Consolidation pressure: medium-to-high.

## AI Operations

- Company count: 1
- Representative companies: Weights & Biases

## Agent Frameworks

- Company count: 12
- Representative companies: LangChain, LlamaIndex, CrewAI, deepset, Microsoft AutoGen, Microsoft Semantic Kernel, AutoGPT, Dust, Relevance AI, Zapier, Manus, H Company
- Buyer problem: compose tools, memory, orchestration, and workflow logic around models.
- Maturity: early-to-mid; developer adoption is strong but enterprise reliability is uneven.
- Consolidation pressure: medium; frameworks can become platform features.

## Developer Platforms

- Company count: 9
- Representative companies: GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Replit, Cognition, Poolside, Sourcegraph Cody, Tabnine, Codeium/Windsurf, JetBrains AI
- Buyer problem: accelerate software delivery and codebase understanding.
- Maturity: high adoption, intense competition.
- Consolidation pressure: high from GitHub, IDEs, and frontier model labs.

## Enterprise Platforms

- Company count: 12
- Representative companies: Snowflake, Microsoft Copilot, Salesforce Agentforce, ServiceNow, IBM watsonx, SAP Joule, Oracle AI, Workday AI, Atlassian Rovo, Adobe Firefly, Box AI, Zoom AI Companion
- Buyer problem: put AI inside governed systems of record and workflow platforms.
- Maturity: high distribution, uneven agent quality.
- Consolidation pressure: high; incumbents bundle AI into existing contracts.

## Foundation Models

- Company count: 12
- Representative companies: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta AI, Mistral AI, Cohere, AI21 Labs, xAI, DeepSeek, Perplexity, Aleph Alpha, Writer
- Buyer problem: access to frontier reasoning, multimodal, coding, and enterprise-safe models.
- Maturity: high at the platform layer, still volatile on cost/performance and differentiation.
- Consolidation pressure: high; distribution and compute access matter.

## Governance

- Company count: 7
- Representative companies: Patronus AI, Giskard, Credo AI, Holistic AI, Arthur AI, Monitaur, Truera
- Buyer problem: prove AI systems are compliant, explainable, tested, and controlled.
- Maturity: early-to-mid; regulation and procurement are demand drivers.
- Consolidation pressure: medium.

## Infrastructure

- Company count: 11
- Representative companies: NVIDIA, AMD, CoreWeave, Lambda, Crusoe, Together AI, Fireworks AI, Modal, Replicate, Baseten, RunPod
- Buyer problem: reliable compute and inference capacity without owning the full data-center stack.
- Maturity: high demand, constrained by GPU economics and operational complexity.
- Consolidation pressure: high; hyperscalers and chip vendors shape margins.

## Knowledge Management

- Company count: 11
- Representative companies: Glean, Hebbia, Unstructured, Pinecone, Weaviate, Chroma, Elastic, Coveo, Sana, Guru, Notion AI
- Buyer problem: make enterprise knowledge searchable, answerable, and action-ready.
- Maturity: mid-to-high; many products converge on enterprise search plus agents.
- Consolidation pressure: high from Microsoft, Google, Atlassian, and data platforms.

## Managed Services

- Company count: 7
- Representative companies: Capgemini, Cognizant, TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCLTech, Coforge
- Buyer problem: outsource implementation, integration, operations, and maintenance.
- Maturity: high; margin pressure rises as AI delivery commoditizes.
- Consolidation pressure: medium.

## Observability

- Company count: 11
- Representative companies: Arize AI, LangSmith, Langfuse, Helicone, HoneyHive, Galileo, WhyLabs, Fiddler AI, Datadog, New Relic, Dynatrace
- Buyer problem: trace, evaluate, debug, and monitor LLM/agent behavior.
- Maturity: early-to-mid; fast-moving because agent failures are hard to inspect.
- Consolidation pressure: high from Datadog/New Relic/Dynatrace and dev platforms.

## Security

- Company count: 6
- Representative companies: Lakera, Protect AI, HiddenLayer, CalypsoAI, Robust Intelligence, Aporia
- Buyer problem: prevent prompt injection, data leakage, model abuse, and AI supply-chain risk.
- Maturity: early; urgency is rising as agents gain tool access.
- Consolidation pressure: medium; security suites may absorb point solutions.

## Vertical AI

- Company count: 14
- Representative companies: Harvey, Abridge, Ambience Healthcare, Hippocratic AI, Innovaccer, Nabla, EvenUp, EliseAI, Sierra, Decagon, Intercom Fin, Ada
- Buyer problem: domain-specific AI workflows with compliance and workflow context.
- Maturity: varies by vertical; legal, healthcare, and support are most visible.
- Consolidation pressure: medium; vertical incumbents will embed AI.
