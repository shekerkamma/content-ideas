# Funding Analysis - Phase 1

Status: `provisional`

Funding data is not finalized in this phase. This file identifies where funding concentration is likely to matter and what must be source-attached in the next pass.

## Funding Concentration By Category

1. Foundation Models
   - Capital intensity is structurally high because training and inference require large compute commitments.
   - Strategic value is high, but differentiation is exposed to benchmark churn and model commoditization.

2. Infrastructure
   - GPU cloud, inference, and deployment platforms are advantaged by enterprise AI demand.
   - Margins depend on utilization, hardware access, and reserved-capacity economics.

3. Developer Platforms
   - Coding agents and AI-native IDEs are one of the clearest paid adoption zones.
   - Funding attention follows visible developer usage and enterprise conversion.

4. Vertical AI
   - Legal, healthcare, customer support, real estate, and operations-specific agents show strong wedge potential.
   - Funding quality depends on workflow ownership, compliance readiness, and measurable labor displacement.
