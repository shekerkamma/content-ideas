# Impeccable direction decision

## Candidate set

1. Conservation proof table — incumbent archival paper/charcoal report system.
2. Swiss decision wall — objective modular grid, white field, one signal color, poster-series rhythm.
3. Factory catalog — matte black, sparse coded strips, strict index navigation, one dominant plot.
4. Data-sublime field — monochrome barcode density, frame indices, full-field inversion.
5. Control-room strip board — operational state lanes, cyan/amber alerts, console readouts.
6. Film-edit decision timeline — evidence clips, edit points, accepted cut, exception markers.
7. Decision Flight Recorder — dark operational canvas, multicolor signal tracks, numbered decision
   states, oversized verdicts, and authentic screens treated as instrument readouts.

The Impeccable direction seed assigned candidate 7. It was retained because it identifies with an
executive operating-model decision while making the product mechanism clearer than the incumbent
proof-table treatment.

## Representative slides used

- Slide 1: decision-first cover and authorization readout.
- Slide 4: exact Worlds evidence as an instrument panel with an explicit management implication.
- Slide 10: human/system responsibility allocation as a native decision board.

## Material differences from the incumbent deck

- Replaces warm editorial paper as the default with a dark operational field and intermittent
  high-contrast white analysis surfaces.
- Replaces vermilion/moss archival semantics with a four-signal state system: cyan evidence, lime
  commitment, violet comparison, coral escalation.
- Replaces conventional title-and-body pacing with numbered readouts, signal tracks, state rails,
  and oversized verdict typography.
- Makes authentic video frames dominant instrument readouts rather than screenshots beside report
  annotations.
- Applies the same grammar across cover, summary, mechanism, proof, matrix, maturity, experiment,
  and closing slides instead of limiting visual authorship to isolated diagrams.

## Rejected alternatives

- Swiss wall was clear but too close to conventional consulting modularity.
- Factory catalog was distinctive but its encoded labels reduced immediate executive comprehension.
- Data-sublime treatment over-indexed on spectacle and weakened the source-evidence boundary.
