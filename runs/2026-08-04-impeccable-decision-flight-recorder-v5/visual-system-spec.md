# Decision Flight Recorder visual system

## Signal semantics

- Cyan: observed evidence and fixed inputs.
- Lime: commitment, authorization, and controlled progress.
- Violet: comparison, alternatives, and operator choice.
- Coral: escalation, risk, exception, and unproven outcomes.

## Reusable archetypes

1. Decision readout: oversized verdict plus authorization panel.
2. Signal ledger: numbered rows, evidence state, and decision bar.
3. State track: sequenced operational nodes joined by direction arrows and signal rails.
4. Instrument proof: dominant authentic screen plus one management readout.
5. Decision matrix: four operational states using signal-colored rails.
6. Boundary ladder: demonstrated and unproven regions separated explicitly.

The native PowerPoint builder is the editable source for all six archetypes. Exact source screens
remain unchanged assets. No generated imagery is used.
