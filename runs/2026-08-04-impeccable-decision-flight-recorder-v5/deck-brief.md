# Deck brief

- Audience: executives deciding whether to authorize an Impeccable pilot.
- Decision: authorize one matched-task pilot; reject enterprise rollout from demonstration evidence.
- Narrative promise: reveal where structured choice helps, where human judgment remains mandatory,
  and how to test value without letting showcase quality redefine success.
- Evidence standard: authentic interface states for demonstrations; native analytical models for
  interpretation; no inferred productivity, usability, or business-outcome claims.
- Visual direction: Decision Flight Recorder—operational signal tracks, numbered states, dominant
  readouts, authentic screens as instrument evidence.
- Editability: PowerPoint-native diagrams and text plus three source-backed raster evidence states.
- Anti-reference: archival report template, screenshot chronology, generic rounded-card consulting deck.
