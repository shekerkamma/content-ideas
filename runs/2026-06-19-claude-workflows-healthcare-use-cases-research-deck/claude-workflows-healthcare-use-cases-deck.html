CHAPTER 04 · COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORKHIPAA · CMS · FDA · ONC · ICH

Every Use Case Has a Governing Framework

HIPAA BAA
All PHI processing requires a Business Associate Agreement. Anthropic offers enterprise BAA coverage for all Claude deployments handling patient data.
CMS-9115-F
Mandates electronic prior authorization by January 2027. Creates regulatory tailwind for PA automation — the CMS clock is running for every payer and provider.
CMS Info Block
Requires patient-accessible report summaries. Claude generates them at appropriate reading levels (≤6th grade) automatically as part of the discharge workflow.
FDA AI/ML 2024
Supports AI-assisted pharmacovigilance with human oversight. Sets the standard for autonomous adverse event parsing and MedWatch submission drafting.
ONC CDS Rules
Requires explainability for AI clinical decision support. Human-in-the-loop is mandatory for clinical decisions; administrative workflows (PA, coding, appeals) can fully automate.
