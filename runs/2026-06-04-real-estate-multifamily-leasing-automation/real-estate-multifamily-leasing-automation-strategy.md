# Full Strategy: Multifamily Leasing & Resident Communication Automation

## Strategic thesis

Multifamily leasing automation is worth pursuing when the offer is framed around response speed, occupancy conversion, and workflow reliability inside the current PMS/CRM stack.

## Why the wedge works

- Leasing teams manage repetitive, time-sensitive communication.
- Vacancy drag creates a concrete ROI story.
- Operators already tolerate outsourced tooling and managed workflow support.

## Ideal entry point

1. Ingest leasing inquiries and resident messages from web, listings, email, and SMS.
2. Classify intent and urgency.
3. Draft replies, update systems, and route next actions to the right team.
4. Expand into application packets, renewals, and resident-document workflows.

## Solution-stack recommendation

- PMS/CRM remains the system of action.
- OpenHands handles orchestration, workflow agents, and reusable skill logic.
- Docusign or equivalent document systems handle agreement execution.
- Reporting focuses on response time, tour progression, application completion, renewal progress, and occupancy conversion.

## Delivery model

- Phase 1: centralized leasing-response workflow
- Phase 2: renewals and lease-document automation
- Phase 3: resident-service workflow packaging and portfolio expansion

## Risks

- Poor resident experience if automation becomes too autonomous
- Tool fragmentation across operators
- Fair-housing and communication-policy concerns
- Workflow adoption risk at the site-team level

## Controls

- Keep sensitive decisions human-approved
- Start assistive, not fully autonomous
- Encode policy rules in workflow skills
- Roll out by portfolio, not enterprise-wide on day one

## Recommended account angle

Target scaled operators that already centralize leasing or resident workflows. Greystar is the cleanest named angle because the operating scale can magnify savings from faster response, cleaner handoffs, and reusable workflow automation.
