# Executive Brief: Multifamily Leasing & Resident Communication Automation

## Bottom line

This is a credible real-estate AI engineering offer when positioned as occupancy protection and admin compression inside the leasing workflow, not as a generic resident chatbot.

## Why now

- Property operators still lose prospects through slow response and disconnected workflows.
- Software incumbents like AppFolio and Yardi are already training the market to expect AI support in leasing and resident communication.
- Agreement and document automation make lease packets and renewals a realistic phase-two expansion path.

## What to sell

Sell a workflow package that:

1. triages leasing inquiries across channels
2. drafts or queues follow-up inside the PMS/CRM stack
3. triggers tours, applications, renewals, and resident workflow tasks
4. keeps humans in control for sensitive resident interactions

## Recommended implementation posture

- Keep the PMS and CRM as the user-facing systems of action.
- Use OpenHands internally for orchestration, skills, guardrails, and repeatable workflow delivery.
- Start with one region, portfolio, or centralized leasing group.

## Verdict

**GO**

This lane has strong workflow structure, measurable business value, and enough incumbent activity to validate the market without making entry impossible.
