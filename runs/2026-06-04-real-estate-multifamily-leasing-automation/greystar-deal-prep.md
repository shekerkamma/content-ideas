# Deal Prep: Greystar

## Why Greystar fits

- Greystar's scale makes centralized leasing and resident workflow automation economically meaningful.
- Large operators have enough workflow volume that even modest response-time or conversion gains compound.
- The pitch is not a generic AI assistant. It is a reusable internal workflow layer for leasing, renewals, and resident communications.

## Positioning

Frame the offer as:

- faster leasing response
- fewer dropped prospects
- cleaner handoffs between leasing, resident services, and regional operations
- a repeatable pattern for expansion into renewals and document workflows

## Discovery questions

1. Where do prospects still wait too long for first response?
2. Which steps still bounce between inboxes, PMS records, and manual task lists?
3. How centralized is leasing today versus site-led execution?
4. Where do renewals or resident-document workflows stall?

## Likely objections

- "We already have software for that."
  - Response: the opportunity is workflow execution across systems, not another surface UI.
- "Resident communication risk is high."
  - Response: start assistive and keep approvals for sensitive messages.
- "Every property operates differently."
  - Response: encode site and regional rules as reusable skills instead of rebuilding the workflow each time.

## Suggested next step

Offer a pilot for one regional portfolio or centralized leasing team with metrics tied to response time, tour progression, and application/renewal completion.
