# Executive Brief: Brokerage Commission & Deal Payout Automation

## Bottom line

This is a credible brokerage AI engineering offer when positioned as payout accuracy, faster back-office throughput, and cleaner transaction-to-accounting workflows.

## Why now

- Commission processing is still rules-heavy and manual in many brokerages.
- Incumbents like Brokermint prove the market already buys commission automation and back-office workflow software.
- Document-state and transaction-state synchronization create a natural expansion path into broader brokerage operations.

## What to sell

Sell a workflow package that:

1. validates transaction and payout data
2. applies commission logic and flags exceptions
3. drafts approval, reconciliation, and payout tasks
4. keeps finance and brokerage ops in control through auditable workflows

## Recommended implementation posture

- Keep the brokerage back office and accounting tools as the systems of action.
- Use OpenHands internally for orchestration, policy logic, exception handling, and repeatable workflow delivery.
- Start with one payout queue or one brokerage ops team.

## Verdict

**GO**

The lane has strong workflow structure, measurable ROI, and a clean operational buyer.
