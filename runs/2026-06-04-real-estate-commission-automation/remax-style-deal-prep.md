# Deal Prep: RE/MAX-Style Brokerage Network

## Why this account angle fits

- Scaled brokerage networks feel the pain of payout delays, commission exceptions, and disconnected back-office workflows.
- Even modest improvements in payout speed and exception handling compound at network scale.
- The pitch is workflow execution and operating control, not another front-end UI.

## Positioning

Frame the offer as:

- fewer payout errors
- faster commission readiness and approvals
- cleaner handoffs between transaction, finance, and broker operations
- reusable workflow patterns across offices or teams

## Discovery questions

1. Where do payout exceptions pile up today?
2. Which commission-plan variations create the most manual work?
3. How disconnected are transaction state, accounting state, and document state?
4. How much of the payout workflow still depends on spreadsheets or inbox approvals?

## Likely objections

- "We already have software for commissions."
  - Response: the gap is usually workflow orchestration across systems, exceptions, and approvals.
- "Finance needs full control."
  - Response: keep release authority human-controlled and use the workflow layer for validation and routing only.
- "Every office runs differently."
  - Response: encode office and plan variance as reusable workflow skills instead of rebuilding each flow manually.

## Suggested next step

Offer a pilot for one payout queue or one brokerage ops team with metrics tied to payout cycle time, exception rate, and approval latency.
