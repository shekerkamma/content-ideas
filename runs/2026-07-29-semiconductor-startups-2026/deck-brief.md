# Deck brief

## Audience

One alliance/channel strategy leader aligning distributor, systems-integrator, and
ecosystem-orchestrator perspectives. The reader understands AI infrastructure at a
business level but does not need transistor-level detail.

## Decision

Decide which companies in CRN's ten-company closed cohort merit action, bounded
validation, or monitoring—and which partner archetype and proof package to test next.

## Narrative promise

The 2026 cohort is attacking distinct infrastructure bottlenecks. Public evidence can
prioritize archetype-level validation actions, while named-partner fit and
organization-specific right-to-win remain gated on calibration and local evidence.

## Voice and tone

Executive, precise, curious, and evidence-led. Distinguish company claims from independent
facts. Avoid hype, unexplained acronyms, and predictions presented as certainties.

## Anti-references

Logo walls, ten repetitive biography pages, generic circuit-board decoration, dark
cyberpunk imagery, dense funding tables, unsupported rankings, and “Nvidia killer”
headlines that erase the distinct workload and go-to-market strategies.

## Evidence and editability

CRN defines the ten-company cohort. Gartner anchors the market context. Product, funding,
availability, and architecture claims use company announcements or other primary sources
where available. Company performance statements are labeled as vendor claims. Titles,
cards, diagrams, comparisons, citations, and matrices must remain PowerPoint-native and
editable. No generated imagery is required.

## Success criteria

- The slide-title sequence tells the argument by itself.
- Each slide carries one main message and structured supporting evidence.
- The deck contains exactly 44 slides and covers every CRN-listed company.
- Every factual slide maps to at least one evidence ID and shows a concise citation.
- Company profiles use a consistent four-part lens: wedge, proof point, ICP/GTM, and risk.
- Dedicated sections compare business models, ICPs, GTM, distributor/SI/ecosystem
  readiness, proof packages, confidence, and validation actions.
- Slides 2 and 40 disclose the closed-cohort and public-evidence boundaries.
- The reviewed deck passes context, visual-spec, structural, design-lint, and render QA.
