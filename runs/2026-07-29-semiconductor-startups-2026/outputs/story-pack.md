# Story pack — semiconductor startups 2026

## BLUF

The 2026 semiconductor startup cohort is fragmenting the AI infrastructure stack into
distinct workload, memory, and connectivity wedges; the most credible near-term strategy is
heterogeneous adoption supported by strong integration ecosystems.

## Audience decision

Choose which startup archetypes deserve technical diligence, partnership discovery,
customer validation, or quarterly monitoring.

## Tension

AI infrastructure budgets are expanding, but so are power, memory-movement, software, and
network bottlenecks. The risk is to treat every startup as an interchangeable “GPU
challenger” and miss the different insertion points and execution requirements.

## Argument arc

1. Establish the market expansion and the ten-company cohort.
2. Reframe the landscape around four system bottlenecks.
3. Show how capital is financing multiple paths—not one architecture.
4. Examine seven compute companies through a consistent wedge/proof/insertion/risk lens.
5. Examine three connectivity companies as complementary AI scaling infrastructure.
6. Compare commercialization maturity with architectural specialization.
7. Convert the landscape into partner actions and a diligence watchlist.

## Evidence map

- **Direct evidence:** named cohort, financing announcements, product availability,
  architecture descriptions, and stated product specifications.
- **Fair synthesis:** four-bottleneck taxonomy, breadth-versus-specialization comparison,
  and heterogeneous adoption thesis.
- **Interpretation:** directional maturity matrix, channel implications, and diligence risks.

## Content cuts

- No founder biographies unless they explain a product or ecosystem advantage.
- No unsourced market-share projections or invented TAM allocations.
- No company logos, screenshots, or chip renders whose reuse rights are unclear.
- No “winner” ranking; companies operate at different layers and maturity stages.

## Rebuild instructions

- Use the navy/teal/gold branded system and native PowerPoint objects.
- Keep every company profile to four evidence-backed cards.
- Label vendor performance claims and analytical judgments explicitly.
- Use section dividers to prevent ten profiles from becoming visually repetitive.
- End with partner actions, risks, conclusion, and methodology.
