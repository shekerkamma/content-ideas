## Caveats

- CRN defines “coolest”; the deck does not convert that editorial selection into a ranking.
- Funding figures are labeled as latest round or cumulative total and are not summed.
- Company performance numbers are vendor claims unless the slide states otherwise.
- Public availability and sampling statements do not prove production-scale adoption.
