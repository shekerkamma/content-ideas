# Company Dossier — Sierra

> **One-line:** The enterprise customer-experience agent that gets paid only when the AI resolves the case — outcome pricing as the entire positioning, sold on brand relationship, not cost-cutting.

## 2. Origin & founders — why this team could win
Taylor and Bavor met at Google in 2005 (Taylor hired Bavor as an APM). Taylor's resume is the moat before the product exists: Google Maps → FriendFeed (sold to Facebook) → Facebook CTO (the "Like" button) → Quip (sold to Salesforce, $750M) → Salesforce co-CEO → OpenAI chairman. After leaving Salesforce in 2023, Bavor took him to lunch and they started Sierra. **Lesson:** when everyone went horizontal (ChatGPT-wrapper UIs), they went *vertical and narrow* — customer service — and recruited 4 design partners to build in stealth for 11 months. Founder brand + enterprise-software instinct *is* the GTM in a trust-gated market.

## 3. The business problem & ICP
$400B/yr is spent on customer service — "the last analog channel." A human-handled US call costs ~$10 (salary + benefits + supervision + 6–12 min). Sierra's per-resolution cost: **$0.10–$1.00.** ICP = Fortune 1000 with **>$100M/yr contact-center spend** (below that, slow enterprise cycle); heavily regulated (financial services, healthcare). The pain is concrete and the unit economics pick the customer list.

## 4. The solution — what they actually do
**Sierra Agent OS:** build, optimize, personalize, scale agents. One agent across chat, SMS, WhatsApp, email, **voice/IVR**, and ChatGPT, in 34+ languages. **Ghostwriter** turns SOPs/transcripts/whiteboard photos into a production-ready agent with guardrails — CX teams go live in *weeks, no engineering*. **Agent Data Platform** unifies conversation data with systems of record (billing, inventory, policy) — memory + next-best-action — moving agents from support into sales, loyalty, retention. Runs on a "constellation of models" + Sierra's fine-tuned proprietary layers.

## 5. Service offerings & USP
1. **Outcome-based pricing** — paid only when the AI resolves the case with no human; escalation is free. The pricing *is* the product positioning.
2. **Speed-to-live** — production agent in weeks without engineers (the real lever under outcome pricing).
3. **Brand-aligned reliability** — positioned as "show up at your best," not cost-cutting; built for the brand, not a generic bot.
4. **Regulated-enterprise trust** — security/compliance for FS + healthcare; Bret Taylor's enterprise credibility opens Fortune 50 doors.
5. **Agent Data Platform** — memory + decisioning that compounds per customer.

## 6. Positioning move & pattern mapping
**Verbatim:** *"Pay for a job well done."* · *"helping companies build deeper relationships with their customers, versus short-term cost cutting and automation."*
- **Primary → #3 Services-as-Software + outcome pricing.** Pricing manifesto (Dec 2024): paid only on resolution; sales = commission. "Wherever we can align our interests with the client's, we choose it."
- **#5 Replacement-leaning** — automates work humans did, but framed as relationship-building not headcount-cutting.
- **#6 Data moat (emerging)** — Agent Data Platform memory as the compounding asset.

## 7. Product mechanics that matter
Agent OS 2.0 + Agent Studio 2.0 (CX builds without eng) + Agent Data Platform (memory/decisioning) + supervised agent updates with full change visibility ("review, validate, ship with confidence"). Multi-channel single-agent is the technical wedge.

## 8. The moat
1. **Outcome accountability** — pricing forces the product to actually work; aligns retention.
2. **Speed-to-live** — the operational lever competitors underestimate.
3. **Enterprise brand trust** — Taylor/Bavor + Fortune-50 references.
4. **Regulated compliance** — FS/healthcare guardrails.
5. **Agent Data Platform flywheel** — memory + systems-of-record integration deepens with use; NDR expands as agents move support→sales→loyalty.

## 9. GTM + pricing
- **Pricing:** per-resolution (no human = pre-negotiated rate; escalation = free); sales = commission. Buyer-funded against a $10/call status quo.
- **GTM:** 4 design partners → stealth build → marquee enterprise references (Cigna, ADT, SoFi) → category-leader narrative. ~$400B TAM framing.

## 10. Traction & proof
$0 → $100M ARR in 7 quarters (one of the fastest in enterprise-software history) → ~$165M (May 2026); 40% of Fortune 50; $15.8B valuation; per-resolution cost 10–100x below human.

## 11. Competitors & differentiation
- **Decagon** — per-resolution, $250M Series D; strongest in AI-native SaaS.
- **Intercom Fin** — $0.99/resolution + $1M guarantee; mid-market.
- **Ada, Salesforce Agentforce, PolyAI** (voice), Zendesk/kore.ai.
- **Sierra edge:** enterprise/regulated brand trust + multi-channel single agent + Agent Data Platform + founder pedigree.

## 12. Lessons for OUR positioning
- **Make pricing the positioning** — outcome/per-result pricing forces product quality and is itself the pitch.
- **The lever *under* outcome pricing is speed-to-live** — sell time-to-value, not just alignment.
- **Go vertical + narrow first**, then expand the agent's job (support → sales → loyalty).
- **Found with a brand the buyer already trusts** in a trust-gated market.
- **Frame as relationship/upside, not cost-cutting** — different (larger) budget, less procurement resistance.
