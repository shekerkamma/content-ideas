# Company Dossier — Cursor (Anysphere)

> **One-line:** The fastest-scaling B2B software company in history — an AI-native code editor that augments the developer (never replaces them) and won with zero marketing spend.

## 2. Origin & founders — why this team could win
Four MIT CS grads incorporated Anysphere in 2022 with a $400K pre-seed — and spent their **first year building the wrong product** (CAD / mechanical-engineering tools), hit data scarcity, and kept gravitating back to code. Pivoted to an AI-native editor early 2023, shipped the MVP alongside GPT-4 (Mar 2023). Initial hype, then usage tanked. The turning point: **Cmd+K instructed edits + codebase indexing** (ask anything about your whole codebase). Truell had built the Halite programming game in high school — tools for coders was the through-line. **Lesson:** admitting you're building the wrong product for people you don't understand is the unlock.

## 3. The business problem & ICP
AI coding tools were capped by **bolting AI onto existing IDEs** instead of reimagining the editor from first principles. ICP started as individual professional developers (bottom-up, word of mouth) and became **60% enterprise revenue by 2026** — classic bottom-up-to-enterprise.

## 4. The solution — what they actually do
A **fork of VS Code** (fully compatible with extensions/settings = zero switching cost), with: **Tab** (predicts the next *edit*/diff with whole-codebase context, not just the next token like Copilot), **Cmd+K** instructed edits, **AI Chat** (entire codebase as context), **Composer** (multi-file agentic editing from a task description), **Background Agents** (async, larger tasks). Vertically integrated proprietary models: **Fusion** (Tab), **Composer** (agentic, RL-trained, Oct 2025), **Composer 2** (Mar 2026, continued-pretrained on open Kimi K2.5 + large-scale RL).

## 5. Service offerings & USP
1. **Fastest, smartest autocomplete** — Tab predicts edits across the whole codebase; the daily-use hook.
2. **Augment, not replace** — "force multiplier for engineers who already code"; the developer stays in the driver's seat.
3. **Zero switching cost** — VS Code fork; adopt in minutes (Adapter pattern).
4. **Proprietary models** — cheaper high-volume inference; gross-margin positive since Composer.
5. **Word-of-mouth distribution** — $1M→$100M ARR with *zero marketing spend*.

## 6. Positioning move & pattern mapping
**Verbatim:** AI-native code editor; "we keep developers central — AI makes them 10x more productive."
- **Primary → #8 Interface paradigm** (own the editor surface) + **#1 Adapter** (VS Code fork = no switching).
- **#5 Augment pole** — the deliberate opposite of Cognition/Devin's "replace the engineer." Both win; the *clarity of the chosen pole* is the lesson.

## 7. Product mechanics that matter
Tab/Fusion (edit prediction), Composer (agentic multi-file), codebase indexing, Background Agents; proprietary models let Cursor run cheap inference on the high-volume Tab/Auto features — the unit-economics unlock behind the margins.

## 8. The moat
1. **Bottom-up → enterprise lock-in** — devs adapt workflows, prompts, and codebases to Cursor; switching cost rises organically.
2. **Proprietary models** — cost + capability advantage incumbents renting APIs lack.
3. **Brand** — "fastest-scaling SaaS ever" is its own funnel.
4. **Capital** — $3.4B+ raised; now inside SpaceX/xAI ($60B).

## 9. GTM + pricing
- **GTM:** zero marketing; word of mouth from devs at OpenAI, Midjourney, Perplexity, Shopify → teams → enterprise (60%). Hired ~1 person every 2–3 months to $100M ARR.
- **Pricing:** Free/Hobby · **Pro $20/mo** (incl. $20 AI credits) · Business $40/user · Enterprise custom (SOC 2, usage pools). Hybrid subscription + usage credits.

## 10. Traction & proof
Fastest B2B SaaS to $1B and $2B ARR on record; $2B ARR with ~50 people; valuation $400M → $60B in under two years.

## 11. Competitors & differentiation
- **GitHub Copilot** — distribution (90% Fortune 100), $10/mo; default-extension reach.
- **Claude Code** — terminal-native, dev mindshare lead.
- **Replit / Lovable** — non-developer builder canvas (different persona).
- **Cognition/Devin + Windsurf** — autonomous-agent pole.
- **Cursor edge:** revenue leader + augment clarity + proprietary models + word-of-mouth gravity.

## 12. Lessons for OUR positioning
- **Own the surface** — a fork/new interface is Adapter *and* new-paradigm at once (zero switching + control).
- **Pick augment-vs-replace loudly** — Cursor's clarity (augment) beat muddier rivals.
- **Word-of-mouth bottom-up** beats a sales team when the product is daily-use and the value is obvious in minutes.
- **Vertically integrate the model once you have the usage data** — it flips margins and adds a moat.
