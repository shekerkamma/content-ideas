# Company Dossier — Cognition (Devin)

> **One-line:** "The AI software engineer" — the company that branded full autonomy before the models could deliver it, then bought the opposite pole (Windsurf's IDE) to own both sides of AI coding.

## 1. Snapshot
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Founded | 2023 (a New York apartment), now San Francisco; entity Cognition Labs |
| Founder/CEO | Scott Wu (IOI gold medalist; ex-founding engineer at Scale AI) + co-founders (decade-long friends, competitive programmers) |
| Category | Autonomous AI software engineer (the Replacement pole) |
| ARR (product) | Devin $1M (Sep 2024) → **$73M (Jun 2025)**; Windsurf $82M at acquisition; company-wide **$492M annualized run-rate** (May 2026) |
| Valuation | $21M seed (Mar 2024, Founders Fund) → $10.2B (Sep 2025, $400M, Founders Fund) → **$26B** (Series D, May 2026) |
| Capital efficiency | total net burn **<$20M** across the company's history |
| Key move | acquired **Windsurf** (Jul 2025) — the agentic IDE — after Google reverse-acquihired Windsurf's CEO/cofounders ($2.4B) and OpenAI's $3B offer expired |
| Customers | 350+ enterprise customers (via Windsurf), "hundreds of thousands" of DAUs |

## 2. Origin & founders — why this team could win
A small group of engineers with a decade of friendship hunkered down in a NYC apartment and "built the product we always wanted for ourselves." Scott Wu is a world-class competitive programmer (IOI gold) and was a founding engineer at Scale AI — pedigree that signals "these people can actually build the hard thing." Launched **Devin on March 12, 2024** as "the world's first AI software engineer" — a deliberately bold *replace-the-engineer* claim. Early reviews found Devin made mistakes ("ahead of its time"); the bet was that models would catch up to the positioning. They did.

## 3. The business problem & ICP
Thesis: "a world of software abundance where **engineers become architects**, tasking an army of autonomous agents." 30M software engineers, each to be made ~10x. ICP = enterprise engineering teams where "Devin is already a top contributor"; 350+ enterprise customers post-Windsurf.

## 4. The solution — what they actually do
**Devin** = an autonomous agent that plans, writes, debugs, tests, and deploys multi-step tasks **asynchronously** — assign it a Jira ticket, get back a PR, like a junior engineer. **Windsurf** = the agentic IDE for when you want to make each decision yourself with AI speedup. Cognition now sells **both forms**: agent (delegate complete tasks) + IDE (augment). Recent: Devin 2.2, Devin Desktop, **Devin in Windsurf** (Apr 2026), the SWE-1.6 model.

## 5. Service offerings & USP
1. **Full autonomy** — the autopilot pole; "the AI software engineer," not an assistant.
2. **The complete suite** — after Windsurf, Cognition owns IDE *and* agent (the two forms engineers want).
3. **Usage pricing ~by the hour, ~10x cheaper than human time** — anchored to a $300K engineer.
4. **Capital efficiency** — <$20M net burn to a $26B valuation; a moat of its own.
5. **Elite-talent pedigree** — Olympiad medalists + Scale AI alumni signal "can build the hard thing."

## 6. Positioning move & pattern mapping
**Verbatim:** *"Devin, the AI software engineer"* · *"engineers become architects."*
- **Primary → #5 Replacement (autopilot).** The explicit opposite of Cursor's augment pole — "automate them completely, as if it were a junior software engineer."
- **#8 Interface (acquired)** — buying Windsurf added the augment IDE, so Cognition now spans both poles instead of betting only on replace.

## 7. Product mechanics that matter
Async multi-step execution in a sandbox; Devin assigned tickets returns PRs; Windsurf IDE with latest Claude models; SWE-1.6 in-house model; "How Cognition uses Devin to build Devin" (dogfooding). The bet: autonomy quality compounds as models improve.

## 8. The moat
1. **Category brand** — "the AI software engineer," first mover on full autonomy.
2. **Enterprise GTM** — Windsurf's 350+ customers + scaled go-to-market machine.
3. **Two-product suite** — IDE + agent under one roof.
4. **Capital efficiency** — <$20M net burn = optionality + investor confidence.
5. **Talent** — competitive-programming pedigree. *Weaker on retention than Cursor* (early quality reputation to overcome).

## 9. GTM + pricing
- **Pricing:** usage-based, ~by-the-hour, "~10x cheaper than the value of your time"; retired Core (pay-as-you-go) + Team ($500/mo) for a 5-tier ladder (Apr 2026); enterprise custom.
- **GTM:** Windsurf acquisition supplied the scaled GTM machine + a bottom-up IDE funnel feeding the top-down Devin agent sale.

## 10. Traction & proof
Devin $1M → $73M ARR in 9 months; company-wide $492M annualized run-rate (May 2026); $26B valuation; <$20M lifetime net burn.

## 11. Competitors & differentiation
- **Cursor** — augment pole, $2–3B ARR, the revenue leader.
- **GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, OpenAI Codex** — assistant/agent hybrids.
- **Cognition edge:** full-autonomy brand + IDE-plus-agent suite + enterprise base + capital efficiency.

## 12. Lessons for OUR positioning
- **Brand the category before the tech is ready** — claim the pole ("the AI software engineer"), let the models catch up to the positioning.
- **Pick the pole loudly** — full replacement; a CTO buying "assign-a-ticket-get-a-PR" is a different (bigger) sale than autocomplete.
- **Buy the opposite pole when you can** — owning both augment and replace beats purity.
- **Capital efficiency is a positioning asset** — <$20M net burn is a story investors and customers reward.
