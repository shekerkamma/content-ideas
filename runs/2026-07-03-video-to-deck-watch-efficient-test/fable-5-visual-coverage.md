## Editability Decision

Final deck mode: PPT-native editable diagrams.

Rationale: the video is mostly demos, terminal screens, article pages, and spoken explanation. Recreating the concepts as native PowerPoint flows is more useful than embedding raw frames, because every box, table, lane, and label remains editable in PowerPoint.

## Meaningful Screen-Change Handling

- Meaningful screens are grouped by workflow rather than copied one-by-one.
- Presenter-only frames are excluded.
- Duplicate terminal/chat states are grouped into the same recreated workflow.
- No screenshot is used as a primary slide visual.
