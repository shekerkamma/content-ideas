## Beacon.li

Sources:
- https://www.beacon.li/
- https://www.beacon.li/platform-overview
- https://www.beacon.li/blog/how-to-choose-implementation-orchestration-platform

Key extracted facts:
- Beacon positions itself as an AI implementation platform/orchestration platform for enterprise implementations from configuration through hypercare.
- Public claims include 60% faster go-lives, 50% lower costs, 90% fewer errors, and 3x delivery capacity.
- Product motions include auto-configuration, contextual UAT automation, hypercare support, UI-level product learning, and a 7-day POC.
- Platform claims include no backend access, UI metadata learning, SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, ISO 27001, bring-your-own-model support, and inheriting existing RBAC/permissions.
- Beacon's own buyer guide frames implementation orchestration as a layer that coordinates and executes requirements, configuration, migration, testing, cutover, and hypercare.

Confidence:
- High for positioning and product claims because they are from Beacon-owned pages.
- Medium for outcome metrics because they are vendor-published and not independently audited in the public source trail.
