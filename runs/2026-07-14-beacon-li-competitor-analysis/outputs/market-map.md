# Beacon.li Market Map

Date: 2026-07-14

## Category 1: AI Implementation Orchestration

Definition:
Platforms that execute or orchestrate enterprise software implementation work across requirements, configuration, validation, testing, migration, cutover, and hypercare.

Representative companies:
- Beacon.li
- Adjacent: Unframe AI, UiPath, Automation Anywhere, IBM watsonx Orchestrate

Buyer problem:
Implementation bottlenecks delay revenue recognition, increase services cost, create go-live risk, and trap institutional delivery knowledge in expert consultants.

Maturity:
Early category. The vocabulary is emerging, and buyer education is still required.

Likely winners:
- Vendors that prove real execution, not just summaries or dashboards.
- Vendors that integrate with existing implementation systems and capture reusable implementation knowledge.

Likely losers:
- Thin AI wrappers around project status, static playbooks, or generic chat over implementation documents.

## Category 2: Digital Adoption Platforms

Definition:
Platforms that guide, train, support, and measure users inside enterprise applications.

Representative companies:
- WalkMe
- Pendo
- Whatfix
- Appcues

Buyer problem:
Users struggle to adopt software; organizations need in-app guidance, support deflection, analytics, training, and measurable AI/software ROI.

Maturity:
Mature. WalkMe/SAP, Pendo, and Whatfix have stronger enterprise credibility than Beacon.

Likely winners:
- Incumbents that extend from guidance and analytics into workflow execution.
- Platforms that prove AI adoption and ROI across enterprise application portfolios.

Likely losers:
- DAP products that remain limited to static walkthroughs and cannot show operational impact.

## Category 3: Customer Onboarding / Implementation Operations

Definition:
Platforms that manage customer onboarding projects, implementation visibility, collaboration, services operations, resource planning, and handoffs.

Representative companies:
- Rocketlane
- GuideCX
- Arrows
- Dock

Buyer problem:
Implementation projects are hard to coordinate across internal delivery teams and customer stakeholders.

Maturity:
Moderately mature. Rocketlane is strongest in services operations; Arrows/Dock are simpler collaboration/workspace tools.

Likely winners:
- Tools that become the operating system for services delivery and integrate AI agents into repeatable work.

Likely losers:
- Tools that provide visibility only but cannot reduce manual delivery effort.

## Category 4: Enterprise AI Delivery / Automation

Definition:
Platforms or managed providers that build, orchestrate, govern, and automate AI workflows across enterprise systems.

Representative companies:
- Unframe AI
- UiPath
- Automation Anywhere
- IBM watsonx Orchestrate

Buyer problem:
Enterprises want production AI workflows and automation without building all infrastructure internally.

Maturity:
Mixed. RPA incumbents are mature; managed AI delivery platforms are scaling quickly.

Likely winners:
- Broad platforms that combine governance, integrations, agent orchestration, and measurable ROI.

Likely losers:
- Generic AI consultancies without reusable product infrastructure or measurable deployment speed.
