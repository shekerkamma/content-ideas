Great algorithms. Wrong cost structure.
India's three most-cited autonomy competitors are hardware-agnostic — they inherit merchant-silicon economics regardless of algorithm quality.

INHERITED COST
Whatever the SoC vendor charges — they don't control it.

SILICON

INHERITED COST
Same — algorithmic edge doesn't compress BOM.
RoshAI
Fleet-safety AI
SILICON
Merchant — commodity dashcam class
STRATEGY
DMS + telematics platform on commodity dashcam hardware. Software-as-service revenue, not chip revenue.
INHERITED COST
Dashcam hardware margin belongs to somebody else.
The structural point
Software-first competitors can win on algorithm quality. They cannot win on BOM. Their unit economics converge to whatever the merchant SoC vendor charges — and that is the Squeeze Zone (slide 3) by definition.
