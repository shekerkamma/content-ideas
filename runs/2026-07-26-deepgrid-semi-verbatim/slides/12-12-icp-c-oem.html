The one entry rule
Enter only via a signed Tier-1 partnership.
Why Tier-1 changes the math. Uno Minda, Spark Minda or Bosch India already sit on OEM homologation panels. They amortise cert cost across their existing portfolio; we ride their AEC-Q100 audit trail.
