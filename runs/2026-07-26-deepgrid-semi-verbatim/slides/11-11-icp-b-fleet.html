Why it stays on the board (not killed)
India's N2/N3 fleet mandate wave (AIS-184/186/187/188) starts landing in FY26–27 and hits enforcement in FY28. That is a genuine ~5M-vehicle addressable base, and it is the segment that eventually justifies the ₹55 Cr raise as a chip-volume business.
