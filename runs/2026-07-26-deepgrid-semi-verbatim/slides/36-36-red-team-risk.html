DEEPGRID SEMI · PRE-SERIES A
Two real gaps, two structural risks. Named, not hidden.
Red-team Stage 5 output — these are the things a bad investor question would find first.
GAP #1 · Competitive benchmark blind spot
Indian defense primes were never assessed as competitors.
Competitive slides 21–25 benchmark against Aurora / Kodiak / Netrasemi / Minus Zero. But for the Defense track the relevant competitor is a BEL / BDL / Tata Advanced Systems / Mahindra Defence with existing security clearance, existing UGV programs, and 30+ years of MoD relationships.

A startup vs an established cleared incumbent is a fundamentally different competitive problem than vs Netrasemi. Fix in Phase 1, Weeks 1–4 (slide 20).
GAP #2 · Evidence pedigree
Nature of the ₹1 Cr revenue is unconfirmed.
Everything in the deck — the ICP scorecard, the Defense-primary decision, the resourcing plan — depends on the ₹1 Cr being a commercial sale.

If it turns out to be a grant, subsidy, or R&D contract, Defense re-scores as evidence-equal to Fleet and the "Defense-first sequence" argument reopens. Third-party audit within one week is Condition 1 (slide 37).
STRUCTURAL RISK · Circular evidence
Defense scores 5/5 on evidence because of the ₹1 Cr.
If Gap #2 resolves against us, the evidence score drops from 5/5 to 1/5, and Defense's weighted score collapses from 3.96 to ~3.20. That still beats Fleet on the other dimensions — but the primary-ICP decision is no longer default-obvious, and the day-90 checkpoint (Condition 5) becomes a real re-plan gate.
STRUCTURAL RISK · Working capital & runway
₹2.48 Cr raised to date vs ₹55 Cr ask.
Government + Defense payment cycles run 90–180+ days. On the current cash position, a single delayed invoice can compress the runway inside a quarter. The ₹10 Cr CGTMSE debt exists specifically to buffer this — but only if the raise closes on schedule. Condition 3 mandates modelling this before external commitments.
