23 · INDIAN SEMICONDUCTOR WAVE
DEEPGRID SEMI · PRE-SERIES A
India's chip wave is real. And a year behind us.
Three well-funded domestic fabless startups — none has commercial autonomous-CV silicon revenue yet.
FABLESS · VISION SOC
Netrasemi
A2000 vision SoC on TSMC 12 nm.
FUNDING
₹125 Cr
LAUNCH
Mid-2027
POSITIONING
General-purpose vision SoC. Broadest but least ADAS-specific of the three. 12 nm node is more expensive per die than our 28 nm — different bet on where value lives.
FABLESS · RISC-V MCU
Mindgrove
RISC-V MCU on TSMC 28 nm.
FUNDING
$10.3M
PARTNER
Bosch MoU
POSITIONING
MCU-class chip, not an inference-class one. Bosch MoU is genuinely interesting but is a supply agreement for MCU sockets, not a competing ADAS SoC. Adjacent, not overlapping.
FABLESS · GaN POWER / RF
Agnit Semiconductors
GaN devices for defense radar.
FUNDING
$7.47M
STATUS
Defense pilots
POSITIONING
Complements rather than competes — GaN power devices sit alongside our R100 digital radar DSP. Potential collaboration for defense radar programs.
Read of the domestic wave
The wave is real and well-funded. But DeepGrid is the only Indian fabless startup with commercial autonomous-CV silicon revenue today. Netrasemi ships mid-2027; Mindgrove is MCU-class; Agnit is a partner, not a competitor.
