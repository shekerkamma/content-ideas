DEFERRED · REVISIT PHASE 3

Automobile OEM / Tier-1
What "deferred" means operationally
Not killed — timed. This is where the volume lives once the chip has a Tier-1 sponsor and an OEM SOP.

Direct OEM effort is paused until at least one of: (a) a signed Tier-1 co-development agreement is in hand, or (b) the Fleet retrofit pilot proves the compute node in field conditions on the same vehicle classes OEMs care about.

The one exception: sovereign OEMs (Tata Advanced Systems, Mahindra Defence) — addressed through the Defense track, not through automotive BD.
Trigger to reactivate
→ Any Tier-1 signs a binding co-development agreement with NRE terms.
→ OR — a sovereign OEM specifies DGrid Alpha for a defence-adjacent platform.
→ OR — the Fleet retrofit generates a public field-safety dataset the OEM can cite in its own homologation package.
ANTI-ICP · LOWEST SCORE
43.0%
Commercial Motor Insurers
Why we are labelling this "never a direct customer"
Lowest score across every segment. The whole insurer thesis in the founder's original draft rested on an unsupported "premium-reduction %" claim. There is no measured accident-reduction data behind it, no actuarial partnership, no signed data-share agreement.

The model has zero insurer-revenue rows. There is no line item to defend.

Insurers may eventually be an evidence partner — a channel we help underwrite fleet-side ROI — but under no circumstances are they a direct customer of the chip in this plan.
Trigger to revisit as evidence partner (not customer)
→ Fleet pilot generates a measured accident-reduction dataset with an insurer already on-record willing to underwrite it.
→ Only then does an insurer channel deserve pipeline effort — and even then it is a data-sharing motion, not a sales motion.
