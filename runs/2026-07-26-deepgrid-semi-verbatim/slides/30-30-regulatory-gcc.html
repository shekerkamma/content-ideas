Saudi TGA / WASL mandate
Every commercial vehicle operating in KSA must be registered on WASL.
Transport General Authority (TGA) requires certified GPS + telematics tracker approved for WASL data ingest.

Fleet operators today install a secondary tracker in addition to any safety hardware — ~$60–120 per vehicle in BOM plus install.

Our S100 chiplet is designed as a WASL-compatible endpoint, so ADAS + telematics ship in one BOM slot.
Adjacent GCC markets
UAE (RTA fleet-safety framework), Oman (ROP transport mandate), and Kuwait (MOI fleet registration) trail the Saudi model. S100 firmware profiles are portable across all four with configuration changes.
Why S100 changes the buyer conversation
Two devices → one device.

Sales-motion consequence
In GCC we lead with the "kill the tracker" narrative, not the "cheap ADAS" narrative. The buyer's existing pain is the redundant tracker they already resent paying for.
