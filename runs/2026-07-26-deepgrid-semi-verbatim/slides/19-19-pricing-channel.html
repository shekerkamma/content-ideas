PRICING LOGIC
Fleet retrofit — AD2 kit
₹2–2.5 Lakh is a roadmap figure, not a validated price. Bottom-up cost build ($200 BOM) plus mandate-driven willingness-to-pay assumption, neither externally tested.
What we collect in the first 20 interviews
→ Ceiling: max ₹ the fleet has ever paid per truck for a safety add-on.
→ Payback horizon they will underwrite (months, not years).
→ Whether pricing is per-truck, per-fleet, or subscription.
→ Fault attribution — who pays if a truck with the kit has an incident.
→ Attached-fleet vs owner-operated split — who has budget authority.
Defense — contract-negotiated
No public price. Sovereign buyers negotiate line-item against program budget. NRE reimbursement + per-unit + support & spares tail.

Discipline: we never publish a defense price. We publish a chip technology envelope. Program-specific pricing lives in the RFP response.
