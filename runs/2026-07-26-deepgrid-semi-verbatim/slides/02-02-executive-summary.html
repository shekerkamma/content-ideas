01 · What is validated
Architecture works on FPGA.
3D Systolic Tensor Cube (64×512 MAC, dual-issue @ 600 MHz) delivers a measured 39.3 TOPS INT8 on FPGA.

Batch-dispatch firmware demonstrates ~200× utilization gain vs naive per-frame scheduling on the same silicon.

Six-chiplet combo-die (A100 / R100 / T100 / D100 / S100 / H100) covers ADAS, radar, LiDAR, secure enclave, telematics and health in one BOM slot.
02 · Why FPGA is not the business
FPGA boards are uneconomic at volume.
FPGA validation board cost is ~₹35,000 / unit. Fine for defense pilots, fatal for retrofit fleets.

The custom 28 nm ASIC path collapses die cost to $3.876 at 1M units (under $4) and total board BOM to ~$30.

This is the entire investment thesis: fund the ASIC transition, not another FPGA generation.
