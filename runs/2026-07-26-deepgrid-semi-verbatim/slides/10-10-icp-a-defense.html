Why it is the primary ICP
Extremely cost-insensitive at the chip level. Sovereign buyers optimise for supply-chain sovereignty and audit posture, not BOM. Our ~$30 board is a rounding error against a UGV platform cost.

D100 secure enclave is a first-order feature. Lockstep RISC-V, on-die HSM, keys never leave the die. This maps directly onto Ministry of Defence data-sovereignty requirements — it is not a checkbox, it is the whole reason to buy.

Off-highway / port variants share the SKU. Container terminals in Mundra, Chennai and Jebel Ali need the same secure-boot AGV compute node. One BOM, two buyer types.
