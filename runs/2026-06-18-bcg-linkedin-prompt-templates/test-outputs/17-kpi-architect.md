# KPI Architecture — Apex HR

## Strategic KPI Hierarchy

### Outcome KPIs (Board / CEO — Quarterly)
| KPI | Target | Owner | Alert Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARR Growth Rate | 25% YoY by Q4 2027 | CEO | <18% for 2 quarters |
| Net Revenue Retention (NRR) | 108% by Q2 2027 | CS Lead | <103% |
| Gross Margin | 72%+ | CFO | <68% |
| Payroll ARR | $5M by Month 18 | CPO | <$2M by Month 12 |

### Driver KPIs (Leadership — Monthly)
| KPI | Target | Owner | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Win Rate (vs. Rippling / Deel) | Recover to 45% | VP Sales | Proximate cause of growth stall |
| Payroll Attach Rate | 35% of new 150–300 logos | CPO | Tests whether payroll closes deals |
| CAC Payback Period | <18 months | CFO | Efficiency of new growth |
| Time to First Payroll Run | <30 days | CS Lead | Implementation speed vs. Rippling |

### Leading Indicators (Weekly — Ops)
| KPI | Target | Owner | What It Predicts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Payroll-cited deal losses per week | Trending to zero | Head of Sales | Win rate recovery |
| Payroll beta customer satisfaction (CSAT) | >8.5/10 | PM | GA readiness |
| Compliance issue count (pre-GA) | Zero P0, <3 P1 | Compliance Lead | Launch safety |
| Engineering velocity vs plan | ≥90% | Engineering Lead | Timeline on track |

## Vanity Metric Kill List
- Total registered users (not a revenue metric at Apex's stage)
- NPS alone without segmentation by account tier (masks SMB churn signal)
- Gross new logos without win rate context (hides competitive displacement)

## Operating Cadence
- Weekly: Payroll build velocity + compliance status
- Monthly: Win rate, NRR, CAC payback, payroll attach
- Quarterly: ARR growth, gross margin, payroll ARR, board review
