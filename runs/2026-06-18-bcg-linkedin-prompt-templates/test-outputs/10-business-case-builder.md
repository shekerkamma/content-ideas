# Business Case — Apex HR Payroll Module (Partner Bridge)

## Investment
Partner integration (Check API): $800K Year 1 (engineering + BD + compliance review)
Internal build (native payroll): $2.4M over Years 2–3
Total 3-year investment: $3.2M

## Revenue / Cost Benefit
Year 1 (Partner live, Q4): Recover 40% of lost deals in 150–300 employee band → +$3.2M ARR
Year 2: Full mid-market payroll coverage → +$6.8M ARR incremental
Year 3: Native payroll + APAC pilot → +$11.2M ARR incremental
Cumulative 3-year benefit: $21.2M ARR

## Assumption Register
| Assumption | Evidence Strength | Impact if Wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Payroll disqualifies >50% of lost deals | Weak (not yet validated) | Investment ROI halved |
| Partner integration takes 6–9 months | Medium (Check is a known API) | Delayed revenue capture |
| Win rate recovers 40% with payroll | Medium (competitive signal) | Revenue model overstated |
| Existing customers upgrade to Payroll at 35% | Weak | NRR gain doesn't materialise |

## Sensitivity Analysis
| Scenario | Year 3 ARR Gain | NPV (10% discount) | Payback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | +$11.2M | $14.8M | 18 months |
| Conservative (30% win rate recovery) | +$7.1M | $8.2M | 26 months |
| Optimistic (60% win rate recovery) | +$15.6M | $21.4M | 12 months |
| Break-even | Win rate recovery >15% | — | 36 months |

## Recommendation
Proceed with partner bridge in Q3 2026. The investment breaks even at 15% win rate recovery — a low bar given the competitive signal. Validate with win/loss analysis before committing to native build in Year 2.
