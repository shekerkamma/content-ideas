# Transformation Roadmap — Apex HR

## Strategy Being Executed
Re-accelerate ARR growth from 12% → 25%+ by closing the payroll product gap, strengthening mid-market position, and building APAC optionality.

## Target State (24 months)
- ARR: $60M+ (from $42M today)
- Payroll: live via partner, native build in progress
- Win rate: recovered to 2023 levels in 150–300 employee band
- NRR: 108%+ (from 101%)
- APAC: pilot with 5–10 customers, validation complete

## Top Risks
1. Payroll compliance delay — mitigation: hire compliance counsel in Month 1
2. Rippling responds with aggressive discounting — mitigation: pre-lock renewals before GA
3. Engineering bandwidth — mitigation: hire 4 payroll-focused engineers in Phase 2
