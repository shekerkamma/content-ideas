# Situation Assessment — Apex HR

## Executive Read
Apex HR is a mid-market HR SaaS business with strong product-market fit in its core segment but decelerating growth that signals a transition risk. Revenue reached $42M ARR but growth decelerated sharply from 35% to 12% YoY — a 23-point drop not explained by market conditions alone. NRR of 101% suggests modest expansion but masks weak upsell motion. Competitive losses to Rippling and Deel are increasing, indicating the payroll gap is becoming a buying criterion rather than a feature preference. Leadership must decide whether to defend core, expand product, or enter APAC before the window closes.

## Fact Base
| Area | Evidence | Interpretation | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $42M ARR, 12% YoY growth | Severe deceleration; likely demand ceiling in core ICP | High |
| Retention | NRR 101% | Retention healthy; expansion revenue weak | High |
| Competitive | Win rate vs Rippling/Deel declining | Payroll gap becoming deal-breaker at 200+ employee mark | Medium |
| Market | Mid-market HR tech growing 18% YoY | Apex growing below market — share loss in progress | Medium |
| Product | No Payroll module | Rippling + Deel bundle payroll; Apex cannot compete in RFPs that require it | High |
| Org | 200 employees, Austin HQ | Capacity constrained for simultaneous APAC + Payroll build | High |

## Momentum Signals
| Signal | Direction | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| ARR growth rate | Declining (35% → 12%) | Structural ceiling or competitive displacement — needs diagnosis |
| Competitive win rate | Declining | Payroll gap expanding as ICP grows past 200 employees |
| NRR | Flat (101%) | Upsell motion not compensating for new logo slowdown |
| APAC pipeline | Emerging | New market but execution bandwidth is constrained |

## Strategic Issues
1. Growth deceleration outpaces market — payroll product gap is likely the proximate cause
2. Payroll module absence is a competitive liability at the 200+ employee ICP inflection point
3. APAC expansion and Payroll module compete for the same scarce engineering and GTM capacity

## Open Questions
- What % of lost deals cite payroll as the reason vs. pricing or reputation?
- What is CAC payback period and how has it trended over 8 quarters?
- Is NRR 101% holding across all segments or masking churn in a specific tier?
- What does APAC expansion require in headcount, compliance infrastructure, and capital?
