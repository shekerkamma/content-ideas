## Core Message

## Pyramid Logic
**Recommendation:** Approve Check partnership integration for payroll, $800K, Q3 2026 start.

Supporting arguments:
1. Payroll gap is the binding constraint — win/loss will confirm, but signal is clear now
2. Partner bridge (Check) is the fastest path — 9 months vs. 18–24 for native build
3. The investment breaks even at 15% win rate recovery — a conservative threshold

Evidence:
- 35% → 12% growth deceleration, below 18% market growth rate
- NRR 101% — customers staying but not expanding
- Competitive signal: Rippling bundles payroll; Deel expanding
