# Strategic Options — Apex HR

## Decision
How should Apex HR re-accelerate ARR growth from 12% to 25%+ within 24 months?

## Options
| Option | Description | Upside | Trade-Off | What Must Be True |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A — Build Native Payroll | Build payroll engine in-house over 18 months | Closes win-rate gap; high NRR upside; defensible moat | High engineering cost; long timeline; compliance risk | Payroll IS the disqualifying factor in >50% of lost deals |
| B — Embed via Partnership | White-label Gusto or Check payroll | Fast time-to-market (6–9 months); lower risk | Lower margin; dependency on partner; less differentiation | Partner economics are acceptable; customers accept white-label |
| C — Acquire Payroll Startup | M&A of Series A/B payroll-adjacent company | Faster than build; acquires talent + IP | Requires $40–80M capital raise; integration risk | Board approves raise; target available at right valuation |
| D — APAC First | Enter APAC with HR-only, defer payroll | New revenue stream; low competition | Doesn't address US win-rate; dilutes focus | APAC demand validated; unit economics comparable to US |
| E — Upmarket to 500–1,000 | Sell deeper HR to larger companies | Higher ACV; Workday gap | Requires enterprise features; different buyer | Existing product works for 500+ employee complexity |

## Evaluation
| Criteria | A — Build | B — Partner | C — Acquire | D — APAC | E — Upmarket |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Addresses core barrier | High | High | High | Low | Medium |
| Time to revenue impact | Low (18mo+) | High (6–9mo) | Medium (12mo) | Medium | Medium |
| Capital efficiency | Medium | High | Low | Medium | High |
| Defensibility | Very High | Medium | High | Medium | Medium |
| Execution risk | High | Low | High | Medium | Medium |

## Recommendation
**Option B (Partner) as bridge → Option A (Build) as destination.** Partner with Check or Gusto to launch payroll in 9 months, capture the win-rate gain, and fund the native build from the resulting ARR growth. APAC deferred until US payroll is live and generating NRR.

- Validate partnership economics with Check (week 1–2)
- Run win/loss analysis to confirm payroll = disqualifier in >50% of deals (week 1–4)
- Get engineering estimate for native build while partnership runs (months 1–3)
