# Value Realization Plan — Prism Analytics

## Value Ambition

Deliver $14M in net new ARR by end of FY2026, bringing total ARR from $48M to $62M (29% growth vs. 14% in FY2025). Simultaneously recover NRR from 104% to 110%+ and win rate from 31% to 42%, establishing Prism's credibility as an AI-first compliance intelligence platform ahead of a potential Series D or strategic sale process. Total investment to capture this value: $3.2M over 12 months, with a target payback period of under 18 months on incremental ARR at 74% gross margin.

## Risks To Capture

**Engineering execution slippage.** The single largest value driver — NRR improvement and new logo wins — is contingent on the LLM interface shipping on time and at compliance-grade accuracy. A 3-month delay pushes the primary value realization event into FY2027, compressing runway to approximately 15 months and eliminating the buffer needed for a strategic sale process if organic growth does not materialize.

**NRR recovery requires churn control before expansion.** At 104% NRR, Prism is losing more from the bottom than it grows from the top in the existing base. The LLM upsell module will not move NRR to 110% if gross churn simultaneously accelerates. Value capture requires both: arresting regional bank churn AND attaching the LLM module to stable accounts. These are sequential, not parallel, priorities.

**Win-rate improvement lags by one sales cycle.** The 8–14 month sales cycle means that wins generated by the LLM repositioning will not show in closed-won data until Q3–Q4 FY2026 at the earliest. In the interim, the value realization plan must use leading indicators — pipeline stage progression, design partner activation, and sales engineer effectiveness — rather than lagging revenue metrics. If the Board evaluates the strategy only on ARR in Q1 2026, it will incorrectly conclude the strategy is not working.

**ACV compression under competitive pressure.** Clearwater's aggressive growth posture ($180M ARR, 55% growth) may force Prism into discounting to win deals during the repositioning window. If ACV falls from $343K to $300K, the win-rate improvement required to hit $62M ARR increases from 42% to ~47% — which may be unreachable within the FY2026 timeframe. Value ledger must be re-evaluated if any quarter shows average ACV below $320K.
