# Initiative Prioritization — Prism Analytics

## Criteria
Initiatives are scored against four criteria weighted to reflect Prism's current constraints (18-month runway, 14% growth, board exit pressure):

- **Impact** — ARR contribution, win rate recovery, NRR uplift, or runway extension within 12 months (H/M/L)
- **Feasibility** — execution risk given current team, capital, and timeline (H/M/L)
- **Urgency** — time-sensitivity; opportunity decays or competitive window closes if delayed (H/M/L)
- **Fit** — alignment with the recommended "AI-first compliance intelligence" strategic path (H/M/L)

All initiatives are evaluated against a $2.1M/mo burn rate and an 18-month runway constraint. No initiative that requires >$3M and delivers impact beyond 18 months qualifies for immediate prioritization without a financing trigger.
