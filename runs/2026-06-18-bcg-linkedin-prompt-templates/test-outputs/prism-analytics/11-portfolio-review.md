# Portfolio Review — Prism Analytics

## Portfolio Scope
This review covers Prism Analytics' four customer vertical segments (Tier 1 Banks, Regional Banks, Insurance, Wealth Management) and three product capability areas (Compliance Reporting Engine, Portfolio Risk Dashboards, LLM Query Interface) as the investment allocation decision. Excluded: geographic expansion (not yet in scope), potential M&A targets (covered separately). The decision question is: where should Prism concentrate engineering, sales, and customer success resources over the next 12 months to maximize ARR recovery and exit positioning?

## Evaluation Matrix — Customer Segments
| Segment | Attractiveness | Performance | Fit | Right To Win | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 Banks (32% ARR, ~$15.4M) | Very High — regulatory complexity is highest; ACV ceiling $500K–$1.2M; Basel IV tailwinds through 2027 | High — low churn; highest ACV; longest relationships; 3 strategic references | Very High — compliance depth, audit trail, board reporting are core strengths | High — Bloomberg does not compete on workflow; Clearwater less entrenched here | **Invest** — prioritize AE coverage, executive relationships, and LLM Intelligence tier upsell |
| Regional Banks (41% ARR, ~$19.7M) | Medium — CECL + DFAST need is real but RFP complexity is lower; ACV ceiling $350K; margin pressure from community bank consolidation | Medium — highest churn source (6% gross); multiple sub-$200K accounts at risk; NRR declining | High — CECL provisioning is a core module; exam-readiness messaging resonates | Medium — SS&C Axiom incumbency; Clearwater entering; pricing pressure from smaller fintechs | **Fix** — introduce Compliance Starter tier to reduce churn; pause new logo investment below $150K ACV; defend the $200K–$350K cohort |
| Insurance (18% ARR, ~$8.6M) | High — IFRS 9 + ORSA adoption accelerating; ACV $400K–$700K; less competitive intensity than banking | High — low churn; growing cohort; IFRS 9 deadlines driving urgency through 2026 | High — ECL calculation and investment risk analytics are direct product strengths | High — limited specialist competitors; Moody's is feature-based not workflow-based | **Invest** — add 1 dedicated insurance AE and 1 solutions engineer; build IFRS 9 LLM use case as reference |
| Wealth Management (9% ARR, ~$4.3M) | Medium — portfolio risk attribution need exists; lower regulatory urgency than banking/insurance; ACV ceiling $400K | Medium — stable but slow-growing; limited upsell surface without real-time data | Medium — product designed for banks; portfolio attribution features less differentiated | Low — Orion, Addepar, and BlackRock Aladdin Lite are well-positioned; Prism is not an ICP fit | **Maintain / Harvest** — retain current revenue; no new investment; opportunistic upsell only |

## Allocation Moves
| Move | Rationale | Resource Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Shift 2 enterprise AEs from Wealth Management to Insurance | Insurance has 2× the ACV potential and higher right-to-win; Wealth Management is a harvest segment | Redeploy 2 AEs + 1 SE from WM to Insurance; no net headcount add |
| Redirect 25% of platform engineering to LLM delivery (8-week sprint) | LLM interface is the highest-value near-term initiative; platform maintenance can run at lower velocity for 8 weeks | ~22 engineers temporarily shifted; platform bug backlog managed by remaining 68; must be communicated as time-boxed |
| Freeze new logo pursuit below $150K ACV in Regional Bank segment | Sub-$150K accounts represent disproportionate churn and CS cost; acquisition cost exceeds LTV at this ACV level | Free up 1 SMB-focused AE for redeployment to Tier 1 pursuits; reduce CS load by ~18 accounts over 18 months as natural attrition occurs |
| Invest in 3 Tier 1 bank "lighthouse" accounts with white-glove LLM onboarding | Reference-able AI-compliance use cases are the fastest way to shift market perception and win enterprise RFPs in 2026 | Assign 2 senior CSMs exclusively to lighthouse accounts; budget $150K for co-developed case studies |
| Pause Portfolio Risk Dashboard feature development for 6 months | Low differentiation; does not drive new business; engineering cost not justified | Saves ~4 FTE-quarters of engineering capacity; redirect to LLM layer and Basel IV compliance updates |

## Decisions Required
1. **Confirm wealth management as a harvest segment** — requires CEO and board alignment; signals a narrowing of TAM story but sharpens the compliance identity for exit positioning.
2. **Approve the 25% engineering reallocation to LLM delivery** — requires CTO commitment to a time-boxed sprint model and public milestone accountability; cannot be done as a quiet priority shift.
3. **Set a regional bank ACV floor of $150K for new logo pursuit** — requires VP Sales to update quota structures and territory assignments; will create near-term quota achievement pressure that must be managed with a transition period.
