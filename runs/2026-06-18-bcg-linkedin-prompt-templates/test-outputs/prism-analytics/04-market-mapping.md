# Market Map — Financial Analytics & Risk Intelligence SaaS

## Market Definition
B2B Financial Analytics and Risk Intelligence SaaS: software platforms serving banks, insurance carriers, and wealth management firms for portfolio risk dashboards, regulatory compliance reporting (Basel III/IV, CECL, IFRS 9), and financial data analytics. Geography: North America primary, Western Europe secondary. Time horizon: 2025–2029. Excluded: raw financial data providers (Bloomberg data terminal licenses, Refinitiv), core banking systems (Temenos, FIS core), and generic BI tools not purpose-built for financial risk (Tableau, Power BI). Included: purpose-built risk analytics platforms, regulatory reporting automation, and AI-native financial intelligence layers.

## White Space
- **Insurance carriers (IFRS 17 compliance)**: Prism has proven IFRS 9 and Basel III/IV depth, but has not built IFRS 17 modules for insurance. This is an adjacent $220M segment growing at 18% where purpose-built competition is limited. A targeted insurance expansion would leverage existing regulatory IP with modest product extension.
- **Regional banks in the $5B–$20B asset range**: The segment between community banks (too small for Prism's current ACV) and money-center banks (served by Bloomberg/SS&C) is underpenetrated. Approximately 600 US regional banks in this tier lack purpose-built Basel IV / CECL analytics at the depth Prism offers. Win rates here should be structurally higher than Tier 1 deals.
- **Natural language regulatory query layer**: No competitor has deeply integrated conversational AI specifically for regulatory report interpretation (e.g., "Why did our Tier 1 capital ratio decline this quarter, and which Basel IV calculation is driving it?"). Prism's LLM interface — if positioned as regulatory-specific NLP, not generic chat — occupies a white space Clearwater has not filled.
- **European IFRS 9 expansion**: European banks face ongoing IFRS 9 pressure; Prism's existing IFRS 9 engine is a direct entry point into UK and DACH markets where purpose-built SaaS alternatives are weaker than the US.

## Where-To-Play Options
1. **Defend and deepen regional bank core** — invest in retention of the $5B–$100B regional bank segment (currently 41% of revenue), arrest NRR compression with a renewal and expansion program, and position the LLM interface as the primary upsell driver within existing accounts before expanding outward
2. **Adjacent expansion into insurance (IFRS 17)** — extend existing regulatory IP into the $220M insurance segment with an IFRS 17 module; leverage existing compliance brand and enterprise sales motion; target CFO and Chief Actuary buyers at mid-size US carriers ($5B–$50B AUM)
3. **Position for strategic acquisition** — use the LLM interface launch to build a reframe narrative around "AI-powered regulatory intelligence"; demonstrate 25%+ growth resumption in 2–3 quarters; target inbound interest from SS&C, Moody's, or FIS who need an AI-layer for their existing regulatory reporting install base
