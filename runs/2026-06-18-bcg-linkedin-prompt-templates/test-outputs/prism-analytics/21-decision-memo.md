# Decision Memo — Prism Analytics LLM Acceleration Investment

**TO:** Board of Directors, Prism Analytics
**FROM:** CEO + CFO
**DATE:** June 2026
**DECISION REQUIRED BY:** June 30, 2026

---

## Recommendation

Approve $3.2M to accelerate the LLM natural language query interface by 2 months (target GA: Q4 2026 vs. current Q1 2027 plan), hire 4 sales engineers to convert the $28M qualified pipeline, and formally defer real-time data replatforming until Prism reaches $65M ARR. Structure the investment as two tranches: $1.8M at Board approval, $1.4M upon hitting the Q3 2026 LLM engineering milestone. This is the fastest path to restoring Prism's growth trajectory, win rate, and NRR — and to creating an optionality-rich position for a premium strategic exit or Series D raise.

## Decision Required

The Board must approve Tranche 1 of $3.2M ($1.8M) by June 30, 2026. Approval authorizes the CTO to begin engineering reallocation immediately and the VP Sales to open the four sales engineer requisitions. Tranche 2 ($1.4M) is subject to the CTO confirming the Q3 2026 LLM milestone at the August Board review.

## Context

Prism Analytics grew to $48M ARR but has decelerated sharply: 42% growth in FY2023, 28% in FY2024, and 14% in FY2025. Win rate has fallen from 47% to 31% over the same period. The company lost a $1.2M TCV deal to Clearwater Analytics three months ago — the clearest signal yet that buyers are choosing AI-native competitors not for superior compliance analytics, but for a natural language interface that Prism does not yet have.

Clearwater Analytics is now at $180M ARR growing at 55%. It is actively calling Prism's accounts. Prism has 18 months of runway at $2.1M/mo burn and $28M in qualified pipeline. The LLM interface is already 6 months from launch on the current plan. The decision is not whether to launch it — it is whether to accelerate it by 2 months to change the competitive dynamic before the window closes.

## Options Considered

| Option | Upside | Trade-Off | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accelerate LLM + hire 4 SEs (recommended) | Closes AI perception gap in FY2026; converts $28M pipeline at recovering win rate; positions for premium exit or raise at $62M ARR | $3.2M burn increase; engineering reallocation creates short-term platform risk; 18-month runway has limited buffer for overruns | Selected — best risk-adjusted path to restoring growth narrative and strategic value |
| Proceed on current LLM timeline (Q1 2027) | No incremental investment required; preserves runway | Clearwater compounds for another 2 quarters; pipeline ages and deals are lost during the window; AI-first category positioning may be conceded permanently | Rejected — the status quo guarantees continued deceleration |
| Replatform to real-time streaming now | Addresses the deepest technical gap; creates long-term defensibility | 18+ months and $15M+ — longer than current runway and a parallel workstream that paralyzes the LLM delivery; net result is neither shipped in FY2026 | Rejected — sequencing risk is existential at current burn and runway |
| Raise Series D to fund replatforming | Provides capital to do everything at once | At 14% growth, a Series D is a flat or down round from the 2022 Series C valuation; highly dilutive; capital markets are unlikely to fund a "we fixed the growth problem we caused" story | Rejected — investment restores the growth story first; raise from strength in FY2027 if desired |
| Pursue strategic sale now | Exits at current valuation; eliminates runway risk | Current story — 14% growth, no AI — commands a discount multiple; Prism is one strategic cycle away from a materially higher valuation; selling now means leaving 40–60% of exit value on the table | Rejected as the primary path; preserved as a contingency if Q3 trajectory falls below $54M ARR |

## Evidence

- **Win rate:** 31% today vs. 47% two years ago. 16-point decline is directionally correlated with Clearwater's AI-native market entry and positioning.
- **Lost deal signal:** $1.2M TCV deal lost to Clearwater 3 months ago. Post-mortem: buying committee cited "Clearwater's AI-native interface for compliance queries" as the deciding factor, not pricing or Basel IV depth.
- **NRR trajectory:** 112% (2023) → 108% (2024) → 104% (2025). Continued erosion means gross churn is approaching the expansion rate; NRR below 100% reframes the strategic narrative from turnaround to distress.
- **Pipeline quality:** $28M in qualified opportunities. At 42% win rate and $343K ACV, this pipeline supports $11.7M in new bookings — the majority of the $14M net new ARR required to reach $62M.
- **LLM readiness:** 27 engineers already assigned to LLM layer; Q1 2027 GA is confirmed on current resource levels. Accelerating to Q4 2026 requires ring-fencing 18 engineers for 90 days with a 90-day non-critical platform change freeze — CTO confirms this is achievable.
- **Economics:** At 74% gross margin, $14M in net new ARR generates $10.4M in incremental gross profit. The $3.2M investment pays back in under 4 months of incremental gross profit. Break-even requires only a 6-point win-rate improvement.

## Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| LLM ships late or fails compliance UAT | Tranche 2 hold trigger at Q3 milestone; 6-week code-freeze buffer built into engineering plan; hard UAT gate (92% accuracy, 3 bank design partner sign-off) before commercial announcement |
| Clearwater pre-empts repositioning before GA | Soft-launch to 10 design partners by September 2026; release compliance accuracy benchmark; design partner reference customers available before full market announcement |
| Gross churn accelerates during the pre-GA window | Dedicated CSM coverage for 30 at-risk regional bank accounts; 12-month rate-lock offers in exchange for design partner participation |
| $3.2M is insufficient — second ask required | Tranche 2 is the natural checkpoint; any additional need is surfaced at August Board review, not as a surprise; incremental ask evaluated against Q3 ARR trajectory |
| Burn exceeds $2.3M/mo cap | CFO holds monthly approval; any overrun triggers immediate reallocation review; SE hiring is phased (2 in July, 2 in September) to manage burn timing |
