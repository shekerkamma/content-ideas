# Customer Segmentation — Prism Analytics

## Segmentation Objective
This segmentation supports three simultaneous decisions: (1) where to focus retention investment to arrest NRR compression from 104% toward a 108%+ target; (2) which segments to prioritize in new logo acquisition to improve the 31% win rate; and (3) which segment profiles to lead with in the M&A narrative to maximize perceived ARR quality. The segmentation must explain why gross churn is concentrated in certain accounts, where expansion revenue is actually coming from, and which accounts have the highest LTV relative to cost to serve.

## Strategic Implications

**Targeting**: Concentrate 70%+ of new logo sales investment on Regional Bank Growth Clients ($5B–$100B assets) where Prism's compliance depth is a genuine differentiator and the LLM interface can change the buying calculus. Deprioritize community bank prospecting immediately — the economics do not support it and churn will continue regardless.

**Retention**: Tier 1 banks must be treated as irreplaceable; losing one account at $500K+ ACV requires 3–4 new regional bank logos to replace. Proactive multi-year renewal outreach should begin for any Tier 1 account within 18 months of renewal. Offer LLM beta access as a retention lever before the commercial launch.

**Product roadmap**: The LLM interface rollout should be sequenced to Regional Bank accounts first — they are the most price-competitive segment where a differentiated demo changes deal outcomes. Tier 1 banks will be more skeptical of LLM features; they need examiner validation first.

**Pricing**: Small Regional and Community Banks below $150K ACV are structurally unprofitable once CSM cost, support overhead, and implementation cost are fully loaded. Consider a tiered "Prism Essentials" offering at $80–$100K ACV with limited feature access and self-serve support, or a resale partnership with FIS/nCino to serve this segment without direct cost.

**M&A narrative**: A potential acquirer (SS&C, Moody's, FIS) will pay most for the Tier 1 and large regional bank base — high ACV, long-term contracts, sticky compliance workflows. Curating the customer list to highlight these accounts (ACV, tenure, NRR by tier) will materially affect the transaction narrative.
