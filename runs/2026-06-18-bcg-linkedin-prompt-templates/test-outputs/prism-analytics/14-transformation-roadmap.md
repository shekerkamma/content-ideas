# Transformation Roadmap — Prism Analytics

## Target Outcome
By month 12 (June 2027), Prism Analytics will have: (1) launched the LLM Intelligence Interface to market with ≥6 referenceable design partners and a credible "AI-first compliance intelligence" positioning, (2) won 15–20 net-new logos in the Tier 1 bank and insurance segments, lifting ARR from $48M to ≥$55M, (3) stabilized NRR at ≥107% through LLM expansion upsell at renewal, (4) reduced burn to ≤$1.85M/mo extending runway to ≥18 months from month 12, and (5) maintained a live M&A parallel track with a prepared data room and ≥2 qualified buyer conversations completed. At the month 12 board review, Prism will make a binary decision: accelerate to Series D and replatform, or close a strategic transaction.

## First 90 Days
**Week 1–2 — Alignment and commitment:**
- CEO announces AI-first strategy and 4-month LLM launch commitment at all-hands; names squad leads and PM for LLM Intelligence Squad
- CTO publishes engineering reorg structure and 14-week sprint OKRs
- CFO initiates discretionary spend audit; identifies $300K/mo reduction opportunities
- VP Sales launches win/loss interview program (external consultant engaged)

**Week 2–4 — Mobilization:**
- 6 design partner accounts identified and NDA/beta agreements signed (VP CS and CPO own)
- Insurance pod AE redeployments complete; territory assignments updated in CRM
- Deal desk governance live in Salesforce; VP Sales briefs all AEs on new discount floors
- Financial advisor engaged; preliminary buyer landscape scope agreed

**Week 5–8 — Early delivery signals:**
- LLM Intelligence Squad mid-sprint review: design partner onboarding feedback incorporated
- Win/loss analysis findings delivered; board briefed on real-time vs. LLM gap breakdown
- First 3 Insurance prospects at active discovery stage
- Data room skeleton (financials, customer summary, product overview) assembled

**Month 3 decision gate:** If design partner NPS at mid-sprint is <35 or if win/loss analysis reveals real-time gap cited in >50% of losses (not ~25%), convene an emergency strategy review to reassess Option A vs. an accelerated M&A process.

## Governance
**Cadence:**
- Weekly: LLM Squad sprint review (CPO + CTO + squad lead; 30 minutes; milestone RAG status)
- Weekly: New logo sprint pipeline review (CEO + VP Sales; deal-level review of Tier 1 and Insurance pipeline)
- Bi-weekly: Cross-workstream PMO sync (CEO, CPO, VP Sales, VP CS, CFO; 60 minutes; interdependency and risk log)
- Monthly: Board update (CEO + CFO; ARR trajectory, burn, M&A track status, key risks)
- Monthly: Design partner advisory council (CPO + 3 design partner CROs/CFOs; product feedback and reference story development)

**Escalation:**
- Any initiative more than 2 weeks behind milestone: CPO or workstream owner escalates to CEO within 48 hours
- Burn exceeds $2.0M/mo for 2 consecutive months: CFO triggers emergency cost review with CEO and board within 5 business days
- Any inbound M&A inquiry from a strategic buyer: CEO notifies board chair within 24 hours; no engagement without board authorization

**Success metrics (tracked monthly):**
- ARR (target: $55M by month 12)
- New logos won in sprint window (target: 15 by month 10)
- Win rate (target: ≥42% by month 6)
- NRR (target: ≥107% by month 9)
- LLM Intelligence tier attach rate at renewal (target: ≥30% of eligible accounts by month 9)
- Monthly burn (target: ≤$1.85M/mo from month 2)
- Design partner NPS (gate: ≥40 at week 12 before GA launch)
