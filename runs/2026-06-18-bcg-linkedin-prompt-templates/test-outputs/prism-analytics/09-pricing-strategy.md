# Pricing Strategy — Prism Analytics

## Pricing Objective
Increase price realization and NRR from 104% toward 112% while using the LLM interface launch as a packaging lever to capture premium pricing in Tier 1 bank and insurance segments, without triggering churn acceleration in the regional bank base.

## Diagnosis
| Area | Evidence | Issue | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| NRR decay | 112% → 108% → 104% over 3 years | Expansion revenue is shrinking faster than gross churn; upsell motion is weakening | Each 100bps NRR decline costs ~$480K in implied ARR at current base |
| Discount leakage | Win rate down from 47% to 31%; $1.2M TCV lost to Clearwater | Sales team discounting defensively on price to compete on capability gaps; no floor governance | Estimated 10–15% average discount rate on new business vs. list |
| Gross churn concentration | 6% gross churn (mostly smaller regional banks) | Price-to-value mismatch for sub-$200K ACV customers; Basel III overhead too heavy for smaller institutions | ~$2.9M ARR at risk annually from regional bank tail |
| Flat packaging | Single-tier enterprise license; no AI/LLM module priced separately | LLM interface launch has no price anchor; at risk of being bundled in for free to close deals | Missed $8–15M premium pricing opportunity on AI tier |
| Competitive anchoring | Bloomberg Terminal at $25–50K/seat; Clearwater at premium AI-native pricing | Prism positioned below Clearwater on price-value perception despite comparable compliance depth | Win rate erodes further as Clearwater raises its perceived premium |

## Segment Price Logic
| Segment | Value Driver | WTP Signal | Pricing Move |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 Banks (32% of ARR, ~$15.4M) | BCBS 239 / Basel IV automated reporting; audit trail; board-level risk dashboards | $500K–$1.2M ACV ceiling; value is regulatory fine avoidance ($50M+ exposure) | Introduce "Compliance Intelligence Suite" premium tier at $550K–$900K ACV; include LLM query layer + dedicated CSM + SLA upgrade |
| Regional Banks (41% of ARR, ~$19.7M) | CECL provisioning + DFAST stress testing; examiner-ready reporting | $150K–$350K ACV; value is exam readiness and reduced internal analyst headcount | Hold current ACV; offer LLM add-on module at $30K–$60K/year; use as retention and expansion lever |
| Insurance (18% of ARR, ~$8.6M) | IFRS 9 ECL calculation + ORSA reporting; investment portfolio risk | $400K–$700K ACV; value is actuarial accuracy and regulatory submission speed | Price insurance segment separately with IFRS 9 + LLM bundle; target $500K ACV uplift at renewal |
| Wealth Management (9% of ARR, ~$4.3M) | Portfolio risk attribution; client-facing compliance reporting | $250K–$400K ACV; lower regulatory complexity than banking | Maintain; add LLM-powered client report generation as upsell at $40K–$70K/year |

## Recommended Moves
1. **Launch a three-tier packaging architecture at LLM interface go-live (Q3 2026):** Core (T+1 analytics, current pricing), Intelligence (Core + LLM query layer, +$45K–$80K/year), and Enterprise Intelligence (Intelligence + real-time data feeds post-replatform, custom pricing). Anchor the market on Intelligence as the default upgrade path.
2. **Install a discount governance floor immediately:** Set a 12% maximum discount without VP-of-Sales approval; 15% requires CEO sign-off. Implement in CRM with deal-desk review for any discount request on new logos above $300K ACV. Run quarterly price realization reviews versus list.
3. **Run a cohort-based renewal repricing for Tier 1 bank customers up for renewal in the next 6 months:** Offer a 3-year lock with 5% annual escalator in exchange for early commit; use LLM access as a sweetener. Target $40K–$100K ACV uplift per account across the 8–10 Tier 1 accounts in the renewal window.
4. **Introduce a "Compliance Starter" packaging for regional banks under $150K ACV:** Simplified CECL-only module at $80K–$120K/year with a clear upgrade path to Core. Reduces churn in the sub-$200K cohort and creates an inbound motion for mid-size banks currently priced out.
5. **Reframe competitive positioning away from Bloomberg on price:** Bloomberg is a seat-based market data tool; Prism is a compliance workflow platform. Train sales on total cost of compliance (TCC) framing: Prism at $343K ACV vs. 3–4 internal analysts + Bloomberg seats at $600K+ loaded cost.

## Risks and Tests
**Risk 1 — Tier 1 accounts resist premium tier at renewal.** Mitigation: Pilot with 3 Tier 1 accounts in Q3 2026 before full rollout. If fewer than 2 of 3 accept Intelligence tier, revisit price point before Q4 renewals.

**Risk 2 — Regional bank churn accelerates on Starter packaging transition.** Mitigation: Offer a 12-month price protection guarantee on existing regional bank contracts before repricing. Monitor churn in the 6 months post-launch; halt Starter repricing if gross churn exceeds 8%.

**Risk 3 — Discount governance creates sales friction and slows already-long cycles.** Mitigation: Build a fast-track deal desk for sub-$400K ACV deals (48-hour turnaround). Provide sales with a pre-approved "competitor response" discount playbook capped at 10% for Clearwater competitive situations.

**Pilot design:** Run the Intelligence tier pricing test with 5 new-logo deals in Q3 2026 before embedding in all contracts. Measure: attach rate, sales cycle impact, and win rate vs. Clearwater in those deals.
