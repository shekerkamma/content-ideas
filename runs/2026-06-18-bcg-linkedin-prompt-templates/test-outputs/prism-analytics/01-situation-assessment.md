# Situation Assessment — Prism Analytics

## Executive Read
Prism Analytics is a financially mature B2B FinTech SaaS with a defensible regulatory compliance product but a growth engine that is decelerating faster than the market. ARR reached $48M on the back of strong institutional expansion, but the growth rate has collapsed from 42% to 14% in three years — a 28-point drop that market conditions alone cannot explain. NRR at 104% signals the expansion motion is eroding and net retention is barely covering gross churn. The loss of a $1.2M TCV deal to Clearwater Analytics three months ago is not an outlier — it is a leading indicator of what happens when an AI-native competitor with 55% growth enters the same RFP as a batch-processing incumbent. With 18 months of runway, $2.1M monthly burn, and a board demanding either profitability or a strategic sale, Prism faces a forced strategic choice: accelerate to AI parity, harvest cash flow and exit, or find a defensible niche narrow enough to own. The LLM natural language interface (6 months from launch) is the most credible near-term wedge — but only if it is positioned and landed before Clearwater makes it table stakes.

## Strategic Issues
1. Growth deceleration is outpacing market — the T+1 batch architecture and absence of real-time data is increasingly a disqualifier in competitive deals, especially against AI-native alternatives
2. The Clearwater loss and 16-point win rate drop signal that Prism's current product position is no longer sufficient to win in contested Tier 1 bank and large regional bank RFPs
3. The board is forcing a binary decision (profitability OR strategic sale) that the current $2.1M burn and 18-month runway makes urgent — the LLM interface launch must serve dual purposes: near-term revenue defense AND M&A narrative construction
4. NRR at 104% and 6% gross churn are not crisis levels today, but the trajectory — if extrapolated — produces a net retention cliff within 18–24 months that would undermine any growth story

## Open Questions
- What % of the 31% win rate losses cite real-time/streaming capability as the primary reason versus AI analytics features, pricing, or vendor reputation?
- Is the 6% gross churn concentrated in accounts below $150K ACV, and if so, what is the NRR for the Tier 1 bank cohort specifically?
- What is the LLM interface's minimum viable positioning — query-based natural language on regulatory reports, or portfolio risk analytics too?
- Has the board defined quantitative thresholds for "path to profitability" (e.g., breakeven by Q4 FY2027) or is the burn tolerance conditional on LLM interface performance?
- Are there inbound M&A signals from likely acquirers (SS&C, Moody's, Bloomberg, FIS) and at what ARR multiple — this determines whether a strategic sale is a live option or aspirational?
