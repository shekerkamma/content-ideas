# Strategic Options — Prism Analytics

## Decision
How should Prism Analytics stabilize growth deceleration (42% → 14%) and reach profitability or a credible exit position within 18–24 months given $2.1M/mo burn and 18 months of runway?

## Options
| Option | Description | Upside | Trade-Off | What Must Be True |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A — Accelerate LLM Interface ("AI-First Compliance Intelligence") | Compress LLM launch from 6 months to 4 months; reposition as AI-first compliance copilot; run a focused land-and-expand sprint targeting 15–20 new logos before the replatforming decision | Re-ignites growth story; low incremental cost (~$2M); exploits 6-month window before Clearwater consolidates further; improves exit multiple as AI-native narrative | Win rate recovery not guaranteed; real-time gap persists; enterprise buyers may still object to T+1 latency | LLM interface materially accelerates win rate; ≥10 new logos in first 6 months validates thesis |
| B — Full Real-Time Replatform First | Pause new product launches; invest $15M+ over 18+ months to rebuild on streaming infrastructure | Closes fundamental capability gap vs. Clearwater; defensible long-term moat | Consumes remaining runway; delays revenue; 18-month window with no growth narrative; burns board confidence | Board approves bridge financing; engineers execute without churn; customers wait |
| C — Vertical Contraction + Margin Recovery | Exit regional bank segment; concentrate on Tier 1 banks and insurance only; cut burn to $1.2M/mo; operate to FCF breakeven by month 12 | Stabilizes runway to 30+ months; defensible niche; higher ACV | Sacrifices $18M+ of addressable ARR; shrinks growth story; complicates future exit valuation | Regional bank churn can be managed without customer service collapse; ACV uplift offsets volume loss |
| D — Strategic Sale / Acqui-Hire | Proactively run an M&A process targeting Bloomberg, MSCI, Moody's Analytics, or a PE roll-up; position as compliance IP + customer base + LLM layer in progress | Clean exit; team protected; board objective met | Likely at 3–4× ARR given growth trajectory; maximizes exit value NOW vs. after further deceleration | A credible buyer exists at ≥3.5× ARR ($168M+); board aligned on exit price floor |
| E — Fintech Platform Partnership | OEM or embed Prism's compliance engine into a larger fintech (e.g., FIS, SS&C) as a white-label compliance module | Recurring revenue with low CAC; preserves independence | Execution distraction; partner controls distribution; diluted differentiation | A tier-1 partner is interested; integration complexity is manageable within 12 months |

## Recommendation
**Option A as primary play, with Option D kept live as a parallel track.** Accelerate the LLM interface to a Q3 2026 launch; reposition as "AI-first compliance intelligence" to arrest win-rate decline and generate a renewed growth narrative. Use the 6-month LLM window to win 15–20 new logos, lifting ARR toward $55M. At the $55M ARR mark (or if Q3 win rates do not recover to ≥40%), run a structured M&A process in parallel. Option C (contraction) is a fallback if burn cannot be reduced without strategic action. Option B (full replatform) is deferred until ARR reaches $65M and a new financing round is closed.
