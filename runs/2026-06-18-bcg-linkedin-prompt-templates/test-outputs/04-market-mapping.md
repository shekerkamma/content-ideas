# Market Map — HR Tech Mid-Market

## Market Definition
Mid-market HR SaaS: companies 100–1,000 employees, US + English-speaking markets, core HR + payroll + compliance, 2026–2029 horizon.

## Size Estimate
| Method | Logic | Estimate | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top-down | ~400K US companies 100–1,000 employees × $8K avg ARR | ~$3.2B US TAM | Medium |
| Bottom-up | 200K reachable accounts × 60% HR SaaS penetration × $12K ACV | ~$1.4B SOM | Medium |
| Analyst benchmark | HR Tech growing 15% CAGR; mid-market ~18% | $3–4B US | Medium |

## White Space
- 150–500 employee segment: no strong HR-first alternative with native payroll at this size
- 500–1,000 employees: gap between overpriced Workday and underbuilt mid-market players
- APAC: low competition but high compliance overhead

## Where-To-Play Options
1. Double down on 150–500 US segment with Payroll module
2. Move upmarket to 500–1,000 with enterprise HR + payroll bundle
3. Enter APAC with HR-only, deferred payroll
