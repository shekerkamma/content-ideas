# Competitive Intel — Apex HR

## Competitor Map
| Competitor | Position | Incentives | Capabilities | Constraints |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rippling | Platform leader, HR+Payroll+IT | Win mid-market before Workday | Deep engineering, $1.2B raised, native payroll | Complex product; long sales cycle; not APAC-ready |
| Deel | Global payroll + compliance, contractor-heavy | Expand EOR into full HR for FTEs | Global payroll infrastructure, compliance brand | HR module thin; weak in US domestic mid-market |
| BambooHR | SMB/lower mid-market, acquired by Rippling 2024 | Feed BambooHR customers upmarket to Rippling | Strong brand, ease-of-use, HCM depth | No payroll; losing customers who grow past 150 employees |

## Response Plan
| Scenario | Early Signal | Recommended Response |
|---|---|---|
| Rippling discounts | Win rate drops despite Payroll launch | Lock 12-month contracts pre-launch; emphasize HR depth |
| Deel launches US HR | Deel raises Series E, hires US HR PMs | Accelerate NRR play; lock existing base |

## Strategic Implication
Apex has 12–18 months before Rippling strengthens its mid-market moat further. Payroll launch timing is the critical variable.
