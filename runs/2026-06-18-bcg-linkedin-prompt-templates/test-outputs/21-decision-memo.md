# Decision Memo — Apex HR Payroll Partnership

**TO:** Board of Directors, Apex HR
**FROM:** CEO + CPO
**DATE:** June 2026
**DECISION REQUIRED BY:** July 15, 2026

---

## Recommendation
Approve $800K investment to integrate Check payroll API as a partner-delivered payroll capability, targeting GA in Q1 2027. Defer APAC entry until Q3 2027.

## Context
Apex HR reached $42M ARR but growth decelerated from 35% to 12% YoY. Win rate against Rippling and Deel is declining in our highest-value segment (150–300 employees). Win/loss analysis (to be completed Week 1 of July) is expected to confirm that absence of a payroll module is the primary disqualifier in >50% of competitive losses. Mid-market HR tech is growing at 18% YoY; Apex is growing below market.

## Options Considered
| Option | Why Rejected |
|---|---|
| Build native payroll from scratch | 18–24 month timeline; delays win rate recovery by 1+ year |
| Enter APAC instead | Doesn't address US win rate; requires payroll infrastructure anyway |
| Raise and acquire payroll startup | $40–80M capital requirement; integration risk; premature at $42M ARR |
| Do nothing | Growth continues to decelerate; risk of being permanently locked out of 150–500 segment |

## Evidence
- ARR growth: 35% (2023) → 12% (2026) — 23-point deceleration
- NRR: 101% — below 108% benchmark for healthy SaaS at this stage
- Market growth: 18% YoY — Apex growing below market, indicating share loss
- Competitive signal: Rippling + Deel both bundle payroll; Apex cannot compete in RFPs that require it

## Risks and Mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Payroll compliance error | Hire compliance counsel Month 1; 90-day parallel run before GA |
| Check partnership collapses | 2-year rate lock; maintain backup vendor (Gusto/Zeal) evaluation |
| Rippling pre-emptive discount | Lock renewals before GA announcement |
| Win/loss shows payroll not disqualifier | Gate $800K commitment on win/loss result; decision point July 15 |

## Next Steps

2. July 1–15: Check partnership term sheet (CTO + CFO)
3. July 15: Board decision call
4. Aug 1: Engineering kickoff
5. Q1 2027: Payroll GA
