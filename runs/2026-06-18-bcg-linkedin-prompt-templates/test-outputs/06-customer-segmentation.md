# Customer Segmentation — Apex HR

## Segmentation Objective
Support ICP focus decision: which segment gets the full payroll+HR investment vs. maintained vs. deprioritized.

## Segment Definitions
| Segment | Defining Traits | Needs | Economics | Behavior |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Growth-Stage Scaler (150–300 employees) | VC-backed, headcount growing 20%+ YoY | Payroll + HR + compliance bundle; speed | ACV $14–22K; NRR 108% | Champion = HR Director; renewal tied to headcount growth |
| Stable Mid-Market (300–500 employees) | Established, slower growth, complex HR | Deep HCM, integrations, reporting | ACV $22–35K; NRR 103% | Champion = CHRO; long cycles; high switch cost |
| Transitional SMB (50–150 employees) | Growing out of spreadsheets, first HR hire | Core HR, onboarding, PTO | ACV $6–10K; NRR 98% | Self-serve; price-sensitive; churns at 150+ |
| APAC Mid-Market | 100–500 employees, ANZ/Singapore | Compliance-first HR; multi-currency payroll | ACV unknown | Compliance-led buying; local partner required |

## Segment Prioritization
| Segment | Size | Growth | Profitability | Fit | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Growth-Stage Scaler | $450M | High | Very High | High | #1 — Invest |
| Stable Mid-Market | $650M | Medium | High | Medium | #2 — Build |
| Transitional SMB | $300M | Low | Medium | Low | #3 — Harvest |
| APAC Mid-Market | $450M | Very High | Unknown | Low today | #4 — Explore |

## Strategic Implications
Lead GTM with Growth-Stage Scaler (150–300). Build payroll for them first. Lock Stable Mid-Market with HR depth. Stop acquiring Transitional SMB below cost — that segment churns at scale inflection.
