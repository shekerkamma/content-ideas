# Growth Barrier Diagnosis — Apex HR

## Growth Gap
Target: 25%+ ARR growth to remain competitive. Current: 12% YoY ($42M ARR). Gap: ~$5.5M in revenue shortfall vs. peer benchmark trajectory.

## Driver Tree
ARR Growth
├── New Logo Revenue
│   ├── Pipeline Volume (MQL → SQL)
│   ├── Win Rate (SQL → Close)
│   └── ACV (deal size)
└── Expansion Revenue
    ├── Upsell (module add-ons)
    └── Seat Growth (usage expansion)

## Barrier Assessment
| Driver | Evidence | Impact | Confidence | Root Cause |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Win Rate | Declining vs Rippling/Deel | High | Medium | Payroll gap disqualifies Apex in deals >200 employees |
| Pipeline Volume | Likely constrained | Medium | Low | ICP ceiling; saturation of 100–200 employee band |
| ACV | No evidence of growth | Medium | Low | No Payroll module = no upsell path to larger deals |
| Seat Expansion | NRR 101% — minimal expansion | Low | High | Customers not growing headcount fast enough |
| Upsell | Weak (implied by NRR) | High | Medium | Module set too thin; no high-value add-ons |

## Binding Constraint
Product scope mismatch at the ICP growth curve. Apex wins at 50–150 employees. As prospects scale past 200 employees, Rippling and Deel bundle payroll + compliance — making Apex a partial solution. This triggers displacement in renewals AND loss of new logo deals above 200 employees. The binding constraint is product, not pipeline or GTM.

## Recommended Actions
1. Build or buy a payroll module within 12 months — this IS the binding constraint
2. Segment pipeline by employee count to quantify exact revenue at risk above 200 employees
3. Launch compliance add-on or analytics tier to create expansion revenue while payroll is in development
