## Benefit Dependency Map
- Win rate recovery depends on: payroll GA on time + sales enablement complete
- Payroll upsell depends on: CS proactive outreach program + pricing clarity
- Analytics revenue depends on: pricing change approved + marketing campaign
- Discount reduction depends on: payroll gives sales a differentiator to hold on

## Review Governance
| Review | Cadence | Forum | Trigger for Re-Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Payroll ARR vs. target | Monthly | CPO + VP Sales | >20% below plan for 2 months |
| NRR segment tracking | Quarterly | CEO + CS Lead | NRR <103% for one quarter |
| Full benefits review | Quarterly | Executive team | Cumulative benefits <70% of plan |

## Contingency Triggers
- If payroll attach rate <15% after 90 days GA: pricing or packaging problem — run rapid customer research
- If win rate doesn't recover within 6 months of launch: payroll not the disqualifier — revisit strategy
- If analytics revenue <$300K in Year 1: bundling habit too entrenched — run more aggressive migration
