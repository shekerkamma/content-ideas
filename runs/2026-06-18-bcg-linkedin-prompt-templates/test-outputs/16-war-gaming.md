# Strategy War Game — Apex HR

## Strategy Under Test
Build payroll via Check partnership, launch in 9 months, and target the 150–300 employee growth-stage segment to recover win rate and re-accelerate ARR to 25%+.

## Scenarios
| Scenario | Trigger | Impact | Likelihood | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rippling launches aggressive 6-month price match | Apex announces payroll GA | Win rate recovery delayed; 30% of pipeline price-shopped | High | High |
| Check raises API pricing or exits | Series funding shift; acqui-hire by Rippling | Apex loses partner; 9-month build window evaporates | Medium | Critical |
| Deel launches full US HR platform | Deel Series E closes; hires US HR PM team | Apex faces two-front war: Rippling on payroll, Deel on HR | Medium | High |
| Payroll compliance incident at scale | Edge case in state tax calculation goes undetected for a quarter | Customer churn event; regulatory scrutiny; trust loss | Low | Critical |
| Win/loss validation shows payroll is NOT disqualifier | Internal analysis; customer interviews | Entire product roadmap invalidated; 6 months of strategy wasted | Medium | Critical |

## Vulnerabilities
| Weak Point | Why It Matters | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Only one payroll delivery pathway (Check) | Single point of failure | Dual-track: Check + evaluate Gusto/Zeal as backup |
| No compliance function today | Payroll is regulated; errors are existential | Hire compliance counsel in Month 1, not Month 6 |
| Win/loss assumption unvalidated | Entire strategy built on unverified assumption | Run analysis Week 1; gate strategy on result |
| Rippling will react faster than modelled | Rippling has more resources and faster sales motion | Pre-lock renewals before GA; compete on HR depth, not payroll price |

## Response Playbook
| Signal | Response | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Rippling discounts >20% in target segment | Compete on implementation speed + HR depth; don't match price | VP Sales |
| Check pricing changes | Activate Gusto/Zeal negotiations immediately | CTO |
| Deel enters US HR | Accelerate NRR lock with existing customers; run Deel-displacement campaign | Head of Revenue |
| Compliance incident | Activate crisis protocol; CEO communicates within 24 hours | CEO + Legal |
