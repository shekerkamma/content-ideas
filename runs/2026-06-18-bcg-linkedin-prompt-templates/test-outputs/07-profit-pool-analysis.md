## Value Capture
| Player / Activity | Value Captured | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Rippling | HR + Payroll bundle margin | Switching cost of payroll integration locks customers |
| Apex HR | Core HR SaaS margin | Strong product but no payroll lock-in |
| Gusto / Check (payroll infra) | Payroll processing margin | Infrastructure players capture slice of payroll revenue |
| Apex (Analytics) | High margin add-on | Low incremental cost; currently under-monetised |

## Strategic Implications
1. Build payroll to capture the highest-margin pool Apex currently surrenders to Rippling
2. Monetise Analytics as a standalone upsell — highest gross margin in portfolio, currently bundled
3. Exit or stop investing in Transitional SMB — below-average margin and high churn drag pool profitability
