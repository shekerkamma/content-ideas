# Stakeholder Alignment — Apex HR Payroll Strategy

## Strategy Requiring Alignment
Build payroll via Check partnership (9 months, $800K), defer APAC, re-focus GTM on 150–300 employee segment. Requires Board approval for spend + CTO/CPO commitment.

## Stakeholder Map
| Stakeholder | Role | Disposition | Decision Criteria | Primary Concern |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CEO | Sponsor + Decision Maker | Supportive | Growth trajectory credibility | Will this actually recover win rate? |
| Board / Lead Investor | Approves capital allocation | Neutral | ROI, timeline, competitive logic | Is $800K the right bet vs. other uses? |
| CTO | Builds the payroll engine | Cautiously supportive | Technical feasibility of Check integration | Compliance liability; team bandwidth |
| CPO | Owns product roadmap | Resistant (has competing priorities) | Product strategy clarity | Other roadmap items deprioritized |
| VP Sales | Delivers the win rate recovery | Strongly supportive | Faster the better | Losing deals right now; needs this in 6 months |
| CFO | Controls capital | Neutral | Payback period <24 months; break-even risk low | What if win/loss shows payroll isn't the issue? |
| CS Lead | Delivers customer success post-launch | Neutral | Clear launch plan + escalation path | Compliance errors will fall to them |

## Pre-Wire Sequence
1. **Week 1**: Win/loss analysis brief to CEO — establishes evidence base before any ask
2. **Week 2**: CTO technical feasibility read-out — confirms Check integration is viable
3. **Week 3**: CPO 1:1 — address roadmap concern; agree what gets deferred vs. defended
4. **Week 4**: CFO business case review — sensitivity model, break-even conversation
5. **Week 5**: Board pre-read (CEO + CFO present) — no surprises in the formal meeting
6. **Week 6**: Formal board approval

## Objections and Responses
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "What if payroll isn't the real issue?" | Win/loss analysis will confirm before we commit. We gate the decision on that data. |
| "$800K is a lot for a partner integration" | It breaks even at 15% win rate recovery. At our current deal volume, that's 3–4 deals. |
| "Why not APAC instead?" | APAC requires the same payroll infrastructure. We build it once, for US first. |
| "Won't Rippling just match us?" | They already have payroll. We're catching up, not leading. Speed is the game. |
