# Impeccable Direction

## Chosen world

**Design systems under inspection** — an editorial, evidence-led world with large typography, sharp rules, asymmetric image crops, marginal annotations, and a restrained navy/teal/coral palette inherited from the branded deck system.

## Why this world fits

The subject is not “pretty slides”; it is whether a design system can remove recognizable machine-made defaults. The deck therefore makes the inspection visible: each claim is paired with authentic demonstration evidence or a native decision graphic, and every slide has one dominant idea.

## Inspiration translation

- Impeccable: use a committed visual world, explicit anti-pattern detection, and a finish-review pass.
- Awwwards storytelling patterns: sequence the argument as claim → mechanism → evidence → decision.
- Awwwards editorial/minimal examples: high-contrast type, sparse palettes, and controlled asymmetry.
- No layouts, artwork, or branded assets are copied from external galleries.

## Anti-slop constraints

- No purple/blue decorative gradients.
- No generic dashboard grids, nested cards, pill clusters, or icon-tile feature lists.
- No invented UI, synthetic screenshots, or decorative evidence.
- No repeated three-card composition.
- No tiny text used to simulate analytical density.
- Every visual earns its place by proving, comparing, or directing a decision.

## Delivery authority

The repo-local branded PowerPoint workflow and its packaged template are authoritative. The documented host fallback template was inspected and found to contain invalid zero-filled bytes, so it was not used.
