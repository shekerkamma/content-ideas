# Deck brief

## Audience

Design, marketing, and consulting leaders deciding whether to add Impeccable to an AI-assisted presentation workflow.

## Decision

Make an Impeccable visual-direction and anti-slop review stage mandatory after evidence capture and before slide planning.

## Narrative promise

Answer whether Impeccable removes AI slop from slides. It materially reduces recognizable defaults when used as a workflow—world selection, alternatives, outside references, detection, and finish review—but it cannot replace evidence discipline, editorial judgment, or PowerPoint QA.

## Voice and tone

Decisive, editorial, evidence-led, and client-safe.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dashboards, nested card grids, decorative gradients, pill clusters, fake analytical density, and copied gallery layouts.

## Evidence and editability

Native 16:9 branded PowerPoint with editable titles, diagrams, scorecard, callouts, and footers. Authentic source frames remain raster evidence. Captured frames are used for analysis of the supplied source; external gallery assets are not reproduced.

## Success criteria

Twelve decision-ready slides; contracts validate; claim/evidence scan is clean; no unresolved structural lint error; a real render is inspected for collisions, overflow, evidence legibility, and visual consistency.
