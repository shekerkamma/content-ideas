# Executive Brief: Title, Escrow & Closing Workflow Automation

## Bottom line

This is a credible real-estate AI engineering offer when positioned as transaction coordination, closing readiness, and less manual file chasing inside title and escrow operations.

## Why now

- Qualia and SoftPro both validate workflow automation as a core operating category.
- Closing teams manage document-heavy, multi-party workflows where delays and missing items are costly.
- Document-state and payment-state orchestration make this lane a strong fit for structured workflow automation.

## What to sell

Sell a workflow package that:

1. opens and enriches files
2. applies checklist and routing logic
3. drafts reminders and status updates
4. routes missing items and exceptions to the right party

## Recommended implementation posture

- Keep title/escrow and document platforms as the systems of action.
- Use OpenHands internally for orchestration, workflow skills, and repeatable closing operations.
- Start with one file queue or one closing team.

## Verdict

**GO**

The lane has strong operator proof, clear workflow structure, and a measurable coordination ROI story.
