## Executive Thesis

Agent SaaS is not a better chatbot category; it is a business model shift from
selling software access to selling completed workflow outcomes.

## Business Decision

Pick one paid, frequent, bounded workflow, shadow the human operator, ship the
smallest useful agent with a trust wrapper, sell pilots like labor, and
productize only after the workflow repeats across customers.

## Storyboard

- The buyer is not buying AI; they are buying work that gets done.
- The first filter is budget: start where someone already pays for a
  receptionist, dispatcher, coordinator, or support role.
- The product spec comes from watching real work, not brainstorming prompts.
- The first agent should be minimum useful: draft, triage, coordinate, or
  bounded action.
- The wrapper creates trust: logs, approvals, controls, handoffs, analytics,
  and evals.
- Commercialization starts as labor-like pilots and moves toward usage or
  outcome pricing.
- Defensibility comes from owning workflow context, exception handling, policy,
  and eval sets.
- Distribution is workflow teardown: old way, agent way, proof, then paid
  amplification.
