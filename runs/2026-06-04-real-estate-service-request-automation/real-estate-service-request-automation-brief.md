# Executive Brief: Property Management Service Request & Maintenance Automation

## Bottom line

This is a credible property-management AI engineering offer when positioned as faster maintenance response, better resident communication, and less service-ops friction inside the current operating stack.

## Why now

- AppFolio and Buildium already frame maintenance workflows as automation-ready.
- Operators face round-the-clock service pressure and staff overload.
- Resident-service quality directly affects retention, reputation, and portfolio performance.

## What to sell

Sell a workflow package that:

1. triages requests across channels
2. classifies urgency and routes the next action
3. drafts resident updates and vendor tasks
4. keeps managers in control with visible queues and approvals

## Recommended implementation posture

- Keep maintenance and resident-service tools as the systems of action.
- Use OpenHands internally for orchestration, service policies, and repeatable workflow delivery.
- Start with one queue, one portfolio, or one after-hours workflow.

## Verdict

**GO**

The lane has strong proof, clear workflow structure, and measurable business value.
