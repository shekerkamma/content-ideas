# Deal Prep: Mid-Market Property Operator

## Why this account angle fits

- Mid-market property operators feel the burden of after-hours service calls, fragmented work-order handling, and resident follow-up gaps.
- Faster response and better queue visibility can improve resident experience without immediately increasing headcount.
- The offer is workflow execution, not a new resident-facing product layer.

## Positioning

Frame the offer as:

- faster maintenance response
- cleaner routing and escalation
- less staff interruption from after-hours volume
- better resident updates and queue visibility

## Discovery questions

1. Where do requests still get lost or delayed today?
2. Which requests most often require after-hours escalation?
3. How disconnected are resident updates, vendor dispatch, and work-order state?
4. Where do property teams spend the most manual coordination time?

## Likely objections

- "We already have maintenance software."
  - Response: the gap is usually workflow execution across intake, dispatch, and resident communication.
- "We cannot risk bad service decisions."
  - Response: keep emergency escalation and sensitive approvals human-controlled.
- "Every property is different."
  - Response: encode policy and routing variance as reusable workflow skills instead of rebuilding the process manually.

## Suggested next step

Offer a pilot for one maintenance queue or after-hours workflow with metrics tied to response time, queue aging, and resident-update consistency.
