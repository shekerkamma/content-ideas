# Full Strategy: Property Management Service Request & Maintenance Automation

## Strategic thesis

Service-request and maintenance automation is worth pursuing when it is sold as operational responsiveness and resident-service quality, not as a generic property-management chatbot.

## Why the wedge works

- Service workflows are constant, repetitive, and time-sensitive.
- Operators already buy maintenance and resident-service tooling.
- Delays and missed requests create visible operational and reputational cost.

## Ideal entry point

1. Ingest requests from portals, calls, email, and SMS.
2. Classify urgency and issue type.
3. Create or enrich work orders and route vendors or staff.
4. Expand into proactive maintenance, vendor coordination, and resident-service reporting.

## Solution-stack recommendation

- Maintenance and resident-service systems remain the systems of action.
- OpenHands orchestrates intake, triage, escalation, and follow-up workflow agents.
- Reporting focuses on response time, queue health, vendor performance, repeat issues, and resident-service quality.

## Delivery model

- Phase 1: intake and routing automation
- Phase 2: vendor coordination and follow-up workflows
- Phase 3: proactive and portfolio-wide service operations

## Risks

- Poor routing or urgency classification can create resident dissatisfaction
- Vendor responsiveness may still bottleneck the outcome
- Teams may distrust opaque automation
- Escalation policies can vary across operators or portfolios

## Controls

- Keep emergency or sensitive service actions human-approved
- Encode explicit service policies and escalation rules
- Start with one queue or one region before broader rollout
- Keep a visible audit trail across every workflow step

## Recommended account angle

Target mid-market or scaled operators where maintenance-call volume and after-hours burden are already painful. AppFolio-style operators are the clearest proof point, but the opportunity is broader across portfolio managers using fragmented service workflows.
