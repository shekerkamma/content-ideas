# Clip narration — the two roadmap/architecture simulators

**computebox** · 32.0 s · The silicon, slide 7
Eleven sensor channels entering one part, and a single perception output leaving it. Seven RGB cameras at eight megapixels over GMSL2 — road left and road right, two hundred-and-twenty-degree side cameras, a dashcam, a driver-monitoring camera and a rear camera. Two thermal cameras beside them. Front and rear four-D radar at seventy-seven gigahertz. Watch them converge on the die: nine cameras and two radars, fused into one AD2 perception output in real time. The bar underneath is the whole argument. Eight point six milliseconds consumed out of a thirty-three point three millisecond frame. Seventy-four percent of every frame is left unused, and that headroom is why one tapeout becomes fifteen products rather than fifteen programmes.

**roadmap** · 32.0 s · The move, slide 5
Nine steps, and five of them are already silicon. SoC1.0 ran sixty-four multiply-accumulates on an Artix-7 at a hundred and twenty-five megahertz: three point three frames a second, whole-frame, with defence revenue behind it. Each rung after it adds exactly one thing and then measures the result — a floating-point unit, the eight-by-eight weight-stationary matrix, triple-mode arithmetic, and then the cube geometry itself. Watch the frame-rate bars fill as the ladder climbs. SoC2, the part this raise funds, is thirty-two thousand seven hundred and sixty-eight multiply-accumulates at six hundred megahertz — thirty-nine point three TOPS, about forty-one frames a second on AD2, with tapeout in the fourth quarter of twenty twenty-six. Note what is missing. SoC3 was removed deliberately.
