# Deck brief — DeepGrid Semi financial model walkthrough

**Deliverable.** A 30-slide, client-ready investor walkthrough of the DeepGrid Semi
financial plan, delivered as native Google Slides.

**Audience.** Pre-Series A investors and their diligence teams.

**Basis of preparation.** Every figure is a forward-looking management projection
prepared for fundraising. Nothing in the deck is audited or reported actuals. This is
stated on the cover, in the running footer (`MANAGEMENT PROJECTIONS`) and on the close.

**Claim.** A platform business that reaches self-funding on this round: one 28 nm SoC
serves four markets, turns EBITDA-positive in FY2028, and finances its successor chip
from operating profit.

**Arc.** Ask → market → platform → revenue plan → profitability → silicon and capital →
risk and decision. Four dark chapter dividers separate the five content parts.

## Editorial rules applied

1. **No model-version language.** "corrected", "older", "supporting", "superseded",
   "previous", "version", "worksheet", "workbook", "business plan" and processing
   commentary are absent from all visible text. Enforced by a build-time scan (0 hits).
2. **No comparison slides.** Nothing contrasts one source against another. The two
   workbooks feed one coherent management case.
3. **Provenance lives in speaker notes only.** Every slide's notes name the source
   sheet and cell range.
4. **Financial figures come from the financial model alone.** The business plan supplies
   only what the model lacks: headcount shape, share register, facility terms and prior
   capital. Its expense schedules total ₹647.2 Cr against the model's ₹598.1 Cr because
   they were built for a different revenue base, so the two are never spliced. The
   unreconciled join is disclosed on the operating-plan slide and listed as a diligence
   deliverable rather than silently merged.

## Two source findings that changed the content

- **Regulatory timing is not an error.** The demand sheet's own note distinguishes the
  DDAWS / blind-spot / lane-departure suite (live April and October 2026) from the
  advanced emergency braking requirement (AIS-162 / IS-11852, deferred to 1 October
  2027). These are two obligations on two timetables. The deck presents them as a
  sequence rather than reporting a discrepancy.
- **The 774x cost advantage is not presented.** The model derives it by comparing a
  $3.88 bare die with a $3,000 incumbent module that includes packaging, board, software
  and support. The slide states the die cost and says the like-for-like system
  comparison is provided in diligence.

## Open items carried as diligence commitments

Share register totals 102,797 shares against the 100,890 used in the pricing basis
(1,907 difference) · facility draw, repayment and covenants are not yet inside the cash
schedule · hiring schedule is not yet linked to the overhead ratios · the monthly burn
figure and its note differ by an order of magnitude and is reconciled before close.
None of these are presented as defects; all appear as evidence management will provide.
