## Video direction

- **Identity:** Aurora Glass. Near-black canvas (#080b11), teal (#2dd4bf) as the lone accent,
  aurora gradient (teal→sky→magenta) reserved ONLY for the hero word on the cover and CTA.
- **Type:** massive lowercase Barlow 800–900, negative-tracked, as graphic primitive; one display
  moment per frame. IBM Plex Mono UPPERCASE 0.14em for all chrome (kickers, tags, catalogue No.,
  stat labels, the `/` bullet marker). No uppercase display, no italics.
- **Register:** DARK only. No full-bleed teal grounds (Aurora Glass: "gradient is a spotlight,
  not wallpaper"). Hierarchy from weight, size, and 1px hairlines (#1f2530) — flat plane,
  zero shadow, zero radius except pills (999px) and framed screenshots (12px).
- **Motion (per motion-principles):** vary ease/direction/duration; never start at t=0; each scene
  builds (stagger in) → breathes (one slow ambient: aurora glow drift behind everything) →
  resolves (crossfade out, exits faster than entrances). Eases: power3.out / expo.out for entrances,
  back.out for pops, sine for ambient. Determinstic only (sine of timeline time, no random/Date).
- **Screenshots** are real captures, framed as insets (12px radius, 1px hairline, soft teal glow),
  never full-bleed — generous near-black canvas around them.
