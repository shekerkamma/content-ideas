## Notes for the storyboard
- The core narrative is **BEFORE → AFTER**: lead with the generic-AI problem, reveal the fix.
- `before-dashboard.png` and `after-dashboard.png` are the same app — a true A/B; frame them
  as a split or a swipe/wipe transition for maximum impact.
- `rebrand-estate-charts.png` is the most data-rich, most "finished product" asset — reserve it
  for the proof/stat beat near the end.
- No logos, no captured brand fonts; the brand identity is the Aurora Glass token set itself.
