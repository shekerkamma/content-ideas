# Current manual workflow inventory

Required by the skill before building. Recorded late — this should have preceded the
Level 3 build, and its absence is why the build was not gated.

| Attribute | Current reality |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Ad hoc. A tender alert, an introduction, or a founder conversation. No defined entry event. |
| **Inputs** | Four documents that disagree (BP-1A, BP-1B, June IM, July deck), plus whatever the founder remembers. |
| **Decisions** | Is this lead worth pursuing · what do we claim · what do we price · who follows up. All made per-instance, by memory. |
| **Outputs** | Emails and decks composed from scratch each time. No reusable artifact. |
| **Owner** | Founder for everything material. No separation between commercial and technical response. |
| **Time** | Unmeasured. The reconciliation tax per outbound message is the cost this build targets. |
| **Failure modes** | Retired claims resurface (the June IM is still in circulation); positioning blurs across tracks; no record of what was said to whom. |
| **Approval points** | None written down. Pricing and claims are decided in the moment. |

## What this tells us

There is no Level 1 or 2 to build on — no saved prompt, no chain — because there is no
repeated process to save. Jumping to Level 3 is correct under the skill's "smallest missing
artifact" rule, but it means the context files are being written from **documents rather than
from observed practice**, which is a known weakness:

- `05-objections.md` should come from real calls. It does not, because no call record exists.
- `03-voice.md` needs real sent messages. None were supplied.

Both are recorded as gaps rather than papered over.

## Automation eligibility check

The skill requires a judgement to have been performed manually ~10 times before automating
it. **No acquisition judgement here meets that bar.** Consequence: this build stops at
Level 3 (a knowledge layer, not an automated judgement) and no Level 4+ automation may be
built until real reps exist.
