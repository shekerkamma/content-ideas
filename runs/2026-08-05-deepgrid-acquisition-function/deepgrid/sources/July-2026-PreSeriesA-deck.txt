Architecture works on FPGA.

 Six-chiplet combo-die (A100 / R100 / T100 / D100 / S100 / H100) covers ADAS, radar, LiDAR, secure enclave, telematics and health in one BOM slot.

FPGA boards are uneconomic at volume.

 This is the entire investment thesis: fund the ASIC transition, not another FPGA generation.

14 · ICP SELECTION SYNTHESIS
DEEPGRID SEMI · PRE-SERIES A
Defense first, Fleet in parallel. Not a pivot — a sequence.
The synthesis of the scorecard, red-team, and the roadmap already in the original pitch.
THE TENSION
Defense wins the scorecard — but flunks repeatability.
Defense scores 5/5 on evidence, 5/5 on budget, 5/5 on fit — but 2/5 on repeatability.
 Defense contracts are lumpy. Two won contracts don't imply twenty. They do not scale into the ~88% systems-revenue business that the ₹55 Cr raise is built to fund.
 Fleet, meanwhile, is a repeatable-by-design segment (5/5 repeatability) — but has 1/5 evidence.
 You cannot ship the deck as "Defense company" and you cannot ship it as "Fleet company either." One has revenue, the other has volume physics.
THE SYNTHESIS
Two-track: Defense as resourced primary, Fleet as experiment.
Months 0–6. Defense runs as resourced primary. Objective: get the second signed contract that turns "1 CR revenue" into a customer signal, and generate the field data Fleet needs to close its first pilot.
 In parallel, months 0–3. Fleet runs as a 20-interview discovery experiment against the paid-pilot kill criterion.
 Month 6 gate. Re-score both segments with fresh evidence. If Fleet passed its kill criterion, it becomes co-primary. If not, Defense stays primary and Fleet drops to watch-only.
 This confirms — not invents — the original pitch's three-phase roadmap.
THE STRONGEST COUNTER-CASE
A defence-first motion risks the fundraising narrative.
Investors are being asked to fund a chip business, priced against a 5M+ commercial-vehicle TAM. A defense-only story does not clear that hurdle.
 So this only holds together as a sequenced two-track plan — never as a flat pivot.
 External framing rule (Condition 4, slide 37). Every deck, one-pager, and investor email must present the plan as Defense-sequenced-then-Fleet, not Defense-only. Any external doc that reads as "pivot to defense" gets pulled.
 Internal escalation rule. If the day-90 checkpoint shows Fleet failing AND Defense-2 not signed, this becomes a Board-level re-plan, not a marketing tweak.
THE ONE-LINE SYNTHESIS
Run Defense as resourced primary for 6 months — the fastest way to generate the missing Fleet evidence — while Fleet runs in parallel as a secondary, kill-criterion-gated experiment.
