# Story pack — DeepGrid client acquisition operating system

The slide count is an output of this spine, not an input. Eleven content slides plus a cover
and three dividers = 14. Any slide that cannot state its role below gets cut.

## BLUF
DeepGrid already has Level-3-grade acquisition thinking. It is trapped in four documents that
contradict each other, so every outbound message costs a human a reconciliation. Writing it
into six files makes it executable — and two segments become sellable immediately.

## Audience and decision
Commercial lead runs it. Founder gates it: voice samples, the claimable set, and pricing.
The decision this deck serves is *what do we do on Monday*, not *is this a good market*.

## Tension
Every message today requires reconciling four disagreeing documents and remembering which of
nine claims have been retired. That tax does not scale, and when it is paid wrongly the claim
fails in front of the buyer's evaluator — costing the credibility of the true claims too.

## Argument arc
knowledge is unusable → make it six files → who is sellable now → what we sell and how it
prices → what we are allowed to say → how a lead travels → where this goes → what is needed
