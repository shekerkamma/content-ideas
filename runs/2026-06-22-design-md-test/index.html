What to look for
  • Teal is the only accent on every screen — never a 3-gradient soup.
  • Cards are --glass on dark, hairline borders — never white-on-grey.
  • Mono-uppercase = metadata; sans = content. Gradient only on one H1 per page.
  • Buttons, inputs, pills, tables are byte-identical across all three pages.
