# Deck brief

## Audience

Technology, AI infrastructure, and procurement decision-makers evaluating emerging semiconductor vendors.

## Decision

How to segment the 2026 startup field and which proof thresholds to apply before evaluation or procurement.

## Narrative promise

Reframe the ten-company list as four infrastructure bottleneck strategies, distinguish current commercial signals from roadmap-dependent claims, and end with an actionable vendor scorecard.

## Voice and tone

Executive, evidence-led, concise, and explicit about vendor claims versus independently demonstrated results.

## Anti-references

Avoid a generic “Nvidia challengers” ranking, decorative chip imagery, logo walls, nested-card dashboards, and funding-as-performance rhetoric.

## Evidence and editability

CRN provides the list and market framing; the local research file records You.com Livecrawl Level 2 and official-company verification. All PowerPoint visuals are native editable shapes and text.

## Success criteria

Thirteen coherent 16:9 slides; branded template authority recorded; contract validation passes; structural validation passes; zero unresolved lint errors; real Office render inspected for overflow, collisions, and legibility.
