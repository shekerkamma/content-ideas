CHAPTER 02ARCHITECTURAL BETS

Workload-Specific Inference Silicon

Startups like Axelera AI, d Matrix, Fractile, MatX, and Etched focus on optimizing inference economics, latency, and deployment for specific AI models or edge scenarios.

This reflects the commercial shift from one-time model training to repeated model serving, demanding specialized efficiency.

Axelera AI: Edge inference with digital in-memory compute.
d Matrix: Data-center inference with rack-scale fabric.
Etched: Transformer models inference via specialized ASIC.

CHAPTER 02ARCHITECTURAL BETS

Memory Movement & Interconnect

Technologies addressing data movement are crucial for maximizing compute utilization and reducing idle time. This includes high-bandwidth photonic interconnects and programmable DPUs.

Companies like Lightmatter, Cornelis, and Xsight Labs are key players in this domain.

Lightmatter: 3D-stacked silicon photonics for bandwidth.
Cornelis: Scale-out fabric for cluster congestion.
Xsight Labs: Programmable Ethernet switching and DPU.

Open Compute & Rack Scale Integration

Emphasizing open architectures and rack-scale systems reduces customer lock-in and fosters software ecosystems, despite potential tooling challenges.

NextSilicon and Tenstorrent lead in customizable CPU paths and open network programmability.

Tenstorrent: Open, licensable AI compute and RISC V CPU IP.
NextSilicon: Runtime-reconfigurable dataflow and RISC V CPU.
d Matrix: (also) Rack-scale fabric/software integration.

CHAPTER 03PRODUCT MATURITY

Shipping Evidence (Today)

Focus on verified production-level performance, system compatibility, utilization, and power efficiency.

Demand independent performance validation for current products.

Evidence of repeatable rack deployments and mature open ecosystems are critical.

Roadmap Claims (2027-2028)

Many flagship products are scheduled for 2027 or 2028, indicating investor conviction in a portfolio of experiments.

Higher risk due to potential architectural shifts and market changes before launch.

Funding signifies runway, not performance validation or market fit.

CHAPTER 03SPECIALIZATION RISKS

Extreme specialization, while efficient, carries significant roadmap risk.

Companies like Etched (Transformer models-specific ASIC) or MatX (LLM-specific processor) achieve high efficiency but are vulnerable to rapid shifts in model architectures or workload patterns.
