# Deck brief

## Audience

Venture and growth investors evaluating AI semiconductor infrastructure.

## Decision

Where to place core exposure, execution-led growth capital, and option-sized moonshot bets.

## Narrative promise

Convert a ten-company media list into an investable hierarchy using ten explicit dimensions, a proof ladder, portfolio construction, and kill criteria.

## Voice and tone

Provocative technical editorial: thesis first, skeptical of marketing, explicit about judgment and uncertainty.

## Anti-references

Company encyclopedia, logo wall, generic GPU-challenger narrative, undifferentiated cards, and funding presented as proof.

## Evidence and editability

CRN and primary-company evidence from the completed You.com Livecrawl Level 2 research run. All visuals are native editable PowerPoint objects.

## Success criteria

Twelve slides; ten-dimension ranking; portfolio hierarchy; proof gates; kill criteria; branded native PPTX; zero OfficeCLI issues; rendered contact sheet reviewed.
