# Metric definitions

The revised deck avoids false precision. It uses ordinal evidence states rather than a composite 50-point investment score.

## Commercial proof

- **0 — roadmap:** announced architecture or product with no working silicon disclosed.
- **1 — silicon:** working or sampled silicon, but no customer system in production.
- **2 — validation:** named partner/customer evaluation or early-access system.
- **3 — production:** shipping/production claim plus a named deployment, distribution route, or contract signal.

## Evidence independence

- **Low:** company claim only.
- **Medium:** company claim corroborated by a named partner, customer, government lab, technical paper, or credible reporting.
- **High:** independent reproducible benchmark, filing-grade commercial data, or multiple named production customers. No cohort company currently clears High across both technical and commercial proof.

## Attachment breadth

- **Broad:** can sell across several accelerator or workload winners.
- **Moderate:** serves several workloads but depends on a distinct software/system architecture.
- **Narrow:** value depends on a specific model family or workload remaining dominant.

## Evidence-adjusted valuation

Publicly reported valuation or cumulative funding is shown beside commercial proof and evidence independence. It is not divided into a synthetic ratio when contract quality, revenue recognition, ownership, and dilution are undisclosed.
