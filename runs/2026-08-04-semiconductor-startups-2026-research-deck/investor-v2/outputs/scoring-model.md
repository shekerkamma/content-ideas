# Evidence-state model

Each company is assessed on five comparable dimensions: buyer bottleneck, attachment breadth, commercial proof, evidence independence, and principal failure mode. The model deliberately does not collapse these into a single numeric score.

Portfolio roles follow from combinations:

- **Core diligence:** broad attachment plus validation/production evidence.
- **Execution diligence:** credible product plus commercialization or systems-integration gap.
- **Milestone option:** narrow attachment, roadmap-stage product, or predominantly vendor-controlled proof.

Sensitivity: a named paid deployment with reproducible workload economics can move a company one role upward; a roadmap slip, failed benchmark, software migration burden, or customer-concentration disclosure can move it downward.
