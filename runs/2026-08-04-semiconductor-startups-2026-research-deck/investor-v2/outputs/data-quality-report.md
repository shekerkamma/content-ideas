# Data quality report

- Cohort coverage: 10/10 companies.
- Comparison arenas: optical interconnect, scale-out fabric, DPU/Ethernet, open accelerator/IP, memory-centric inference, edge inference, reconfigurable HPC, transformer ASIC, frontier-model processor, in-memory inference.
- Evidence mix: official product/newsroom pages, a US national laboratory, SEC filing, MLCommons, peer-reviewed/preprint technical work, standards body, and credible financial/technology reporting.
- Primary/near-primary share: sufficient for product architecture and announced milestones; weaker for private-company revenue, margins, pricing, yields and customer concentration.
- Systematic gap: none of the ten appears in MLPerf Inference 5.1; cohort-wide apples-to-apples performance and power comparison is therefore unavailable.
- Bias: vendor-published benchmarks dominate. Partner announcements reduce but do not remove this bias.
- Staleness: current through 2026-08-04 for volatile funding and product-status claims.
- Search failures: several Exa fresh extractions timed out or errored; those URLs were excluded unless separately verified.

Decision consequence: the deck uses evidence states, bull/bear debates, and proof gates instead of a precise performance leaderboard.
