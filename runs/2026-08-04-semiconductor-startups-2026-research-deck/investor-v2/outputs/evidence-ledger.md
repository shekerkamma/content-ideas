# Evidence ledger summary

The ledger contains 30 decision-relevant claim rows covering all ten cohort companies, incumbent power, independent benchmark coverage, software ecosystem, historical exits, customer concentration, and geopolitical risk. The strongest evidence is primary: SEC filings, official product/funding releases, a US national laboratory, MLCommons and standards bodies. The weakest evidence is vendor benchmark language without reproducible workload conditions.

The deck must distinguish three things visibly: a product exists, a customer is evaluating it, and a paid workload runs repeatedly in production. Those are not synonyms.
