# Story architect pack

## BLUF

Invest behind interfaces and systems that monetize heterogeneous compute; price specialist accelerators as milestone options until customer workloads, software migration, manufacturing and system economics are independently visible.

## Audience decision

An institutional/VC investor must decide which companies deserve core diligence now, which belong in execution diligence, and which should receive only milestone-contingent attention.

## Tension

AI infrastructure demand is real, but funding and performance narratives are outrunning comparable public proof. The cohort appears diversified by company name while remaining correlated through TSMC, HBM, packaging, CUDA migration, hyperscaler procurement and model evolution.

## Content cuts

- Remove the previous synthetic 50-point ranking and 70/30 allocation; neither was supported by ownership, price, revenue or dilution data.
- Do not imply that cohort peers are always direct competitors.
- Keep vendor performance claims only when labeled and paired with missing benchmark conditions.
- Move pipeline/tool details out of client-visible slides.
