# Run learnings

1. **The June IM is the pre-corrections artefact.** It repeats four of the five claims the
   company's own July Corrections Ledger bans. Any external circulation undoes the correction
   work. Highest-value finding for the operator, and it cost nothing to find — it required
   reading two of their own documents against each other.
2. **The mandate is phased and the decks lead with the wrong date.** GSR 184(E) was notified
   March 2025. April 2026 bound only *newly introduced* models; **October 2026** binds existing
   production models. That is the commercial trigger, and it is eight weeks out.
3. **The whitespace is closing ~18 months early.** Netrasemi — mapped "(2027)" in July — went
   production-ready May 2026 with 3 OEM trials on a ₹107 Cr Series A. Caveat retained: the
   A2000 is a general edge-AI SoC, not a certified ADAS combo-die. Narrowed, not closed.
4. **Three margin numbers were never contradictory, just unlabelled**: compute board ~84–93%,
   kit 72%, blended portfolio FY32 88%. Naming the level dissolves the apparent conflict.
5. **Tooling:** `~/.hermes/.env` (WSL) holds an OpenAI `sk-…` key under `YOU_API_KEY`. The valid
   `ydc…` key is Windows-side. Always invoke You.com under `env -u YOU_API_KEY`.
6. **QA gap found:** `qa_geometry.py` does not detect opaque-rect-over-rect (a summary band
   sitting on card bodies with no text collision). Caught only by contact-sheet review.
