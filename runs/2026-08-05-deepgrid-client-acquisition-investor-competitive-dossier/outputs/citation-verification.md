## Root cause
All regulatory evidence came from vendor and consultancy blogs; none was reconciled against
the gazette. Five pages agreeing was **correlated error from a common upstream draft**, not
corroboration. One cited a "February 2026 MoRTH notification" that does not exist.

**Process fix:** for any regulatory claim, the gazette or ministry source is mandatory before
the claim may appear on a slide. Vendor blogs are discovery only.
