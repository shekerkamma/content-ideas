# Deck brief

## Audience

Client-facing teams, design leaders, and AI-assisted product teams evaluating how to introduce
taste and control into generative design workflows.

## Decision

Adopt Impeccable as a structured feedback and design-governance layer—not as a one-shot styling
button. Keep human judgment at every commitment point.

## Narrative promise

Explain the source video's complete argument as a standalone briefing: why generic outputs
converge, how Worlds and Live Mode change the decision loop, where the terminal still matters, and
why design memory plus independent review make taste durable.

## Voice and tone

Direct, editorial, evidence-aware, and practitioner-oriented. Use confident action titles while
clearly separating presenter judgment from measured fact.

## Anti-references

Screenshot recaps, generic consulting card grids, decorative gradients, vague AI claims, and slides
that require playback to make sense.

## Evidence and editability

Hybrid native: 12 fully native slides plus native annotations on three exact source-frame proof
slides. Claims link to transcript windows in the presentation evidence contract.

## Success criteria

The deck communicates the source argument without playback; at least 75% of slides are native
synthesis; no more than three slides use screenshots; claims and visuals link to source evidence;
PowerPoint renders all 15 slides without collisions, clipping, or missing media.
