## Feed-Grounded Context

### Challenge
- Brokerages waste 60-70% of agent time on unqualified leads — manual lead scoring relies on gut feel, not data
- Transaction coordination requires 150+ touchpoints per deal across MLS, CRM, DocuSign, title companies, and lenders — most handled manually
- Market analysis takes hours per listing — agents pull comps from 3-5 sources and manually reconcile pricing recommendations

### Solution
- AI-powered lead scoring analyzes behavioral signals (property views, search patterns, engagement frequency) to prioritize leads with 25-40% higher conversion rates
- Agentic transaction coordinator automates document routing, deadline tracking, compliance checks, and stakeholder communication across the full deal lifecycle
- Automated CMA generation pulls real-time MLS data, applies ML-driven pricing models, and produces client-ready market reports in minutes instead of hours

### Workflow
1. New lead enters CRM → AI scoring engine evaluates 50+ behavioral and demographic signals → assigns priority tier → triggers personalized nurture sequence or immediate agent handoff
2. Listing or offer accepted → transaction agent activates → orchestrates document collection, compliance timeline, inspection scheduling, and lender coordination via API integrations
3. Agent requests CMA → system pulls MLS comps, public records, market trends → applies pricing model → generates branded PDF report with recommended list price and confidence interval
