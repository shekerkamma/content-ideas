# Real Estate Brokerage AI Stage 1 Write-back

## Summary

Stage 1 validated a brokerage automation wedge built around three linked workflows:

- AI lead scoring and routing inside the CRM
- transaction coordination across CRM, docs, title, lender, and compliance steps
- automated CMA generation for pricing and client communication

## Durable findings

- The commercial wedge is stronger when the entry point is speed-to-lead and prioritization, then expands into transaction coordination after workflow trust is established.
- The workflow story is concrete enough for operator-facing packaging because the value path is measurable: better lead prioritization, faster transaction movement, and faster client-ready market analysis.
- The recalled implementation spine from prior memory remains useful: CRM specialist workflows, document automation, marketing automation, and integrations such as Zillow, DocuSign, Stripe, and QuickBooks.
- The use case should stay anchored in incumbent systems of action rather than replacing brokerage CRM and transaction tooling.
