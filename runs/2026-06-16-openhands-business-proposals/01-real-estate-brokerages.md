## 1. The Business Problem

### What Is Happening Today

Every brokerage, regardless of size, runs the same three manual workflows:

**Transaction Coordination:**
An agent closes 6 transactions per month. Each deal requires a human coordinator
to track 12–18 deadline dates, chase 5–7 parties for documents, and send daily
status updates to title, escrow, lender, buyer, and seller. Cost: $300–$500 per
transaction. At 6 deals/month: **$1,800–$3,000/month in TC fees** — and agents
still report spending 4–6 hours per month chasing their own coordinators.

**Listing Administration:**
Each listing requires 45–90 minutes of writing: MLS description (character-limited),
syndication copy for Zillow and Realtor.com, social captions, email announcement.
For a brokerage with 4 active listings per month: **6–12 hours of agent time** on
a task that produces the same output every time.

**Lead Management:**
The average agent has 300+ contacts. Without scoring, outreach is random. Agents
report missing 3–5 closeable transactions per year because the right lead did not
receive a timely follow-up at the right moment. At a $10,000 average commission,
that represents **$30,000–$50,000 in unrealized annual revenue**.

### Why Current Solutions Fail

| Solution | What It Does | What It Misses |
|---|---|---|
| Dotloop / Skyslope | Document storage | Does not track deadlines or chase parties |
| Follow Up Boss / KVCore | CRM | Does not score leads or automate nurture |
| Human TC | Coordination | Does not scale; $300-500/transaction cost |
| Generic SaaS tools | Partial automation | Not built for this brokerage's workflow |

The gap is not a missing tool. It is a missing team — one that reads contracts,
understands deadlines, writes copy, scores leads, and acts autonomously on the
90% of tasks that are identical every time.

## 2. The Proposed Solution

We deploy an AI Engineering Team — built specifically for your brokerage, running
continuously, and costing a fraction of its human equivalent.

This is not software you subscribe to. It is software we build for you: configured
to your CRM, your email address, your MLS account, your calendar, your forms.
When a deal opens, it reads the purchase agreement. When a listing is ready, it
writes the description. When a lead goes hot, it alerts you.

### What Gets Built (Initial Engagement)

**Workflow 1 — Transaction Coordinator Automation**
Your AI team reads every executed purchase agreement, extracts all deadline dates,
opens the deal file, sends a welcome email to all parties, runs daily status check-ins,
chases missing documents, and closes the file at funded. You receive a text message
only when a contingency is at risk or an exception requires judgment.

*Replaces: human TC fees of $300–$500/transaction*

**Workflow 2 — Listing Description + Syndication Pipeline**
Agent submits address + bullet points + photos. Your AI team writes the MLS
description, the Zillow/Realtor.com syndication version, three social captions,
and the email announcement. Stages everything for a single approval click, then
publishes across all channels.

*Replaces: 45–90 minutes of agent writing per listing*

**Workflow 3 (Month 2 expansion) — Lead Scoring + Hot-Lead Alerts**
Your AI team scores every contact in your CRM on recency, frequency, and inquiry
signals. Each morning you receive an SMS with your three hottest leads and the
exact reason they scored hot — so the first call of the day is always the right one.

*Generates: 1–3 additional closed transactions per year in recovered leads*
