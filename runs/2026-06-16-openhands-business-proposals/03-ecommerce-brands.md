## 1. The Business Problem

### What Is Happening Today

**Abandoned Cart Recovery:**
Industry average cart abandonment rate: 70–85%. For a brand doing $50,000/month in
GMV, $35,000–$42,500 in revenue is abandoned before checkout every month. Standard
Klaviyo flows recover 5–10% of that at most — because they are generic, not personalized,
and they do not adapt based on why the customer left. A brand with a smart recovery
system can push recovery rates to 15–25%, adding **$5,000–$10,000/month** in incremental
revenue without acquiring a single new customer.

**Customer Service Repetition:**
A CS team in a DTC brand receives roughly the same 12 questions on rotation: Where is
my order? How do I return this? Can I change my address? Is this in stock? What's your
return policy? These 12 question types account for 60–80% of all tickets. Every hour
your team spends on them is an hour not spent on reviews, retention, and VIP handling.
At 50 tickets per day with a 4-minute handle time, that is **3+ hours of team time daily**
on questions your AI team can answer in seconds.

**Product Content at Scale:**
When a brand adds 20 new SKUs, someone has to write 20 product descriptions, 20 SEO
meta titles, 20 alt text strings, and 20 email feature blurbs. For a small team, that
is 3–5 hours of writing. For a seasonal brand that adds 100+ SKUs per quarter, it is
a full-time job. Without it, descriptions are thin, SEO is weak, and conversion is lower.

### Why Current Tools Miss the Point

| Tool | What It Does | What It Misses |
|---|---|---|
| Klaviyo | Email sequences | Not personalized by cart content or exit behavior |
| Gorgias / Freshdesk | Ticket routing | Does not auto-respond; still requires human to open each ticket |
| ChatGPT | One-off writing | Not connected to your product catalog or brand voice |
| Shopify apps | Point solutions | Each solves one problem; none talk to each other |

## 2. The Proposed Solution

We deploy an AI engineering team configured for your Shopify store — connected to your
cart data, your product catalog, your email platform, and your customer service inbox.
Every workflow is built to match your brand voice, not a generic template.

### What Gets Built (Initial Engagement)

**Workflow 1 — Abandoned Cart Recovery (Intelligent)**
When a cart is abandoned, your AI team reads what was in the cart, checks the customer's
order history, and sends a personalized recovery sequence — not a generic "You left
something behind." If the cart contained a skincare bundle, the recovery message speaks
to that specific combination. If the customer has ordered before, it references their
history. Sequences adapt based on whether the customer opens, clicks, or ignores each
message.

*Recovers: 12–22% of abandoned carts vs 5–8% industry baseline = $3,000–$7,500/mo on $50K GMV*

**Workflow 2 — CS Deflection + Auto-Response**
Your AI team connects to your Gorgias or Freshdesk inbox, reads each incoming ticket,
and auto-responds to the 10–12 repeating question types with accurate, brand-voice
answers — pulling order status from Shopify, checking inventory, quoting your return
policy, providing tracking links. Complex tickets and complaints are flagged for your
team. First-response time drops from hours to seconds for 60–80% of volume.

*Deflects: 30–50 tickets/day · Saves: 2–3 staff hours daily · Reduces: churn from slow support*

**Workflow 3 (Month 2 expansion) — Product Description Factory**
When your team uploads a new SKU with a title, images, and bullet points, your AI team
generates the full product description, SEO meta title, meta description, alt text, and
email feature copy — in your brand voice — and stages everything for a single approval
click before it goes live.

*Generates: full content package per SKU in 3 minutes vs 20–30 minutes manual*
