## 1. The Business Problem

### What Is Happening Today

**Monthly Client Reporting:**
Every month, every client wants a performance report. The standard agency process:
pull data from Google Analytics, Meta Ads, LinkedIn, Google Ads, and the client's
CRM; paste it into a Looker Studio or Google Slides template; write the narrative;
format everything to the client's brand; send. For an agency managing 10 clients,
that is **30–50 hours of account manager time monthly** on a deliverable that clients
read in 8 minutes and then ask three questions your AM could have answered in the
report if they had had time to think.

**Content Calendar Production:**
Most agency retainers include 15–30 social posts per month per client. The workflow:
brief → write → review → revise → approve → schedule → publish. At a 3-round revision
average and 45 minutes per piece of content, a 10-post-per-month client consumes
**7.5 hours of writer time** before scheduling. Across 8 clients at 15 posts each:
90 hours of content writing per month — a full-time headcount just on social.

**Campaign Monitoring + Alerts:**
Paid media campaigns can drift significantly between weekly check-ins. A Google Ads
campaign that started bidding into the wrong audience segment on Tuesday costs real
money by Friday. Most agencies catch these issues at the next scheduled review.
An AI team watching CPM, CPC, ROAS, and conversion rate around the clock catches
them in hours, not days.

### The Margin Problem

Agency gross margin should be 40–55%. For most agencies, it sits at 25–35% because
the labor cost of fulfillment — reporting, content, monitoring — scales linearly
with clients. Every new client adds headcount. Automation breaks this pattern: the
same team can handle 30% more accounts without hiring.

## 2. The Proposed Solution

We deploy an AI engineering team connected to your analytics stack, your ad platforms,
and your project management system. Reporting, scheduling, and monitoring all run
through your existing toolchain — we build the connective tissue your team has been
doing manually.

### What Gets Built (Initial Engagement)

**Workflow 1 — Automated Monthly Reporting**
On the 1st of each month, your AI team pulls performance data from every source
(GA4, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Google Ads, Klaviyo), compares against prior month
and against targets, writes the narrative commentary in your agency's voice, and
assembles a client-ready PDF/Slides report. Your AM receives it ready to review
on the 2nd. They spend 15 minutes checking the narrative and adding context — not
4 hours pulling the data.

*Saves: 3–5 hours per client per month · At 10 clients: 30–50 hours/month recovered*

**Workflow 2 — Content Pipeline + Scheduling**
When a content brief is approved, your AI team drafts the posts, writes the captions
in the client's approved brand voice, stages them in Buffer or Hootsuite for AM
review, and publishes on approval. Revisions are flagged and returned to queue
automatically. The AM's job becomes approving, not writing.

*Reduces: 7–10 hours per client per month on content production · 70+ hours at 10 clients*

**Workflow 3 (Month 2 expansion) — 24/7 Campaign Monitoring + Alerts**
Your AI team monitors every active paid campaign on 4-hour intervals. If ROAS drops
below target, CPM spikes 40%+, or a campaign starts spending without conversions,
your AM receives an SMS alert with the campaign name, the anomaly, and a suggested
action — before the client's Monday morning email arrives.

*Prevents: budget waste from undetected campaign drift · Reduces: firefighting calls*
