## 1. The Business Problem

### What Is Happening Today

**Missed Calls = Missed Jobs:**
The phone rings during a job. The dispatcher is already talking to another customer.
The caller hangs up, calls the next company on Google, and books with them. Industry
research suggests home service companies miss 20–35% of inbound calls during business
hours and nearly all calls after hours. For a company doing $1.5M in annual revenue,
$300,000–$525,000 in billable work may be walking out the door every year because
nobody answered the phone.

**Quote Turnaround Delays:**
A customer calls with a HVAC replacement need. The technician visits, submits a job
description. Someone back at the office needs to look up equipment cost, calculate
labor, and send a quote. Average time: 24–48 hours. Customer who needed the decision
made today calls two other companies. Whichever company sends the quote in 2 hours
wins the job. **Speed of quote is one of the top 3 conversion factors in home services.**

**Unrenewed Service Agreements:**
A maintenance agreement customer whose contract lapsed 6 months ago is worth $200–$400
in annual recurring revenue plus priority-dispatch rates on emergency calls. Most
companies have a list of lapsed agreements that nobody has time to work. At 100 lapsed
agreements and a 30% reactivation rate, that is **$6,000–$12,000/year in recoverable
recurring revenue** sitting in a spreadsheet.

## 2. The Proposed Solution

We deploy an AI dispatch-support team connected to your scheduling system (ServiceTitan,
Jobber, Housecall Pro), your phone system (via SMS or web form capture), and your
customer database. Technicians in the field are not disrupted. Dispatchers handle only
what the AI team cannot.

### What Gets Built (Initial Engagement)

**Workflow 1 — After-Hours + Overflow Call Capture**
When a call goes unanswered, your AI team sends an immediate SMS to the caller:
"Thanks for calling [Company Name]. We're with a customer right now. Reply with your
name, address, and what's going on and we'll call you back within 30 minutes [or first
thing tomorrow if after hours]." 60–70% of callers text back. Your dispatcher opens
the next morning to a pre-populated list of callbacks with customer info and job
description — no voicemail-to-transcription guessing.

*Recovers: 15–25% of missed calls that currently result in zero revenue*
*At 20 missed calls/month × 20% recovery × $1,000 avg job: +$4,000/month*

**Workflow 2 — Quote Generation + Follow-Up**
After a technician visits and submits a job ticket, your AI team generates a quote
draft using your price book, sends it to the customer within 2 hours, and follows up
automatically at 24 hours and 48 hours if no response. If the customer replies with
a question, it flags the dispatcher. If they accept, it marks the job as sold.

*Reduces: quote turnaround from 24–48 hours to 2 hours*
*Improves: close rate by 15–25% through speed alone*

**Workflow 3 (Month 2 expansion) — Service Agreement Renewal Campaigns**
Your AI team identifies customers with lapsed maintenance agreements, runs a renewal
campaign via SMS and email, and books renewals directly into your scheduling system.
Customers who do not respond after 3 contacts are flagged for a personal dispatcher call.

*Reactivates: 20–35% of lapsed agreements · At 100 lapsed × 30% × $300/yr: $9,000/yr recovered*

## 6. Why Act Now

**Summer is the highest-volume season for HVAC.** Peak emergency calls, peak AC
replacement demand, and peak competitor spending on Google Ads all converge in June–August.
A company that installs the missed-call capture system before the peak recovers its

the tail end.

**Quote speed is a winner-take-all game.** If three companies receive the same lead
from Google Local Services, the company that quotes in 2 hours will close it. The
companies that quote in 24 hours are competing over the remainder. Being fastest does
not require being cheapest — it requires having a system.
