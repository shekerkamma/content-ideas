## Common Structure

Each proposal follows the same 7-section format:

1. **Executive Summary** — 2-sentence ROI hook + proposed engagement + timeline
2. **The Business Problem** — 3 specific pain points with dollar-quantified cost of inaction
3. **The Proposed Solution** — 2 initial workflows + 1 Month 2 expansion
4. **Business Case** — ROI table (Month 1) + 12-month or 3-year projection
5. **Commercial Terms** — 3-tier pricing table (Starter / Growth / Scale) + setup fee
6. **Implementation Timeline** — Week-by-week to autopilot
7. **Why Act Now** — Urgency without pressure (first-mover advantage, compounding data, seasonal timing)
