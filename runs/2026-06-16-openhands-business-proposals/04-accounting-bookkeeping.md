## 1. The Business Problem

### What Is Happening Today

**Transaction Categorization + Reconciliation Backlog:**
A bookkeeping team processing 200–500 transactions per month per client spends 4–8 hours
per client monthly on categorization, matching, and exception review. For a firm with
20 monthly bookkeeping clients, that is 80–160 hours per month of work that follows
the same 85–90% pattern every time. The remaining 10–15% exceptions are the only
part that actually requires judgment. **Your staff is spending 85% of their time on
the 85% that should be automated.**

**Document Collection Before Deadlines:**
Tax season, quarterly closes, and audit prep all require collecting the same documents
from the same clients who forget every year: bank statements, receipts, payroll
registers, prior-year returns. The average firm reports spending 15–25% of pre-deadline
capacity on email follow-up — chasing a client who has not uploaded their Q3 Chase
statement yet. At 10 minutes per follow-up across 30 clients, that is **5 hours per
week**, every week from January through April.

**Client Onboarding Data Entry:**
Every new client requires setup in QuickBooks, Xero, or your practice management system,
chart of accounts configuration, historical data import, and a first-month reconciliation.
For a firm onboarding 3–5 new clients per month, this consumes 4–6 hours per client —
**12–30 hours monthly** of setup work that a staff accountant should not be doing.

### The Advisory Gap

The most profitable work any accounting firm does — tax planning, CFO advisory,
strategic financial guidance — bills at $200–$500/hour. The data-entry work bills at
$50–$100/hour. Firms that fail to automate the low-margin work never create capacity
for the high-margin work. They are stuck in the $50/hour lane forever.

## 2. The Proposed Solution

We deploy an AI engineering team connected to your practice management system, your
clients' accounting software, and your document portal. Every workflow is built to
your firm's categorization rules, not a generic template.

### What Gets Built (Initial Engagement)

**Workflow 1 — Transaction Categorization + Reconciliation Automation**
Your AI team connects to your clients' QuickBooks or Xero accounts, pulls transactions
nightly, categorizes them using your firm's chart of accounts rules, matches bank
feed transactions against the ledger, and flags exceptions for staff review. Staff
opens the month and sees: 430 transactions categorized, 7 exceptions flagged, 2 hours
of their time saved before they touch anything.

*Saves: 4–8 hours per client per month · At 20 clients: 80–160 hours/month recovered*

**Workflow 2 — Document Collection Automation**
Before every quarterly close, tax deadline, or audit prep window, your AI team sends
a sequenced, personalized document request to each client — listing exactly what is
missing, what format is needed, and which deadline it supports. It follows up automatically
at 72-hour intervals, escalates to a firm partner cc: if no response after 5 days, and
closes the request when all documents are received.

*Eliminates: 5 hours/week of partner/staff time on manual follow-up during peak season*

**Workflow 3 (Month 2 expansion) — New Client Setup Automation**
When a new client is signed, your AI team creates the QuickBooks/Xero company file,
imports historical data, configures the chart of accounts to your firm's standard,
and generates the welcome packet and first-month reconciliation checklist —
before your staff touches the file.

*Reduces: 4–6 hours per new client onboarding to under 30 minutes of staff review*
