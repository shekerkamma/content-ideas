## Portfolio thesis

Real-estate AI is strongest when pitched as workflow automation across connected operating surfaces rather than as a generic assistant:

- brokerage revenue workflows
- multifamily leasing and resident communications
- commission and payout back office
- maintenance and service operations
- title, escrow, and closing coordination

Across all five lanes, the pattern is consistent:

- existing industry software proves budget and buyer awareness
- the AI wedge is a workflow bottleneck, not abstract intelligence
- the best starting point is assistive orchestration in the current system of record
- OpenHands is the reusable internal execution layer for multi-agent logic, MCP-connected tools, and repeatable runs

## Use-case selection logic

### 1. Brokerage lead routing and transaction coordination

- Best for: brokerages and revenue teams
- Outcome: faster speed-to-lead, better routing, cleaner handoffs

### 2. Multifamily leasing and resident communication

- Best for: leasing and community operations
- Outcome: faster prospect response, smoother renewals, better resident messaging

### 3. Commission and payout automation

- Best for: brokerage ops and finance
- Outcome: fewer payout exceptions, faster processing, cleaner auditability

### 4. Service-request and maintenance automation

- Best for: property operations
- Outcome: faster triage, better queue visibility, stronger resident updates

### 5. Title, escrow, and closing workflow automation

- Best for: title, escrow, and closing teams
- Outcome: cleaner file setup, fewer missing items, better closing readiness
