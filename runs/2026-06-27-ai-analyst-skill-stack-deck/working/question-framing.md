## User Question

## Corrected Interpretation

The prior framing over-weighted "implementation blueprints." That was the wrong lead. The core artifact is the whole operating system:

- 10 skills that replace the traditional dev-shop journey from diagnostic through post-launch iteration.
- Human operator gates that explain where raw AI output fails and where expert judgment adds value.
- 52 reviewed opportunities as evidence that the operating system can be applied across a broad market surface.

## Business Question

How should we present the 10-skill operator stack as a market-facing capability system for 30-day agentic MVPs, using the 52 reviewed opportunities as proof of breadth?

## Audience

- Founder/operator selling or packaging the capability.
- Prospective buyers who are comparing slow traditional dev-shop discovery against AI-native execution.
- Partners or collaborators who need to understand the repeatable method, not just individual use cases.

## Decision the Artifact Must Support

Whether the opportunity portfolio should be marketed as:

1. A collection of one-off use-case blueprints, or
2. A repeatable operator system that compresses diagnostic, scoping, architecture, QA, market scan, deployment, and iteration into a structured 30-day MVP motion.

Recommended answer: position it as the operator system first. The use cases prove the system.

- Do not treat implementation architecture as the whole story.
