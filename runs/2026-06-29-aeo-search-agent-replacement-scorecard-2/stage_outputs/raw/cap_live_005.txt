# AI Shopping/Sales Consultant Tech Stack & Workflows

## Common Software Solutions

### **Conversational AI Platforms**
- **Zendesk AI** - integrates with existing helpdesk
- **Intercom** - AI chatbots with human handoff
- **Drift/Qualified** - B2B sales chat with routing
- **Ada/Kustomer** - customer service automation
- **Salesforce Einstein** - CRM-integrated AI

### **Custom-Built Stack** (more common for sophisticated use cases)
```
Frontend: Widget/Chat UI
    ↓
LLM Layer: GPT-4, Claude, or fine-tuned models
    ↓
Knowledge Base: Vector DB (Pinecone, Weaviate)
    ↓
Integrations: Product catalog, CRM, inventory APIs
    ↓
Monitoring: LangSmith, Helicone, custom analytics
```

### **Workflow Tools**
- **Zapier/Make** - connect AI to existing systems
- **LangChain/LlamaIndex** - orchestration frameworks
- **Botpress/Voiceflow** - visual bot builders

## Where AI Agents Work Well ✅

### **High-Value Zones**
1. **Product Discovery & Filtering**
   - "Find me winter boots under $150 with good traction"
   - Handles 20-50 filtering dimensions better than traditional search

2. **FAQ & Simple Troubleshooting**
   - Shipping policies, return windows, sizing guides
   - 70-80% resolution rate for common questions

3. **Lead Qualification (B2B)**
   - Gathering requirements before human sales involvement
   - Scheduling demos, routing to right rep

4. **Upsell/Cross-sell Suggestions**
   - "Customers also bought..." with conversational context
   - Works when product catalog is well-structured

5. **After-Hours Coverage**
   - Handling inquiries when humans aren't available
   - Capturing leads for follow-up

## Where AI Agents Struggle ❌

### **Realistic Limitations**

1. **Complex Negotiations**
   - Custom pricing, bulk discounts, contract terms
   - **Handoff to human essential**

2. **Emotional/Sensitive Issues**
   - Complaints, defective products, billing disputes
   - Customers want human empathy, not algorithms

3. **High-Stakes Purchases**
   - Enterprise software, cars, medical devices
   - Trust factor still requires human relationship

4. **Nuanced Product Advice**
   - "Which camera is best for wildlife photography in low light?"
   - Requires deep expertise AI often lacks or hallucinates

5. **Unstructured Data**
   - Product info scattered across PDFs, images, videos
   - Garbage in = garbage out

6. **Return/Refund Processing**
   - Requires system access and exception handling
   - Legal/financial implications need human oversight

## Realistic Hybrid Model

Most successful teams use this approach:

```
1. AI handles initial contact (80% of volume)
   ↓
2. AI detects complexity/frustration signals
   ↓
3. Seamless handoff to human with full context
   ↓
4. Human resolves, AI learns from interaction
```

### **Key Metrics Teams Track**
- **Containment rate**: % resolved without human (target: 40-70%)
- **Handoff smoothness**: Context preservation score
- **CSAT differential**: AI vs human satisfaction
- **Cost per interaction**: Should be 5-10x cheaper for AI
- **Hallucination rate**: Wrong info given (must be <2%)

## Emerging Patterns (2024-2025)

- **Voice AI** gaining traction for phone sales (Bland AI, Retell)
- **Visual search integration** (upload photo → find similar products)
- **Proactive outreach** (AI initiating conversations based on browsing)
- **Multi-agent systems** (specialist bots for different departments)

## Bottom Line

AI works as a **first-line triage and simple resolution tool**, not a complete human replacement. Teams that succeed treat it as an *assistant to both customers and human reps*, with clear escalation paths and continuous monitoring for accuracy.
