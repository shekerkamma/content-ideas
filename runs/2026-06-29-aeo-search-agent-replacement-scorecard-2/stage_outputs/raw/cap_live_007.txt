# Typical Support Software & Workflows

## Common Tools Teams Use

**Helpdesk Platforms:**
- Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk for ticket management
- Built-in workflows for routing, assignment, SLAs
- Knowledge bases and canned responses

**Live Chat:**
- Drift, Zendesk Chat, Intercom Messenger
- Real-time conversations, often blending human + bot

**Messaging Integration:**
- WhatsApp Business, Facebook Messenger
- Slack/Teams for internal support

**Workflow Typically:**
1. Customer contacts via chat/email/form
2. Bot handles initial triage or simple questions
3. Escalates to human agent when needed
4. Agent uses internal tools/docs to resolve
5. Ticket closed, feedback collected

---

# Where AI Agents Are **Actually Useful**

## ✅ High Success Areas

**Deflecting repetitive questions:**
- "What's my order status?" → lookup + respond
- "How do I reset my password?" → guide through steps
- FAQs that don't require judgment

**Tier-0 triage:**
- Collecting initial context before human handoff
- Routing to right department
- Gathering account details, screenshots

**After-hours coverage:**
- Basic help when humans aren't available
- Setting expectations about response times

**Internal knowledge retrieval:**
- Helping *agents* find answers quickly from docs
- Suggesting relevant help articles mid-conversation

---

# Where AI Agents **Struggle or Fail**

## ❌ Realistic Limitations

**Anything requiring empathy/judgment:**
- Angry customers needing to vent
- Complex billing disputes
- "This ruined my wedding" scenarios
- Nuanced policy exceptions

**Multi-step problem-solving:**
- Debugging technical issues requiring back-and-forth
- Cases needing creativity or connecting dots across systems
- When the answer isn't in the documentation

**Account actions with risk:**
- Refunds, cancellations, data deletion
- Most companies keep humans in the loop here for liability

**When the bot becomes the problem:**
- Customer has already tried help docs
- They're frustrated by automated responses
- "Just let me talk to a human" situations

---

# The Pragmatic Hybrid Approach

Most successful teams use:

**AI for:**
- Intent detection and routing
- Suggesting responses to agents
- Handling straightforward transactional queries

**Humans for:**
- Complex cases
- Emotional situations
- Edge cases and escalations
- Building relationships with high-value customers

**The handoff is critical:**
- Clear escalation triggers
- Context preservation (don't make customers repeat themselves)
- Easy escape hatch ("talk to human")

---

# Actual ROI Reality Check

Teams see value when AI:
- Reduces resolution time for simple issues (30-40% deflection realistic)
- Helps agents work faster (not replacing them entirely)
- Provides 24/7 basic coverage

Teams get frustrated when:
- Over-promising automation ("we'll replace 80% of tickets!")
- Bot can't actually *do* things, just talks about them
- No investment in keeping knowledge base current
- Metrics focus on deflection over customer satisfaction

The unsexy truth: AI agents work best as **efficiency multipliers for humans**, not replacements.
