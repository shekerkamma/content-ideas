# Deck brief

## Audience

Designers, operators, and AI builders who want to understand and reproduce the Impeccable 4.0 workflow.

## Decision

Understand the sequence, visual controls, and review loop demonstrated by Impeccable 4.0.

## Narrative promise

Recover the demonstrated visual sequence and attach the explanation needed to understand each screen without replaying the video.

## Voice and tone

Faithful, visual-first, concise, and instructional.

## Anti-references

A consulting deck about the video; small evidence thumbnails; generic feature cards; new claims detached from the demonstrated screen.

## Evidence and editability

Hybrid editable: exact source screens are dominant raster evidence; titles, context bands, and footers are native PowerPoint elements.

## Success criteria

Every selected visual state remains dominant, each has attached spoken context, sequence is preserved, and no replacement narrative displaces the demonstration.
