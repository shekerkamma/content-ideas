### #1 — Real Estate Brokerages (33/35 — GO)

```
VERTICAL SCORECARD: Real Estate Brokerages
══════════════════════════════════════

  Dimension               Score   Signal
  ──────────────────────  ─────   ──────────────────────────────────────────────
  Intelligence Ratio      5/5     CMA, listing copy, drip campaigns, lead scoring
                                  are 90%+ pattern recognition — almost no novel judgment
  Outsourcing Readiness   5/5     Transaction coordinators and marketing VAs already
                                  standard outsource; TCs bill $300-500/transaction
  TAM Accessibility       5/5     106K brokerages, ~$100B commissions; spend on
                                  Dotloop/BoomTown proves willingness to pay
  Data Moat Potential     5/5     MLS + transaction history + buyer behavior =
                                  predictive pricing / lead scoring moat
  Regulatory Friction     4/5     NAR rules exist but don't block AI; no HIPAA
                                  equivalent; much lower friction than healthcare/legal
  Incumbent Vulnerability 5/5     Dotloop, Skyslope, BoomTown are expensive,
                                  fragmented, and despised by agents for UX
  Mirage PMF Risk         4/5     Realtors adopt tools fast when they see lead/deal
                                  flow impact; commission check size justifies spend
  ──────────────────────  ─────
  COMPOSITE               33/35

  VERDICT: GO
  Rationale: Highest scoring vertical across all 7 dimensions. The combination of high
  intelligence ratio, established outsourcing culture, fragmented incumbents, and
  clear ROI link to commission revenue makes this the strongest niche to own first.
  Every real estate office has the same 5-6 problems and will pay to solve them.

  Copilot → Autopilot Path:
  Week 1-4: Co-pilot (agent drafts listing descriptions, agent reviews leads)
  Month 2-3: Assisted autopilot (agent manages drip campaigns, auto-qualifies leads)
  Month 4+: Full autopilot (agent handles intake → CMA → listing → follow-up loop)

  Key Risk: NAR/MLS data access — if brokerages can't feed live MLS data to agents,
  the data moat and CMA capability are weaker.
```
