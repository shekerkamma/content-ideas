### For real estate agents and small brokerages

**"I'm paying my TC $2,000 a month and half the time I still have to chase people myself."**

That's the deal most agents are stuck in. A human TC handles your paperwork — until they don't. Missed contingency dates, documents that never got sent, title companies waiting on you to chase your own client.

---

## What it does

Your Transaction Coordinator Bot manages every deal from executed contract to close — without you touching it.

- Reads the purchase agreement and extracts every deadline automatically
- Adds all contingency dates to Google Calendar with reminders for every party
- Sends daily deal status emails to buyer, seller, title, and lender
- Chases missing documents with automated follow-up sequences
- Closes the file and archives everything when the last doc is signed

You get notified when something actually needs your attention. Everything else runs without you.

---

## How it works

1. You (or your assistant) upload the signed purchase agreement
2. The bot reads it, extracts dates, and opens the deal file
3. All parties receive a welcome email with their deadline calendar
4. Daily automated check-ins keep everyone moving — the bot chases, you supervise
5. When the last document arrives, the file closes and you get a completion summary
