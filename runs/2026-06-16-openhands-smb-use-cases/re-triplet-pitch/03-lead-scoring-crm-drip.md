## What it does

Your Lead Scoring System reads your contact list, scores every contact by transaction readiness, and runs a personalized follow-up sequence for the ones most likely to move in the next 90 days.

- Imports your contacts from any CRM, spreadsheet, or phone export
- Scores each contact on 12 signals: recency of contact, life events, market behavior, past transaction history
- Surfaces your top 20 "most likely to transact now" every Monday morning
- Runs automated drip sequences calibrated to where each contact is in their journey
- Alerts you when a lead opens an email, clicks a listing, or fills out a form — that's your call signal

You stop guessing and start calling the right people at the right time.

## How it works

1. We import your contact list (CSV, Google Contacts, Follow Up Boss, or a simple spreadsheet)
2. The system scores every contact based on your interaction history and market signals
3. Every Monday you get a ranked list: "These 20 people are most likely to transact this quarter"
4. Each contact enters a drip sequence matched to their stage (watching, ready, referred, past client)
5. When a lead takes a hot action (email open, listing click, form fill), you get an immediate text alert
