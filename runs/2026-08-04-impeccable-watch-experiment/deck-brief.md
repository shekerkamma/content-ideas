# Deck brief

## Audience

Design leaders, AI builders, and client-facing teams evaluating Impeccable 4.0.

## Decision

Understand the complete design workflow and decide whether to adopt its world-selection, visual-comparison, detection, and design-record practices.

## Narrative promise

Recreate the demonstration visually, preserve the product screens and sequence, and attach only the context needed to understand each state without playing the video.

## Voice and tone

Confident, visual-first, editorial, and explanatory.

## Anti-references

Generic consulting cards, screenshot thumbnails, decorative gradients, invented product states, presenter imagery, and commentary detached from the demonstrated screen.

## Evidence and editability

Hybrid editable: exact product screens remain authentic raster evidence; titles, annotations, sequence map, benchmark, and footers are native PowerPoint objects.

## Success criteria

All 17 selected states are visible and legible; annotations replace only the presenter-obscured region; the Impeccable treatment beats baseline without lowering fidelity, legibility, or authenticity; contracts and lint pass with documented waivers; PowerPoint renders all 19 slides without collisions, clipping, or missing media.
