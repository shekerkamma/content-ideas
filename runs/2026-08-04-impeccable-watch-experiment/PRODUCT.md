# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Presentation authors and client-facing teams reconstructing a video's visual explanation as standalone slides.

## Product Purpose

Turn Watch's captured frames and timestamped context into a presentation that preserves the video's meaning while testing whether committed design templates materially improve how recovered visual assets are cropped, framed, prioritized, annotated, and sequenced.

## Positioning

The experiment compares the same visual/context pairs and the same recovered assets across a baseline template and an Impeccable-directed treatment; improvement must be attributable to design treatment rather than changed content or substituted imagery.

## Operating Context

The input is a Watch work directory containing a public demonstration video, frames, and transcript. The output is a PowerPoint deck plus baseline and improved representative treatments and an asset benchmark.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Watch is the sole authority for frames, context, sequence, and claims.
- Video-to-deck is not part of the pipeline.
- Impeccable may improve template and asset treatment but may not change source meaning.
- Exact product screens remain authentic extracted evidence.
- Generated imagery cannot replace source assets, UI, diagrams, people, logos, or results.
- Final slide titles and context remain editable.

## Product Principles

- Keep recovered visual assets dominant.
- Bind every design decision to the corresponding spoken context.
- Compare treatments using identical content and evidence.
- Improve clarity and distinctiveness without sacrificing fidelity.
- Treat real-render legibility as part of design quality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable presentation-scale type, high contrast, meaningful reading order, and concise native alt text for embedded evidence.
