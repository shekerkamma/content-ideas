# Impeccable direction

## Assigned grounded direction

**The conservation proof table** — each recovered screen is treated like an original plate under
inspection. The asset occupies the illuminated field; concise marginal notes attach the spoken
context; registration marks and proof labels distinguish baseline, alternate, and chosen states.

This was candidate six in the grounded list assigned by Impeccable's direction seed. It fits the
experiment because the same source plate can be compared across treatments without confusing new
content for design improvement.

## System grammar

- Warm proof-paper ground, charcoal type, restrained vermilion registration accents.
- Large asset field with disciplined crops; no screenshot thumbnails.
- Marginal annotation rather than a competing prose panel.
- One proof number and one context note per state.
- Alternating full-bleed, paired-proof, and detail-magnification archetypes.
- Native titles and notes; authentic recovered assets remain unmodified evidence except disclosed
  presenter-overlay masking.

## Challengers considered

- **Labanotation score:** strong temporal sequencing, but its bilateral notation competes with
  rectangular product screens.
- **Shibuya sleeve with obi band:** strong density contrast and a useful annotation band; retained
  as an alternate treatment pattern.
- **Orizuru fold sequence:** excellent state-to-state continuity, but too literal for software
  screenshots and risks decorative metaphor.

## Benchmark hypothesis

The conservation proof table should improve asset prominence, crop/focus, context binding,
hierarchy, distinctiveness, and cross-slide coherence while matching baseline fidelity,
legibility, authenticity, and editability.
