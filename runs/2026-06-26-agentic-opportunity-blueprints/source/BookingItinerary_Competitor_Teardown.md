# Competitor Teardown: Booking / Itinerary Tools

**Target SaaS Incumbents:** SAP Concur, Navan (TripActions), TravelPerk, Travefy.

**Company Overview:** 
Corporate travel and expense (T&E) and itinerary building tools that manage policy enforcement, bookings, and expense reporting.

**Product Teardown & Pricing:**
*   **SAP Concur:** Custom enterprise pricing, opaque and module-based.
*   **TravelPerk:** Transactional / Subscription models.
*   **Travefy:** Flat-rate subscription.

**Where They Are Strong:**
Policy enforcement, HRIS/ERP integrations, and handling crisis support (flight cancellations).

**Where They Are Weak (Onboarding Friction):**
Concur is notorious for its "old school," clunky UI and steep learning curve requiring massive configuration. TravelPerk is often seen as a "pointless middleman" causing friction when changing plans. Users complain of opaque pricing, discrepancies during checkout, and over-complicated booking flows.

**Disruptive Strategy:**
*   **Direct Threats:** Navan and TravelPerk.
*   **Table Stakes:** Flight/hotel inventory API access, policy guardrails.
*   **What NOT to do:** Do not build a massive, clunky dashboard for users to click through 10 pages to book a flight.
*   **The Agentic Wedge:** *Invisible Orchestration.* The agent manages bookings via chat or email. A user says "Book me for the London conference next week," and the agent checks their calendar, references company policy, and executes the booking via APIs natively, completely collapsing the heavy portal UI.
