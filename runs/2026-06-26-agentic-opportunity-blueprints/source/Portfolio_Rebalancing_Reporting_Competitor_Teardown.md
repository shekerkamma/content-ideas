# Disruptive Competitor Teardown: Portfolio Rebalancing Reporting (Use Case 40)

## 1. Company Overview & Legacy Competitors
**Target Incumbents:** Envestnet (Tamarac), Orion Advisor Tech, SS&C Black Diamond, Morningstar Office, Addepar.
These are the dominant wealth management platforms for RIAs, attempting to be "all-in-one" solutions but failing at personalized, scalable reporting.

## 2. Product Teardown (Orion & Black Diamond)
*   **Top 3 Features:** Multi-custodial data aggregation, customizable performance reporting, portfolio rebalancing (tax-loss harvesting).
*   **Pricing:** Expensive tiered AUM pricing or high per-account fees. Aggressive, sudden price hikes upon contract renewal are a major Reddit complaint.
*   **Onboarding Friction:** Steep learning curves. Users complain about "clunky" interfaces, terrible customer support (passed around to junior reps), and the pain of manually setting up custom report templates.

## 3. Where They Are Strong
*   **Stickiness:** Creating polished, professional deliverables. Deep API ecosystems with custodians (Schwab, Fidelity).

## 4. Where They Are Weak
*   **Manual Workflows:** The "all-in-one" curse means modules are mediocre. Handling alternative investments (alts) causes massive data discrepancies. Advisors still spend hours manually writing commentary to explain *why* a portfolio performed a certain way.

## 5. The Disruptive Strategy
*   **Direct Threats:** Orion Advisor Tech, Envestnet Tamarac.
*   **Table Stakes:** Accurate multi-custodial data ingestion, standard performance metrics (TWR/IRR).
*   **What We Deliberately NOT Do:** Do NOT build another complex portfolio accounting ledger. We are the intelligence and synthesis layer.
*   **The Agentic Wedge (3 Gaps to Exploit):**
    1.  **Replace Charts with Narrative:** Move beyond static PDF charts. Generate highly personalized, natural-language performance narratives for HNW clients based on raw data.
    2.  **Solve the Alts Discrepancy:** Intelligently ingest and reconcile unstructured alternative asset statements (K-1s, capital calls) that currently break Orion's data feeds.
    3.  **Zero-Click Customization:** Eliminate the clunky setup process by using conversational AI to generate custom ad-hoc reports instantly.
