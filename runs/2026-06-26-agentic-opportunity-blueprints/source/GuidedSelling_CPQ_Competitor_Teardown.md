# Competitor Teardown: Guided Selling / CPQ

**Target SaaS Incumbents:** Salesforce CPQ (Revenue Cloud), DealHub, Conga (Apttus), Oracle CPQ.

**Company Overview:** 
Enterprise Configure, Price, Quote (CPQ) tools are designed to handle complex pricing matrices and massive product catalogs for sales teams.

**Product Teardown & Pricing:**
*   **Salesforce CPQ:** End-of-sale for new customers, forcing migrations to Revenue Cloud. Requires massive SI (Systems Integrator) contracts.
*   **Conga / Oracle CPQ:** Custom, expensive enterprise pricing requiring minimum 3-year contracts.
*   **DealHub:** Per-user pricing, scaling expensively for large orgs.

**Where They Are Strong:**
Handling extreme pricing complexity, integration into the core CRM, and enforcing strict pricing governance.

**Where They Are Weak (Onboarding Friction):**
Incredibly high implementation complexity. Systems become over-architected, requiring dedicated administrators. Users complain of slow quote calculations and high friction, often resulting in sales reps abandoning the tool to "take their quoting offline in spreadsheets."

**Disruptive Strategy:**
*   **Direct Threats:** DealHub and Salesforce Revenue Cloud.
*   **Table Stakes:** accurate pricing guardrails, CRM integration, PDF generation.
*   **What NOT to do:** Do not build a massive visual rule-builder that requires an administrator to maintain.
*   **The Agentic Wedge:** *Conversational Quoting.* We bypass the months-long SI implementation. Reps simply describe the deal context in natural language, and the AI agent instantly calculates and generates the quote by dynamically pulling from the pricing logic, eliminating the need for complex, rigid UI flows.
