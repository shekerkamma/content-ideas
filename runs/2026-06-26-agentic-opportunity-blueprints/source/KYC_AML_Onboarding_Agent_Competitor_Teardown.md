# Disruptive Competitor Teardown: KYC/AML Onboarding Agent (Use Case 38)

## 1. Company Overview & Legacy Competitors
**Target Incumbents:** Fenergo, NICE Actimize, Thomson Reuters (CLEAR), LexisNexis (Bridger Insight), Dow Jones Risk & Compliance, Alessa by Caseware, Onfido, Jumio.
These platforms dominate institutional KYC/AML but rely on bloated, rules-based engines that demand massive teams of human analysts to review alerts.

## 2. Product Teardown (Fenergo & NICE Actimize)
*   **Top 3 Features:** Multi-jurisdictional rules engine, native core banking API connectors, enterprise case management & audit trails.
*   **Pricing:** Custom enterprise-negotiated. Notoriously opaque. Often requires $50,000–$100,000+ in initial implementation/onboarding fees, followed by high volume/per-seat licensing.
*   **Onboarding Friction:** Implementation cycles average 6-9 months. The system generates massive volumes of false positives, forcing banks to hire armies of "human middleware" (analysts) simply to clear alerts.

## 3. Where They Are Strong
*   **Stickiness:** Deep integration into the core banking tech stack and a perceived "safety net" for enterprise compliance officers.

## 4. Where They Are Weak
*   **Manual Workflows:** Highly rigid rules engines cannot parse nuanced, unstructured data (like complex corporate registry PDFs). Users complain that the UI is bloated and time-to-value is incredibly slow.

## 5. The Disruptive Strategy
*   **Direct Threats:** Fenergo, NICE Actimize.
*   **Table Stakes:** Sanctions/PEP screening, Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) unwrapping, and immutable audit logs.
*   **What We Deliberately NOT Do:** Do NOT attempt to replace the bank's core database or the hardware ID scanners. We sit on top as the intelligent cognitive layer.
*   **The Agentic Wedge (3 Gaps to Exploit):**
    1.  **Eliminate the $100k/9-month Setup:** Use AI to instantly configure onboarding rules dynamically based on regulatory text, rather than manual hardcoding.
    2.  **Eradicate "Human Middleware":** Auto-clear 80% of false positives by contextualizing alerts natively, rather than just flagging them.
    3.  **Unstructured Synthesis:** Read and extract entities from messy corporate registries, trust documents, and watchlists instantly, bypassing the manual googling and data entry analysts currently perform.
