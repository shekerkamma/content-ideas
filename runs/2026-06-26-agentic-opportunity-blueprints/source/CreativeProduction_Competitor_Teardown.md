# Creative Production Agent - Competitor Teardown

## 1. Legacy Incumbents
- Adobe Creative Cloud (Illustrator, InDesign)
- Canva Enterprise
- Figma
- Sketch
- Bynder (Print & Creative modules)

## 2. Company Overview & Product Teardown
- **Positioning:** Digital canvas and visual editing platforms for design professionals and teams.
- **Pricing:** Heavy per-seat pricing. Adobe CC charges ~$90/month per user. Canva Enterprise adds expensive per-seat licenses at scale.
- **Onboarding Friction:** Steep learning curves for Adobe; Canva is simpler but becomes a chaotic mess of folders over time. Users complain about spending "70% of their time resizing" graphics. Reddit/G2 reviews show designers experiencing intense burnout ("two designers handed in notice") simply from doing manual resizing, versioning, and formatting rather than actual creative work.

## 3. Where They Are Strong
- **Deep Control:** Pixel-perfect control over assets (Adobe).
- **Ubiquity:** Everyone knows how to use them. They act as the default system of record for design source files.

## 4. Where They Are Weak
- **Human Middleware Requirement:** They require a human to manually click, resize, export, and route every single asset variation. 
- **Feature Bloat:** Massive UI bloat with 90% of features completely unused by the average marketer.

## 5. Disruptive Strategy
- **Direct Threat:** Canva Enterprise and Adobe Express.
- **Table Stakes:** Must adhere to strict brand guidelines (colors, fonts, logo placement, margins).
- **What NOT to do:** Do not build a new canvas UI. We don't want to compete on pixel editing tools.
- **Agentic Wedge:** 
  1. **Autonomous Resizing & Formatting:** The agent automatically takes a core asset and generates the 50 required variations (IG, LinkedIn, email banner) instantly.
  2. **No Per-Seat Pricing:** We charge for output/campaigns, destroying the legacy per-seat software tax.
  3. **Zero-Click Workflow:** Marketers prompt the agent via Slack, and it delivers the assets without anyone opening a heavy design app.
