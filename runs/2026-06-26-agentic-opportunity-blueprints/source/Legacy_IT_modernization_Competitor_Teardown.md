# Disruptive Competitor Teardown: Legacy-IT Modernization (Use Case 16)

## OSINT Source Map & Methodology
- **Sources Scraped:** Reddit (r/SAP, r/ERP), G2 Reviews, Gartner Peer Insights.
- **Search Queries:** "SAP S/4HANA migration experience", "Legacy modernization consulting costs".
- **Methodology:** We analyzed the ecosystem of SAP consulting giants and iPaaS tools (e.g., Boomi, Signavio) that enterprise companies hire to modernize legacy ERPs.

## Company Overview: Legacy Incumbents
1. **Big 4 Consultancies (Accenture, PwC, Deloitte):** Massive services firms driving ERP transformations.
2. **SAP Signavio:** Business process transformation and mining.
3. **Boomi (iPaaS):** Legacy system integration.
4. **SNP Group:** Data transformation software for SAP.
5. **Panaya:** ERP change intelligence.

## Product Teardown
- **Top 3 Features:** Process mining, fit-to-standard mapping, legacy data integration.
- **Pricing Tiers:** Multi-million dollar consulting engagements. Software components (Signavio, Boomi) use complex, consumption-based or node-based pricing hidden behind sales.
- **Onboarding Friction:** Takes 6+ months just to complete "readiness assessments." Customers on Reddit complain about being locked into multi-year migration roadmaps that stall out due to custom ABAP debt.

## Where They Are Strong
- **Risk Mitigation:** Nobody gets fired for hiring Accenture to migrate SAP.
- **Deep Enterprise Context:** They understand the tangled web of Fortune 500 business processes.

## Where They Are Weak
- **Human Middleware:** They require armies of expensive consultants to manually map COBOL/ABAP to modern APIs.
- **Time to Value:** Years before the modernization shows ROI.

## Disruptive Strategy
- **Top 2 Direct Threats:** Traditional IT Consulting Firms and heavy iPaaS tools like Boomi.
- **Table Stakes:** High security, data governance, and SAP ecosystem compatibility.
- **What We Must NOT Do:** We must not try to replace SAP as the system of record.
- **3 Specific Gaps our Agent Exploits:**
  1. **NL to Legacy:** Instead of full migrations, our agent puts a natural-language interface over legacy systems, extracting value immediately without migration.
  2. **Automated Code Translation:** Automatically translates legacy business logic (ABAP/COBOL) into modern microservices, eliminating thousands of billable consulting hours.
  3. **Instant Process Mapping:** Agentic discovery of custom workflows instead of 6-month manual assessments.
