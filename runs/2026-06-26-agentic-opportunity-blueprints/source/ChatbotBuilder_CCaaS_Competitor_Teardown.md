# Competitor Teardown: Chatbot / CCaaS Bots

**Target SaaS Incumbents:** Intercom Fin, Ada, Genesys, LivePerson.

**Company Overview:** 
Contact Center as a Service (CCaaS) and enterprise bot builders focused on deflecting volume before it hits a human agent.

**Product Teardown & Pricing:**
*   **Intercom Fin:** $0.99 per resolution. (Paradoxically, better bots lead to "billing shock").
*   **Ada / Genesys / LivePerson:** Highly custom, opaque enterprise contracts requiring deep integrations.

**Where They Are Strong:**
High-volume deflection, deep routing rules, and enterprise-grade SLA compliance.

**Where They Are Weak (Onboarding Friction):**
Reddit threads are full of complaints about the "Repetition Trap"—bots failing to pass context to humans, forcing customers to repeat themselves. They have a massive setup friction: building intent maps and decision trees takes months. Users hate the "escalation resistance" where bots trap them in loops.

**Disruptive Strategy:**
*   **Direct Threats:** Ada and Intercom Fin.
*   **Table Stakes:** Handoff to human agents, integration with existing KB/Helpdesk.
*   **What NOT to do:** Do not build a visual drag-and-drop intent flow builder.
*   **The Agentic Wedge:** *Zero-Setup Contextual Handoff.* The legacy model requires manual intent mapping. Our agent works instantly by ingesting historical transcripts, holds conversational state perfectly, and executes a flawless, context-rich handoff to human agents when it detects frustration, destroying the multi-month consultative setup model.
