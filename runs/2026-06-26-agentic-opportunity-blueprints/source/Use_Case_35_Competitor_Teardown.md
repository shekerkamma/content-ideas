# Maintenance Ticket Orchestration - Competitor Teardown
## OSINT Source Map & Methodology
- **Sources Mined**: r/PropertyManagement
- **Query Strategy**: Targeted legacy incumbents (AppFolio, Buildium, Property Meld) combined with 'maintenance ticket complaints', 'pricing'.
- **Rationale**: To uncover operational bottlenecks where existing software fails to eliminate human dispatching and vendor coordination.

## 1. Competitor Overview
- **AppFolio**: Leading cloud-based property management software for medium to large portfolios (50+ units).
- **Buildium**: Popular PMS for smaller to mid-sized portfolios, offering built-in work order tracking.
- **Property Meld**: Specialized, dedicated maintenance tracking platform that integrates with PMS systems.

## 2. Product Teardown
- **Top 3 Features**: Tenant portal requests, vendor dispatching, and maintenance contact centers.
- **Pricing Tiers**: AppFolio starts around $280/month (minimums apply). Buildium starts at $62/month + $1.10/unit for call centers. Property Meld charges $1.60/unit/month.
- **Onboarding Friction**: High barrier for vendor adoption. Many 'old-school' vendors refuse to use the complex software portals, breaking the automated loop.

## 3. Where They Are Strong
- **Stickiness**: Embedded deeply in the tenant and owner portals. Once a tenant is trained to use the app, the PMS owns the relationship.

## 4. Where They Are Weak
- **Human Middleware Requirement**: Requests often linger when vendors don't update the system. The software requires property managers to manually chase vendors.
- **Complexity**: AppFolio's maintenance modules are frequently described on Reddit as 'over-complicated,' especially the integrated inspection systems.

## 5. The Disruptive Strategy
- **Top 2 Direct Threats**: AppFolio (for its scale) and Property Meld (for its specialized focus).
- **Table Stakes**: Multi-channel intake (SMS, email, portal) and basic vendor dispatch.
- **What We Must NOT Do**: Do not force vendors to download a new app or log into a portal.
- **The 3 Specific Gaps (Our Agentic Wedge)**:
  1. **Vendor-Native Communication**: Our agent interacts with old-school vendors via plain text/SMS (acting like a human dispatcher), entirely bypassing portal adoption friction.
  2. **Automated Triage & Diagnosis**: Use multimodal AI to analyze tenant photos and automatically categorize the issue before a human manager even sees it.
  3. **Self-Healing Workflows**: The agent autonomously follows up with unresponsive vendors and updates the PMS, eliminating the 'falling through the cracks' complaint.
