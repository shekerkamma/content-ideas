# Competitor Teardown: Procurement & Spend Management (Use Case 26)

## 1. Company Overview
**Top Legacy Incumbents:** SAP Ariba, Coupa
**Primary Positioning:** Enterprise-grade procurement, spend management, and supplier networks.

## 2. Product Teardown
- **Top 3 Features:** P2P (Procure-to-Pay) automation, supplier risk management, spend analytics.
- **Pricing:** Opaque, enterprise-only pricing. Frequently involves six-figure upfront implementation fees and long deployment timelines (months to years). No transparent unbundled pricing.
- **Onboarding Friction:** High. Supplier onboarding is notoriously painful, involving endless email chains to collect tax forms and banking details despite using an "enterprise" tool.

## 3. Where They Are Strong
- Deep integration with monolithic ERPs.
- Massive existing supplier networks (e.g., Ariba Network).
- Established as the "System of Record" for Fortune 500 companies.

## 4. Where They Are Weak (The "Human Middleware" Problem)
- **Manual Interventions:** When integrations fail between Ariba/Coupa and internal ERPs, employees act as "human middleware" to manually move data and reconcile invoices.
- **UI/UX Friction:** Ariba is widely cited on Reddit/G2 as clunky and unintuitive. Coupa is better but remains rigid and prone to "demo fatigue."
- **Poor Flexibility:** Forcing unique business processes into rigid pre-built software workflows causes massive friction.

## 5. Disruptive Strategy (Our Agentic Wedge)
- **Top 2 Direct Threats:** SAP Ariba, Coupa.
- **Table Stakes:** PO generation, invoice matching, vendor credentialing.
- **What We Must NOT Do:** Do NOT build a new financial System of Record or ERP. We sit on top of their existing messy databases.
- **3 Specific Gaps We Exploit:**
  1. *Opaque, 6-figure setup vs. Usage-based Agentic scaling.*
  2. *Months-long, consultant-heavy implementation vs. Instant integration via natural language.*
  3. *"Human middleware" data entry vs. Autonomous AI vendor chasing and automated document extraction.*
