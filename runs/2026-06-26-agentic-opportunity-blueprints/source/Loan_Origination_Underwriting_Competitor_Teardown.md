# Disruptive Competitor Teardown: Loan Origination Underwriting (Use Case 39)

## 1. Company Overview & Legacy Competitors
**Target Incumbents:** ICE Mortgage Technology (Encompass), Black Knight (Empower), Finastra (Fusion Mortgagebot), Fiserv (Velocity), Blend.
These legacy Loan Origination Systems (LOS) hold the mortgage industry hostage with archaic interfaces and expensive per-loan fees.

## 2. Product Teardown (Encompass & Empower)
*   **Top 3 Features:** End-to-end loan tracking, massive compliance & regulatory engines, document vaults.
*   **Pricing:** Heavily bundled, often equating to $300–$500 per closed loan for enterprise shops, or expensive per-seat licenses.
*   **Onboarding Friction:** Universally decried by Reddit users as having a "Windows 95 era" UI. Extremely clunky and slow, requiring dedicated, highly paid administrative teams just to keep the software running.

## 3. Where They Are Strong
*   **Stickiness:** They act as the ultimate system of record. Mortgages cannot be sold to Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac without the compliance trails these systems generate.

## 4. Where They Are Weak
*   **Manual Workflows:** Despite the high cost, extracting data from W-2s and tax returns still requires "stare and compare" manual entry. Moving files between loan officers, processors, and underwriters is incredibly frictionless and manual.

## 5. The Disruptive Strategy
*   **Direct Threats:** Encompass (ICE), Finastra.
*   **Table Stakes:** Secure document vaulting, standard Fannie/Freddie compliance guardrails, basic LOS integrations.
*   **What We Deliberately NOT Do:** Do NOT build a new core LOS. We build the "intelligent underwriter" that lives via API inside their existing LOS.
*   **The Agentic Wedge (3 Gaps to Exploit):**
    1.  **Eradicate "Stare and Compare":** Instantly calculate complex Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratios from unstructured, messy tax returns and bank statements.
    2.  **Bypass the Clunky UI:** Act as an invisible, headless agent that pre-fills LOS data fields, saving loan officers from clicking through 40 screens.
    3.  **Undercut the Per-Loan Tax:** Replace the $500/loan overhead with a highly efficient flat processing fee per document package.
