# Agent Use Cases - Phase 2 (Industry Verticals)

## Category G: Supply Chain & Logistics
26. **Freight Quote & Routing Agent** - Autonomously negotiates and books freight across carrier APIs, replacing manual freight brokers and emails.
27. **Customs & Bill of Lading Extraction** - Ingests unstructured, multi-language international shipping PDFs and routes data into the ERP, replacing manual data entry clerks.
28. **Inventory Forecasting & Re-routing** - Dynamically re-routes shipments based on real-time weather, port delays, and demand spikes, replacing static supply chain planning tools.
29. **Supplier Dispute Resolution** - Autonomously matches vendor invoices against POs and receiving reports, resolving pricing disputes without AP clerk intervention.

## Category H: Healthcare Administration
30. **Prior Authorization Agent** - Reads patient EMR charts, extracts necessary medical necessity codes, and submits directly to insurance portals, replacing massive manual billing teams.
31. **Claim Denial Management** - Analyzes 835 denial codes, cross-references with payer policies, and autonomously drafts the appeal letter with cited chart evidence.
32. **Patient Intake & Triage** - Conversational agent that ingests raw patient symptoms, updates the EMR, and pre-populates the physician's diagnostic workflow.
33. **Clinical Trial Matching** - Scans unstructured patient histories and autonomously matches them against complex clinical trial inclusion/exclusion criteria.

## Category I: Real Estate & PropTech
34. **Commercial Lease Abstraction** - Reads 100-page commercial leases and extracts key financial terms, CAM charges, and renewal dates directly into Yardi/AppFolio.
35. **Maintenance Ticket Orchestration** - Receives tenant complaints, diagnoses the issue, identifies the correct vendor, issues the work order, and manages scheduling.
36. **Tenant Screening & Underwriting** - Autonomously analyzes bank statements, pay stubs, and background checks to render an instant lease decision.
37. **HOA Compliance & Violations** - Ingests photos/reports, matches against HOA bylaws, and autonomously drafts and sends violation notices and fine escalations.

## Category J: Financial Services & Wealth
38. **KYC/AML Onboarding Agent** - Synthesizes unstructured identity documents, corporate registries, and watchlists to instantly clear or flag institutional clients.
39. **Loan Origination Underwriting** - Extracts data from W-2s, tax returns, and bank statements to calculate debt-to-income and pre-approve mortgages.
40. **Portfolio Rebalancing Reporting** - Generates highly personalized, natural-language quarterly performance reports for high-net-worth clients based on raw portfolio data.
41. **Audit & Tax Document Synthesis** - Ingests the "shoebox of receipts" and disparate financial PDFs, categorizing them directly into audit-ready tax schedules.

## Category K: Revenue & Sales Operations
42. **Bid & RFP Response Automation** - Ingests 200-question enterprise RFPs and autonomously drafts perfect responses based on the company's historical winning bids.
43. **Commission Dispute Resolution** - Reconciles complex tiered sales commission structures against closed-won Salesforce data, settling rep disputes instantly.
44. **CRM Data Hygiene & Auto-Logging** - Listens to sales calls and autonomously updates MEDDPICC fields, next steps, and close dates in Salesforce without rep intervention.
45. **Deal Desk Pricing Approvals** - Reviews custom quote requests, checks historical margin data, and approves or rejects pricing discounts autonomously.

## Category L: E-Commerce & Retail Ops
46. **Vendor Catalog Enrichment** - Ingests raw, messy product spec sheets from suppliers and autonomously rewrites them into SEO-optimized ecommerce product listings.
47. **Dynamic Competitor Pricing Agent** - Scrapes competitor sites and autonomously adjusts Shopify pricing to win the buy-box while preserving floor margins.
48. **Influencer Campaign Orchestration** - Autonomously scouts micro-influencers, drafts outreach emails, negotiates rates, and tracks campaign deliverables.
49. **Returns & Refund Triage** - Reviews customer return requests, checks shipping carrier APIs, and processes refunds or exchanges without CX team involvement.
50. **Retail Inventory Reconciliation** - Reconciles POS sales data against warehouse shipments to identify shrinkage and autonomously reorder stock.
