# Gold-Standard Opportunity Summaries

These summaries are deck-ready inputs. They are not final slides yet; they are
the strategic content layer to convert into a branded PPTX after the upgraded
Markdown blueprints pass review.

## Conversational Support

**Positioning:** Tier-1 resolution capacity paid by verified outcome, not
another chatbot or help desk.

**Incumbents:** Zendesk, Salesforce Service Cloud, Freshdesk, Intercom/Fin,
Gorgias, Ada, NICE, Genesys, Five9, BPO providers such as Concentrix and
Teleperformance.

**Market pain:** Customers expect faster, 24/7 support but resist bad bot
gatekeepers. Enterprises still pay per-seat help desk licenses and BPO labor
for repetitive tier-1 workflows.

**Disruptive wedge:** Attach to the existing help desk, automate 2-3
high-volume workflows, and charge against verified resolutions. Escalate with
full context when confidence is low.

**30-day proof:** Shadow mode then limited live traffic for order status,
refund status, billing explanation, or account access. Measure resolution rate,
escalation rate, policy failures, handle-time saved, and cost per verified
resolution.

## HR Onboarding Agent

**Positioning:** Approval-first onboarding orchestration over existing HRIS,
identity, collaboration, and knowledge systems.

**Incumbents:** Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, Oracle HCM, UKG, Dayforce,
BambooHR, HiBob, Rippling, Gusto, Deel, Enboarder, ServiceNow HRSD, Okta,
Microsoft Entra.

**Market pain:** Onboarding is fragmented across HR, IT, managers, facilities,
payroll, benefits, and security. Public evidence shows persistent onboarding
quality gaps and manual cross-functional delays.

**Disruptive wedge:** Do not replace the HRIS. Generate role/location-specific
onboarding plans, answer policy questions with citations, detect blockers, and
draft approved tickets/reminders across HRIS, Slack/Teams, ITSM, and identity.

**30-day proof:** Pilot with a 100-1,000 employee company using BambooHR or
Gusto plus Slack/Teams and Okta/Entra. Measure days-to-ready, HR ticket
deflection, manager SLA, unanswered questions, and app-access completion before
day one.

## KYC/AML Onboarding Agent

**Positioning:** CDD/EDD review copilot that turns messy onboarding evidence
into an auditable decision packet.

**Incumbents:** Persona, Sumsub, Veriff, Trulioo, Alloy, Socure, Sardine,
ComplyAdvantage, Unit21, Fenergo, NICE Actimize, Feedzai, Arva AI, Middesk.

**Market pain:** KYC/KYB onboarding is evidence-heavy, fragmented, and
regulatorily exposed. Teams reconcile IDV, sanctions, adverse media, registries,
UBO forms, CRM/core banking data, policies, and analyst notes.

**Disruptive wedge:** Sit on top of existing KYC vendors. Ingest outputs and
documents, detect discrepancies, map to customer policy, recommend approve/RFI/
EDD/escalate, and generate a defensible audit packet.

**30-day proof:** Business onboarding/KYB pilot for fintechs or sponsor banks
using one vendor export and CRM/CSV. Measure L1 review time reduction, first-pass
completeness, straight-through processing for low/medium-risk cases, and human
override reasons.

## Prior Authorization Agent

**Positioning:** Provider-side prior-auth copilot that completes the packet and
follows it until disposition.

**Incumbents:** Cohere Health, Anterior, EviCore, HealthHelp/WNS, Carelon,
CoverMyMeds, Surescripts ePA, Availity, Waystar, PriorAuthNow/Rhyme, Experian
Health, Honey Health.

**Market pain:** Prior authorization delays care, consumes staff time, and
creates physician burnout. CMS-0057-F adds regulatory forcing functions around
API access, denial reasons, timing, and public metrics.

**Disruptive wedge:** Start provider-side in one specialty. Detect PA need,
pull chart evidence, map to payer criteria, generate forms/letters/attachments,
submit through available channels, and track status without making clinical
coverage decisions.

**30-day proof:** Outpatient imaging, MSK, cardiology, DME, or specialty-drug
pilot. Measure cycle time, staff minutes saved, first-pass completeness,
more-info requests, and approval/denial outcomes.

## AI Code Assistant

**Positioning:** Governed codebase operations agent for proprietary enterprise
repos, not another autocomplete tool.

**Incumbents:** GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Cursor,
Devin/Windsurf, Tabnine, Sourcegraph, Amazon Q Developer, Qodo.

**Market pain:** Adoption is high but trust is low. Enterprises face cost
uncertainty from usage-based agent workflows, IP/security concerns, and review
burden when generated changes are not grounded in local architecture.

**Disruptive wedge:** Attach to GitHub/GitLab/Jira/CI, build a repo-specific
graph, and handle bounded workflows: onboarding, change-impact analysis, test
plan generation, migration PR prep, flaky-test triage, and security-remediation
PR drafts.

**30-day proof:** One proprietary repo, read-only first, then approved branch/PR
write. Measure time-to-answer architecture questions, PR-prep time saved,
test/lint pass rate, review rejection rate, and audit completeness.
