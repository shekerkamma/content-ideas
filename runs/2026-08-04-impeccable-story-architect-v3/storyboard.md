# Analytical storyboard

1. AI design's bottleneck is judgment allocation, not generation.
2. Plausible defaults create convergence and push expensive correction downstream.
3. More choice creates value only through explicit commitment gates.
4. Impeccable separates macro direction from within-direction execution.
5. The feature count is incidental; the decision architecture creates the value.
6. Generality raises the ceiling and shifts risk to the operator.
7. Change altitude determines the correct interface and escalation path.
8. Live Mode compresses the local judgment loop.
9. Local optimization becomes waste when the macro direction is wrong.
10. Human judgment belongs at four high-leverage moments.
11. DESIGN.md and detection turn taste into organizational memory.
12. Fresh-context review is a maker-checker control, not proof of quality.
13. The demo reaches Level 2 and shows pieces of Level 3; Levels 4–5 remain unproven.
14. Adoption requires four readiness gates and a bounded pilot.
15. Success is fewer wrong-direction cycles without quality regressions.
