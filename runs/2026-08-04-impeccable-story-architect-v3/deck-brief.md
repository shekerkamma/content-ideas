# Deck brief

## Audience

Executive product, design, engineering, and transformation leaders deciding whether to authorize
a bounded Impeccable pilot. The requested decision is pilot approval, not enterprise rollout.

## Decision

Treat Impeccable as a governed accelerator for structured exploration and refinement. Pilot it on a
bounded workflow and scale only when wrong-direction cycles fall without quality, accessibility,
provenance, or cost regressions.

## Narrative promise

Move beyond feature description to explain the causal mechanism, central trade-off, operating
roles, escalation rules, maturity gap, adoption gates, and measurement design.

## Voice and tone

Executive, analytical, evidence-aware, and decisive. Clearly distinguish observed demonstration,
presenter assertion, and analyst inference.

## Anti-references

Product tours, screenshot recaps, unsupported ROI claims, generic consulting cards, feature-count
theatre, and philosophical conclusions without decisions or measures.

## Evidence and editability

Hybrid native: 15 fully editable analytical slides plus native annotations on three exact source
frames. Claims link to timestamped transcript windows.

## Success criteria

The deck articulates a causal thesis, explicit counterarguments, decision ownership, controls,
maturity, rollout gates, and a falsifiable pilot scorecard; PowerPoint renders all 18 slides without
collisions, clipping, or missing media.
