# Presales Deal Prep: Compass

## Prospect snapshot
- Compass is the clearest official proof point that brokerage AI is moving from generic assistance to embedded workflow execution.
- Compass announced a June 16, 2025 release that includes drafting follow-ups, managing contacts, surfacing opportunities, and curating transaction timelines.
- The account question is not “should Compass use AI?” It is “where can Compass extend workflow depth, integration coverage, or rollout speed?”

## 3 key facts
1. Compass says it is the largest residential real estate brokerage in the U.S. by sales volume.
2. Compass AI is embedded in an end-to-end brokerage platform rather than positioned as a standalone assistant.
3. Compass is already automating exactly the categories that matter to lead-routing and transaction-adjacent workflow.

## Positioning angle
Position the offer as the layer that accelerates rollout of deeper workflow automation across routing, document preparation, and transaction support while preserving Compass’s existing product surface. OpenHands can be the internal orchestration layer for packaging repeatable automation skills, not the product the agent sees.

## Likely opening line
"Compass already proved that agents want AI inside the workflow they use every day. The next unlock is packaging deeper routing and transaction automation without creating a second operating surface."

## Top objections and responses
- "We already built this ourselves."
  - Response: Good. The wedge is not replacement. It is reusable internal orchestration, integration acceleration, and faster packaging of new workflow automations.
- "Agents will not trust more automation."
  - Response: Start with assistive routing and follow-up suggestions, then graduate to deeper workflow automation only where usage and conversion prove out.
- "Our platform is already end-to-end."
  - Response: That is exactly why this fits. A reusable internal agent layer compounds better in a brokerage with a unified operating stack.

## Meeting prep
- Ask which workflow beyond follow-up is next on Compass’s AI roadmap: transaction tasks, listing collateral, or document workflow.
- Ask where manual work still survives inside Compass One despite the current AI release.
- Ask how product teams measure whether AI features improve conversion versus simply usage.
- Ask whether deeper orchestration is constrained more by product bandwidth or integration reliability.

## Recommended next step
Propose a scoped design sprint focused on one post-lead workflow such as listing presentation prep, client invitation automation, or transaction milestone execution, and structure it so the resulting workflow becomes a reusable internal skill package.
