# VERTICAL SCORECARD: Real Estate Brokerage Lead Routing & Transaction Automation

Dimension | Score | Signal
--- | --- | ---
Intelligence Ratio | 4 | Lead qualification, assignment, follow-up prioritization, and transaction workflow decisions benefit from context-rich AI rather than static rules.
Outsourcing Readiness | 4 | Brokerages routinely buy external software and services, and the workflow pain is operational rather than core-IP sensitive.
TAM Accessibility | 4 | There are many brokerages and teams with direct revenue exposure to slow lead response and admin drag, though contract sizes are smaller than heavy enterprise verticals.
Data Moat Potential | 3 | CRM history, listing engagement, and internal pipeline data create some compounding value, but switching costs are lower than in deeply regulated verticals.
Regulatory Friction | 3 | Lower than healthcare or finance, but fair-housing, disclosure, and record-keeping rules still constrain fully autonomous automation.
Incumbent Vulnerability | 3 | Strong incumbents like Compass, Zillow, and Cloze are active, but many brokerages still run fragmented tooling and weak workflow automation.
Mirage PMF Risk | 4 | The demand is real, but this market is noisy, crowded, and full of “AI follow-up” claims that fail if agents do not trust routing or if CRM adoption is poor.

COMPOSITE SCORE: 25/35
VERDICT: GO
KEY RISK: The workflow only sticks if it improves conversion without creating agent resistance or CRM adoption fatigue.
COPILOT TO AUTOPILOT PATH: Start with AI-assisted lead scoring, assignment, and draft follow-up; then automate document and transaction steps after trust is earned.
