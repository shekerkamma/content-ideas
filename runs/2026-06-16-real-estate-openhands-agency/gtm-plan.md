## 7-Day First Client Acquisition Plan

### Day 1-2 — Build the Evidence Base

```
□ Confirm the ROI math for each use case — write it down so you can say it in 30 seconds:
  TC Bot: "You close 5 deals/mo at $400 each = $2,000/mo TC fees. I charge $2,500.
           Break-even is 5 deals. Above that, you're ahead."
  Lead scoring: "If it finds you 1 extra deal/year at $10K commission = $833/mo value.
                 I charge $2,500. You need 2 extra deals to be 2.5× ahead."
  Listings: "90 min per listing × 4 listings/mo = 6 hours/mo of your time.
             I write it in 3 minutes."

□ Write the one-page pitch for Use Case #1 (TC Bot)
  — Use the template at: re-triplet-pitch/01-transaction-coordinator-bot.md
  — Replace [your contact] with your actual info

□ Set up a simple intake page: Calendly link + 1-sentence description
  "Book a 30-minute call to see if an AI engineering team is right for your brokerage."
  Post it as a link, not a full website. You don't need a landing page to get the first call.
```

### Day 3 — Activate the Warm Network

```
□ List 10 real estate agents or brokers you already know (or know through 1 connection)
  — Previous clients, referrals, LinkedIn connections, local Facebook group members
  — NAR affiliate contacts, local association members, Realtor friends-of-friends

□ Send this message (DM / text / LinkedIn) to each:
  "Hey [name], I've been building software automation for real estate brokerages —
   specifically for transaction coordination and lead management. Would a 20-minute
   call be useful? I want to hear what's eating your time right now, not pitch you anything."

□ Do NOT pitch. The goal is discovery calls, not immediate closes.
  Every response — even "not interested" — is market research.
```

### Day 4-5 — Publish One Piece of Content

```
□ Post on LinkedIn (and/or X if you're active there):

  Draft 1 (TC Bot angle):
  "Real estate agents pay $300-500 per transaction to a transaction coordinator.
   If you close 6 deals a month, that's $1,800-$3,000 going to TC fees.
   Here's what I built instead:
   - Reads the purchase agreement, extracts every deadline automatically
   - Sends daily status emails to buyer, seller, title, and lender
   - Chases missing documents so you don't have to
   Cost: $2,500/month flat. Handles unlimited transactions.
   Break-even: 5 deals. Most agents close more.
   If this is your situation, DM me."

  Draft 2 (Lead scoring angle):
  "Real estate agents have 300 contacts in their CRM.
   Maybe 15 of them are thinking about buying or selling this month.
   The other 285 aren't. But you don't know which 15.
   So you call everyone randomly and miss the commission that was already in your database.
   I built a lead scoring system that tells you which 3 people to call first thing tomorrow morning.
   It runs on top of whatever CRM you're already using.
   DM me if you want to see it."

□ Record a 3-minute screen share demo if you have something running (even a test environment)
  — No editing required. Raw is fine. Agents want to see it work, not production quality.
  — Post as a LinkedIn native video or a YouTube unlisted link.
```

### Day 6 — Run Discovery Calls

```
□ For each discovery call, follow this agenda:
  "Tell me about your current transaction process — what happens between executed contract
   and close? What do you actually touch yourself versus what does your TC handle?"
  [Listen for the pain — "I still have to chase...", "My TC missed...", "I end up..."]

  "How many transactions do you close in a typical month?"
  [This is the ROI number. Every answer above 5 makes the TC Bot math obvious.]

  "If you had one less thing to deal with on every deal, what would it be?"
  [This tells you which use case to pitch. Lead the follow-up pitch with their answer.]

  "What does that currently cost you — in time, money, or both?"
  [Let them calculate their own pain. Your pitch writes itself.]

□ Do NOT present pricing on the call. Present it in writing within 24 hours.
□ Take notes. Every "I know" moment and every buyer line is content for your next post.
```

### Day 7 — Send the Pitch + Convert

```
□ Send a 1-page pitch to each discovery call within 24 hours:
  — The specific use case they described
  — The ROI math using their numbers from the call
  — The Growth tier price ($2,500/mo + $500 setup)
  — What's included (their AGENTS.md configuration)
  — Timeline: live in 10-14 days
  — The cancel clause: 30 days notice, no penalty
  — A Calendly link to book the next 30-minute call to sign up

□ Target: 1 signed contract by end of Day 7
□ If no conversion:
  — Follow up with the 3 warmest leads on Day 10
  — List the next 10 warm contacts and message them
  — Post a second piece of content (different use case angle)
```

## Content Rules

- NEVER use: OpenHands, subagents, MCP servers, AI agents, LLM, prompts, embeddings
- ALWAYS use: AI team, the bot, the system, what we built, it handles
- Lead with the buyer's pain — not your solution
- Include a number in every post (ROI, hours saved, cost difference)
- End with a specific action: "DM me", "Link in bio", "Comment 'agent' and I'll send it"
