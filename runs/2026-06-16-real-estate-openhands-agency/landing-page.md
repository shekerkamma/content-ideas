## FAQ

**Q: How is this different from Dotloop or Follow Up Boss?**
Dotloop stores documents. Follow Up Boss is a CRM. This builds software that does things those tools don't — reads your purchase agreements, chases your documents, writes your listing descriptions, scores your leads. It's not a subscription to a category of tool. It's a team that builds what you specifically need.

**Q: What if the AI makes a mistake?**
Every workflow has human checkpoints. Listing descriptions stage for your approval before posting. Deal emails go out only after you've seen the first 2-3 runs and confirmed they're right. You're not handing over your business — you're adding a layer that handles the repetitive work while you handle the judgment calls.

**Q: What software do I need to already have?**
A CRM (Follow Up Boss, KVCore, or a spreadsheet), Gmail, Google Calendar, and a phone. Everything else we set up for you.

**Q: How long until it's live?**
Most clients are live on their first use case within 10-14 days of signing. The first week is configuration and testing. The second week is supervised live runs. Week 3 you're on autopilot.
