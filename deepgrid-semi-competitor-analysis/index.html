Mobileye defines the maturity bar Deepgrid has not yet cleared

Mobileye positions EyeQ as purpose-built for ADAS and self-driving systems. Mobileye states its driver-assistance systems are in over 200 million vehicles. That production footprint is the compression risk: buyers may trust mature incumbents even if they are not India-price optimized.

NVIDIA and Qualcomm can compress the story through platform breadth

Even if Deepgrid avoids general-purpose AI compute competition, buyers know NVIDIA and Qualcomm as automotive compute platforms. Their risk is not necessarily price-fit for Indian trucking; it is roadmap credibility, ecosystem, and OEM familiarity. Deepgrid needs proof that a narrower SoC is enough for the target use case.

TI and Ambarella matter because “good enough ADAS SoC” can be enough

Texas Instruments and Ambarella sit closer to embedded automotive/vision compute expectations. If buyers prioritize qualification, thermal reliability, and ecosystem over the lowest target cost, Deepgrid’s wedge narrows. The battlecard should compare required feature set, not peak AI performance.
