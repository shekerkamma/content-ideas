## Six-Dimension Extraction

### Dimension 1: Tone

| Spectrum | Position | Evidence |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Formal ↔ Casual | Professional-casual — contractions, direct address, but precise | "That's not an AI problem — that's an automation problem" (email) |
| Serious ↔ Playful | Serious with dry wit | "The technology is boring. The results aren't." (deck) |
| Reserved ↔ Bold | Bold — makes definitive claims | "You're not ready for AI. You're ready for data engineering." (blog) |
| Simple ↔ Sophisticated | Simple words, expert-level thinking | "An actual number" — refuses to hide behind abstraction (LinkedIn) |
| Warm ↔ Direct | Direct-first, warm through efficiency | "Happy to walk through the analysis if useful" — warm but economical (email) |

### Dimension 2: Vocabulary

**Signature words/phrases:**
- "measurable" (appears 2x) — core value signal
- "That's it. That's the whole secret." — signature dismissive closer
- "The distinction matters because" — precise framing transition
- "Here's what we tell every..." — authority opener
- "Note:" — mid-thought callout, spoken-presentation style

**Jargon level:** Translated — uses "data estate", "fine-tuned classifier", "ROI model" but always with context
**Profanity:** None
**Anti-patterns:** Zero instances of: leverage, synergy, transformative, game-changing, cutting-edge, disruptive

### Dimension 3: Rhythm

- **Sentence length:** Mix of short punchy ("That's it.") and medium declarative (15-20 words). Rarely compound-complex.
- **Fragments:** Yes, intentional — "An actual number." / "That's it." / "The results aren't."
- **Paragraph length:** 2-4 sentences. Uses single-sentence paragraphs for emphasis.
- **Distinctive pattern:** Statement → contrast → punchline. "The technology is boring. The results aren't."

### Dimension 4: Structure

- **Openings:** Lead with a contrarian insight or a data point. Never throat-clearing.
- **Closers:** Short, definitive. Often a reversal or reframe.
- **Formatting:** Numbers and specifics as structural anchors ("47 enterprise pilots", "$2.3M", "6 weeks and $80K")
- **Lists:** Rare. Prefers flowing argument with numbered specifics embedded.

### Dimension 5: Perspective

- **Person:** We (organizational) in formal content. Shifts to direct "you" for the reader.
- **Reader address:** "You" — direct, never "one" or passive
- **Relationship:** Senior peer / advisor — not above, not below
- **Stance:** "I've done this and here's what I found" — experience-backed authority

### Dimension 6: Conviction Patterns

| Zone | Topics | Markers |
|------|--------|---------|
| Full authority | Pilot success factors, automation vs AI distinction, data readiness | Declarative sentences, no hedging ("That's it. That's the whole secret.") |
| Earned perspective | ROI modeling, vendor stack selection | "What we've seen..." / "Here's what we tell..." |
| Active pushback | Industry hype, mislabeling, buzzword culture | Direct challenges ("Can we stop calling everything 'AI-powered'?") |

## Extracted Voice Profile Summary

**Core identity:** Evidence-backed enterprise advisor who distinguishes real from performative, automation from AI, measurable from vague. Signature move: taking a popular assumption and precisely dismantling it with a specific number or concrete distinction.

**Voice in one line:** "Show me the number, not the narrative."
