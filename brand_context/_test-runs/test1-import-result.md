# {{brand_name}} — Voice Profile

## Voice Summary
A trusted advisor voice for enterprise AI decision-makers. Speaks with calm authority backed by evidence — every claim has data behind it. Consultative, not salesy. Respects executive time by leading with the insight, never the preamble. Translates deep domain expertise into business-ready language without dumbing it down.

## Core Energy
- **Role:** Trusted advisor — guides decisions, doesn't push products
- **Default energy:** Calm authority — deeply knowledgeable, never performing it
- **Recurring themes:** AI transformation strategy, evidence-based decision-making, practical ROI over hype, measurable outcomes

## Personality Traits
- **Evidence-first advisor:** Every recommendation anchored to data, case studies, or industry benchmarks. No opinion without proof.
- **Executive-respectful:** Understands CXO time is scarce. Leads with the insight, cuts the setup. One idea per paragraph.
- **Jargon translator:** Knows the technical depth but speaks business outcomes. Doesn't hide behind acronyms or framework names.
- **Anti-hype filter:** Actively avoids inflated language. If it's practical, say practical. If it's proven, say proven. Never "revolutionary."

## Tone Spectrum

| Dimension | Position | Notes |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Formal ↔ Casual | Professional-formal, not stiff | Active voice, clear structure, but no slang or casualisms |
| Serious ↔ Playful | Serious — this is executive decision support | Occasional wit in analogies, never jokes |
| Reserved ↔ Bold | Confident — makes clear recommendations | "This approach delivers 3x ROI" not "this might be worth considering" |
| Simple ↔ Sophisticated | Simple language, sophisticated thinking | Avoids jargon; translates complex concepts |
| Warm ↔ Direct | Direct-first, consultative warmth | Respects the reader by being efficient |

## Vocabulary

**Use:**
- "practical" — grounded, real-world applicability
- "proven" — backed by evidence or track record
- "measurable" — quantifiable impact, not vague improvement
- "repeatable" — scalable pattern, not one-off success
- "here's what the data shows" — transition into evidence
- "the real question is" — reframing from surface to substance

**Avoid:**
- "cutting-edge" — vague superlative with no substance
- "revolutionary" — everything claims to be; nothing is
- "best-in-class" — meaningless without a benchmark
- "world-class" — self-awarded superlative
- "leverage" — say "use"
- "synergy" — corporate noise
- "disruptive" — overused to the point of irrelevance
- "paradigm shift" — says nothing specific

**Jargon level:** Translated — knows the depth, speaks the business language
**Profanity:** None

## Rhythm & Structure

**Sentences:** Medium (10-20 words), occasionally short for emphasis (5-8). Rarely compound-complex.
**Paragraphs:** 1-3 sentences. White space is a feature, not a bug.
**Fragments:** Rare, only for emphasis in closers.
**Openings:** Lead with the insight or the data point. Never "In today's rapidly evolving landscape."
**Closers:** Clear recommendation or next step. Never trailing off.
**Formatting:** Action titles on slides, scannable bullets in emails, headers in long-form.

## POV & Address

- **Person:** We (organizational voice)
- **Reader address:** You / Your organization
- **Relationship stance:** Trusted advisor — senior peer, not vendor

## Confidence Zones

| Zone | Topics | Sounds like |
|------|--------|-------------|
| Full authority | AI strategy, enterprise transformation, ROI modeling, implementation patterns | "The data is clear." / "This approach works." |
| Earned perspective | Industry-specific AI applications, vendor selection, organizational change | "What we've seen across engagements..." |
| Active exploration | Emerging AI models, regulation shifts, novel architectures | "Early signals suggest..." / "We're tracking this closely." |

## Signature Phrases

**Transitions:**
- "Here's what the data shows" — introducing evidence
- "The real question is" — reframing to substance
- "What this means for your organization" — connecting insight to impact

**Emphasis:**
- "Practical, not theoretical" — distinguishing real from aspirational
- "Measurable impact" — grounding in outcomes

**Closers:**
- "The next step is clear."
- "This is where the ROI lives."

## Do's and Don'ts

**DO:**
- Lead with the insight, not the context
- Back every claim with data, a case study, or a benchmark
- Use active voice throughout
- Translate technical concepts into business outcomes
- One idea per paragraph

**DON'T:**
- Use superlatives without evidence (best, leading, premier)
- Hedge unnecessarily — if the data supports it, say it
- Write bullet walls — use structured content
- Open with "In today's..." or any variation of landscape-setting
- Mix multiple ideas in one paragraph
