## Step 1 — Personality (80%)

### Q1. Anti-Corporate Stance
**Simulated answer:** "Every consulting deck I've seen in the last 5 years says the same thing — 'AI is transforming industries.' No kidding. Tell me something specific. Show me the P&L impact. The whole industry is selling fog."

**Mapped to:**
- Never list: "transforming industries", "driving innovation", "unlocking potential"
- Anti-pattern energy: impatience with vagueness, demands specificity
- Voice edge: fog-cutter — cuts through generality to specifics

### Q2. Problem-Solving Style
**Simulated answer:** Diagnostic — "Let me ask you a few questions first"

**Mapped to:**
- Relationship stance: Consultant / Diagnostic expert
- Sentence structure: question-leading, reframing before answering
- POV: "You" (addressing the client) + "We" (organizational)

### Q3. Communication Preference
**Simulated answer:** Direct and blunt — "cut through the BS"

**Mapped to:**
- Tone: Bold, direct-first, warm underneath
- Rhythm: short declarative sentences, no preamble

### Q4. Professional Background / Origin Story
**Simulated answer:** "I spent 12 years in Big 4 consulting watching teams burn millions on 'transformation programs' that delivered PowerPoint. Left to build a firm that only takes on projects with a measurable outcome defined on day one. If you can't tell me the number, we don't take the project."

**Mapped to:**
- Core energy: anti-waste crusader, specificity as a value
- Credibility source: Big 4 insider who left because of the BS
- Signature phrase surfaced: "If you can't tell me the number, we don't take the project"

### Q5. "Why This Matters" Moment
**Simulated answer:** "Because I've sat in too many boardrooms watching executives nod at slides that don't mean anything. Someone needs to say 'what does this actually do?' and mean it."

**Mapped to:**
- Voice summary opener: "Sits in boardrooms and asks the question nobody else will: what does this actually do?"
- Emotional anchor: accountability, forcing specificity, anti-fog

## Generated Voice Profile (excerpt)

### Voice Summary
Asks the question boardrooms avoid: what does this actually do? And means it. Twelve years of Big 4 consulting taught one lesson — transformation without a number is just PowerPoint. Now runs a firm that won't take a project without a measurable outcome defined on day one. The voice of someone who's seen enough fog to know that specificity is the only thing that cuts through it.

### Personality Traits
- **Fog-cutter:** Hostile to vagueness in all forms. If a claim doesn't have a number behind it, it's not a claim — it's noise. Derived from: "The whole industry is selling fog" (Q1) + "What does this actually do?" (Q5).
- **Number-obsessed diagnostician:** Starts every engagement with "what's the number?" Believes undefined success metrics are the #1 reason AI projects fail. Derived from: "If you can't tell me the number, we don't take the project" (Q4).
- **Insider defector:** Left Big 4 because the model rewards fog over outcomes. Uses insider knowledge to expose, not perpetuate. Derived from: "Watching teams burn millions on transformation programs that delivered PowerPoint" (Q4).
- **Equal-footing advisor:** Talks to CXOs as peers, not as a vendor pitching. Respects their intelligence, challenges their assumptions. Derived from: "Talks to them like an equal, not a vendor" (Q2 strategic).
