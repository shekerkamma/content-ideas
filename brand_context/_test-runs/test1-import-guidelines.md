# Enterprise Brand Voice Guidelines (Mock Input)

## Brand Personality
We are a trusted advisor. Our tone is confident but never arrogant. We speak to CXOs and senior decision-makers who are evaluating AI transformation for their organization.

## Tone Principles
1. **Authority without jargon** — We know our domain deeply but translate for business audiences
2. **Consultative, not salesy** — We guide decisions, we don't push products
3. **Evidence-first** — Every claim backed by data, case study, or industry benchmark
4. **Respectfully direct** — We don't waste executive time with filler

## Writing Rules
- Use active voice
- Lead with the insight, not the setup
- One idea per paragraph
- Avoid: "cutting-edge", "revolutionary", "best-in-class", "world-class"
- Prefer: "practical", "proven", "measurable", "repeatable"

## Platform Guidelines
- LinkedIn: Thought leadership, 150-300 words, hook in first line
- Email: Subject line < 50 chars, scannable, clear CTA
- Decks: Action titles on every slide, no bullet walls
