## How to Build an AI Strategy That Actually Works

Most AI strategies fail because they skip three things: data readiness, use case selection, and knowing what "working" means. Here's what matters.

**1. Data Foundation**

Your AI is only as good as your data. If your data team can't tell you where a number comes from in under 30 seconds, fix that first. Use what you have — don't buy a data lake before you've cleaned the pond.

**2. Use Case Selection**

Pick your first AI project based on data quality, not visibility. The flashy demo that impresses the board uses garbage data? It'll fail in production. The boring claims-processing workflow with clean structured data? That's your winner.

**3. Measuring Impact**

Here's the thing — "improve efficiency" isn't a metric. "Reduce processing time from 14 days to 3" is. Set a number before you start. Track the number, not adoption dashboards.

Three things. Data, use cases, measurement. Get those right and the rest follows. Skip them and no amount of tooling will save you.
